After the Demon World Creature explains the crumpled paper theory, she advises that the mc go into the neighboring world, known as the 'god realm'. The mc's power is still incomplete. Cultivating in the human world, she learned to go against heaven's path and take the world energies for her own, but her inheritance is from the demon world, which believes in going with the natural flow.

For the mc, whose core has fractured into many separate pieces, cultivating to higher levels will be impossible unless she fixes her core.

The prince who is fighting for the throne at the same time the main character is ousted eventually fails in his revenge plot. But he is affected by the black energy and the mc's unique energy, and at his moment of death he is gifted a second soul. This second soul is far better than his original one and he becomes the main antagonist of the world.

He is a follower of the white demon but he doesn't believe in it himself. His true goal was to amass as much of the white demon's power by collecting others that are 'gifted' like him. One of his targets was the mc's son.

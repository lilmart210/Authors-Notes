The mc has been hidden under the guise of training while her sect in the decay was destroyed in her absence. While there, she is trying to figure out the 'mission'. There wasn't a goal; the master lied to her, but she figured out her own goal and broke free.

This treasure was a 'time keeper' treasure, usually used for oracle work and keeping other treasures preserved. It had an ephemeral world that uses the user's mental powers to create many places of their imagination. Her cultivation is suppressed depending on the world she is in. When worlds are destroyed they reset — the reset is the history being rewritten, and she starts to remember the changes.

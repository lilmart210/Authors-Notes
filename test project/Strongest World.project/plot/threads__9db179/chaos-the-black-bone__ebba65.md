Chaos is the disharmony in the world, but that doesn't mean it destroys things. The opposite of chaos is the five elements. Chaos is the source of 'mutation' in the five elements — variant abilities and special bloodlines are the results of chaotic energy interfering with them.

The Black Bone will be in the spinal column of the mc and cause her to suffer from the affliction of chaos. It lets her travel between 'folds' at a lower cultivation level and fight people above her level. The drawback: it is not suitable with the five elements and threatens her physical existence.

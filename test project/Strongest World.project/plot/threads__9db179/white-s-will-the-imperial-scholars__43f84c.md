The first Imperial Scholar was the demonic cultivator White. This imperial scholar had an unnatural craving for destruction due to the mc's influence. Because of Jin Dandan, she could not experience normal sensations. One day she realized that she could elicit feelings out of others, experiencing what she could not. White was the main character's first attempt at resurrection. However it was foiled by humans, manifesting into a certain hatred and disdain for them.

Upon her death, those who defeated her had their physiques changed forever. Their bodies had become carriers of Jin Dandan's spirit as it spilt from White's body. Those that succumbed to White's will became devils and those that listened to the will of the other scholars became imperial scholars. Both wills are exactly the same.

Some developed white eyes and white hair, as if suddenly filled with wisdom. Those who succumbed to the devil developed blackened veins.

Spirit has no physical presence and no quantity — it is subjective, universally present, and relies on understanding. You cannot increase the amount of spirit in an area; you alter it through understanding and physical properties (e.g. absorbing spirit stones). Living beings can’t project spirit, but they can share understanding — which lets them manifest attacks and phenomena. Where understanding breaks, manifestation occurs; **Dreg** is Decay’s manifestation — homogenous, boundary-less matter.

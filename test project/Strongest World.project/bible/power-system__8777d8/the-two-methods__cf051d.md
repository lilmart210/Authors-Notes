The **Human Method** creates a *temporary* imbalance: cultivators manifest pure energy and store it in origin points (spirit cores). The **Demon Method** is a *constant* imbalance (superior) — demons have a spirit core and refine it by stacking layers, energy leaching into the surrounding body. “Demon” means born with a spirit core; “human” means without one. Some demons practise human techniques by never strengthening their core while young, trading longevity for versatility.

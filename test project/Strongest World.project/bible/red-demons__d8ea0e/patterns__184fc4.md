The **Queen’s Physique** is a straight/eye-mark pattern: many eye-marks across the body except the forehead. Jin Meixiu and Jin Dandan both bear it. Patterns are per-member — a red demon may instead carry a Sword-Heart lean (Jin Guangli) or Bloodline-Memory gift (Jin Hai).

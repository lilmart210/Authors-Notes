Red-demon power manifests as colour — beginning in the eyes, sometimes spreading to hair, nails, or aura. Their two branches are **illusions** (Jin Meixiu’s Plane of Illusions) and **law/element utilization** — wielding the elements by force, tearing apart the laws that hold them together (Heavenly Reversal).

Structure is composed of **vitality** (the resilience of a material — a low accumulation of fatigue) and **core** (its elemental makeup). Decay is made of **fatigue** (the incurred debt of existence over time) and **impurities** (the dregs within the elemental makeup). Between them lies a degree of homogeny that permits **manifestation** — phenomena occurring in reality when core elements are mixed into certain shapes and concentrations. Fully-structured or fully-decayed matter devolves into another system, balancing itself out.

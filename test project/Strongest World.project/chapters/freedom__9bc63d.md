Roar!

A furious bellow rang out, teeth, several inches long dug into the shoulder of the outstreached arm. Lin Mei recoiled and winced as the bone crushing maw dug deep into her flesh without regard. Lin Mei grabbed whatever fleshy substance she could inside but she was Unable to resist. The bear snatched her small body up into air and hurled it back into the cave.

Skittering accross the stone floor, the pain had not yet completely registered as she was caught by the cold embrace of the stone wall. The sound of a loud impact resounded as she had the wind, completely knocked out of her. She could neither inhale or exhale properly.

'Freedom...Ah...Its so, so close.'

More than the pain that seared her body and her senses that screamed at her in many ways. Lin Mei wanted to leave and go back into the world. 

Her ears were filled with the desperate cries of bear cubs and the groans of a large one. The heavy paw steps resounded but as she listened she felt a burden lift off her shoulders.

'Its not, pursuing me.'

Despite not saying anything, her thoughts were laborously intensive. 

'I am, so, fortunate. I didn't, kill it. But I, survived! ha-'

This was the plan, she could only attack without holding back once and hope she could recover enough to leave. Sprawled on her side, one of her arms pressed against the stone floor. The arm exerted strength shakily as it forced her to sit straight up.

'I need to leave while we both are too injured to fight each other.'

But as she tried to move the arm she struck the bear with, she didn't feel a response. Instead, waves of searing heat began taking root into her shoulder, sending tendrils of agony through her side. This was not something she had felt before, not even at her worse moments of pain. For a moment, she had no idea how to react.

Lin Mei's face twisted in response as she tried to bear with the pain. The more she tried to hold it in, the less she felt aware of her surroundings. The mental image she had built up had become scrambled and even the clarity moments ago had shattered. Countless distorted feelings began mixing in her mind and spreading across her body. Weakness had started to settle into her arms but before it could take root, she felt a soothing feeling spread throughout her body.

The areas it passed, the pain subsided and the bleeding stopped. 

She sat for a period of time before her thoughts could finally be pieced togethor.

'This is, mending flow right? What is this, Its never been this intense before.'

Although the injuries inside her body had not changed at all, she could feel the mending flow moving to certain areas, repairing them and continuing onwards. It had never felt so clear to her before.

She thought that if she opened her eyes, her outside appearance would look far better than her internal situation. Two eyelids cracked sligthly but still there was nothing to be seen.

'I am still in the cave after all, there's likely not any light for a long ways in.'

Closing them, she decided that if she was going to push forward, it had to be done now. With a great heave, she pushed up to her feet but immediately stumbled back to her knees. her hand reached out to catch her but one arm failed to move while the other could not find the ground in time. Her face struck the ground as she grunted.

She was very disoriented.

'First, I need to figure out where I am...'

Behind the momma bear were two cubs and they all were running with all the might. As they ran the largest bear left a trail of blood and pieces of entrails. It had been nearly a week since its last encounter but it never imagined one was hiding inside of its own den. It did not know where the rest were but if it was to stay it would definitely not bode well for it.

As it reached the entrance to its rock den, it barreled through the opening. 

But as its eyes had begun to focus, it saw light reflect off something shiny. It was stunned, but had not broken motions. Unable to comprehend fully as it could not see clearly.

Several blades, some aimed directly at its throat began to piece into its body and with a decisive strike, the bears' head was sent rolling, along with the rest of its body  into the ground.

"Finally! I thought senior brother would never give the sign to do it. Any longer and we would have missed the deadline."

Outside a cave entrace stood a group of people with weapons drawn. 

"your right, luo bao, if I hadn't heard senior use the whispering techinque, I would have had to attack myself. We've watched it for so long yet only now we attack."

Even Pan Zhengshang had to agree, they already wasted so much time, why wouldn't they attack.

"Fools, its only because the fight was this easy that you reflect like this. Can you tell me any other time that beast ever ran so carelessly outside. Its obvious it did not even know we were here. Had you attacked at a different time, the outcome wouldn't be so obvious. You would instead be walking back with nothing. Take its-"

But as the words came out of his mouth froze as Zhengshang heard wails and heavy pawsteps rushing from inside the cave. He quickly turned its gaze to the cave and called out, "Everyone get ready for another bear!"

The group of five reacted quickly, focused on the cave entrance.

'I didn't realize there would be more bears, I would have picked a different target had that been the case. Facing off against multiple adult stone clawing bears is unusual.'

As the bears made their appearance, a refreshing sigh made its way past his lips. The stature of these bears were not so big, they weren't even as big as the smallest of their group.

"Its only a few cubs, let them pass."

The tall disciple questioned, "Why aren't we killing these cubs? They won't survive without their parent for long out here."

Zhengshang snorted.

"Do every human orphan die without a parent? Of course not, some say there's too many of them! They might become prey to other animals but atleast they won't just die simply because they have no parent. Besides, not many creatures live digging through rock. They will return back to their dens instincively."

And he was right.

The cubs stalled at the entrance, visibly shaken as their heads shifted between their mother and the hunters. Their hind paws lifted as they took a step back. And another step. Before turning around running.

"See? Now go and cut up the bear so we can return. We have to move quickly if we don't want to fail."

The group nodded in response.

One of the female disciple had started to inspect the bear for the right parts to cut. With a great heave, She pushed over but before her blade made its way into the body of the bear she noted, "Did any of you rip open its belly like this?"

As the others gathered around they also saw the peculiarity. 

Zhengshang fingers landed on his chest, rubbing a circular pattern as he thought aloud, "Aside from our initial encounter, I don't think anyone has landed a clear blow on it since. Could it have fought something recently, but this seems almost too recent. Its still bleeding. Junior sister Shuang, open it up."

Lian Shuangs sword pierced the hard skin and into the tissue, further worsening the injury and allowing them to see inside. What should have been a stunning revelation made all of their faces sour.

"Its organs are badly damaged. Even if we didn't kill it I don't think it would have been long before it would die. Can we still turn it in like this?"

Lian Shuang's eyes looked up to her senior for an answer and so did the rest. Could they go back like this?

Rubbing his temples, he sucked in a breadth and responded, "We can but then what of our score? Whatever we gathered would certainly be judged by the medical department. If its this badly damaged, its not even useful. Its shameful but, it would be better to bring back those cubs than to return with this mess."

Their faces soured. If it was like this, then they would definitely score the lowest.

But more importantly, Zhengshang didn't know what could do this? The belly was a particularly sensitive location. No creature would ever leave it open to such an attack.

Just as he was questioning, the sound of paw steps came rushing out. Wails pervaded the air as they tried to push past the disciples but were unsuccessful. Each cub was being forcibly held down by several hands and Zhengshang had grappled with one of the cubs. Premature teeth sunk into his clothes but he paid it little attention. 

After a short moment, their arms, mouths and legs were heavy rope that bound them togethor.

"Atleast, we didn't have to hunt the cubs in that cave, but I wonder what they were running from?"

"Could it be the thing that injured the momma bear?"

As discussion arose, a certain sound begin to creep into their ears.

Plat.

Plat.

Plat.

It was slow, methodical and eery. The longer they listened the more the hairs on their arms stood up.

'I can smell it. Its stronger here.'

Lin Mei took one rocky step after the other. Despite the injuries she wouldn't rest until she was free. Her foot drug accross the ground laborously as she followed the fresh forest scent. Something she hadn't breathed in such a long time. Was it a year or maybe two? She did not have the mind to bother about the exact details anymore.

With a final step, she felt it. She quickly raised her only hand to cover her eyes as the warmth from the sun began to reach her body. The light seeped through the rags she called clothes and she dug her bare feet into soft blanket that was the earth. She could not believe that it was that time.

'Slowly. Slowly.'

Her eyes cracked a bit but while she waited in anticipation, the everlasting darkness still lingered in front of her.

Abruptly her eyes opened up beneath her hand, peering through the gaps in her fingers. They scanned the clearing left to right in a frantic wave.

'T-This can't be! Where, Where is-"

She looked forward to this moment but now what fueled her seeped out of her body. The mending flow had slowed greatly, and she could hardly feel it anymore. Lethargy and weakness pervaded her muscles and joints as she collapsed in the clearing. 


'the light...'

Golden warmth washed over grass, plants and stone walkways. Paths criss crossed under this light, sometimes shaded from an overhang or leading to decorated door.   

Amidst the beautiful scenary was a Large opening. Behind the doors was an ornate table. At the head of the table sat Jin Guangli, with Jin Meixiu standing behind him. Six elders sat along the sides of the table, each in a heated discussion.

One of the elders observed the arguing five, a vein began popping out of her head as she spoke, "You old fools, none of these are feasible!"

The others simmered down at her outburst, they collectively turned their attention towards her.

This was the second elder, Xue Rong. Dressed in the Jin family attire, she let her anger simmer down a bit before she continued speaking, "You want to select new talent, expand operations and concur more areas of the desert? With what? Money? Although we have our materials trade, the resources generated can only run our city. Nothing more!."

Grimmicing, a man with a scar running amongst his head spoke, "That is a problem." 

Taking a slow look around, he spoke once more, "I would like to propose that we focus on nurturing new talents, my guards are more than enough to handle disputes in the city and while the desert is largely unsearched, our neighbors are not strong enough to threaten our borders. Our biggest concern should be building a sizable force to establish ourselves around the city."

The man with a scar was named He Shen, the leader of the Jin family guardians. He supervised the city guards training and the city safety. 

The others thought hard about this. On the surface it seemed like a logical statement, but who was he? He was He Shen, the leader of the family guardians. Such a plan would benefit the family, but leave the others unable to fund their own goals. Putting them aside proved difficult but focusing solely on one thing seemed wrong. It would be a long time before they could work towards other goals once they decided on one.

Xue Rong took a glance at a certain elder, "Advisor Tingfeng, you've been listening for so long. What do you think we should prioritize?"

He had been reading over the submitted statements from the other elders. There was six elders here but only three submitted plans that needed urgent handling. Hu Tingfeng had not submitted anything and neither had Xue Rong.

"Hmmm. These matters... If I had to pick, I wouldn't pick any certain one."

The others kept listening, third elder Tingfeng was like this. He spoke words as if others could see what he was thinking. None of them doubted him as he was definitely one of the smartest demons here. He was slightly odd, but he never gives out bad advice.

"oh yes, theres also that too."

Hu Tingfeng began writing his thoughts onto some paper. Ink flowed onto the paper neatly, every now and then he would would blot out certain lines with a single stroke, before restarting further down the page.

A piece of paper was held up, "How about this?"

Eyes began peering onto the paper, there was a lot to digest but they took the general idea in quickly.

Xue Rong gasped, "You want to use use the desert as a way to temper trainees?" 

He Shen mulled the Idea over. All of the elders here are in the Ascension realm, so to them, the desert did not contain the greatest of risk. But the same could not be said for those below their realm. Training in the desert would require a lot of supervision.

"Not training, missions for merit. Most people here are attracted by the treasures found inside the cave worlds. How could they resist if we disclose the locations of some treasures in the desert? Naturally we won't show this information to everyone, only those in the family will have such knowledge."

The other elders felt that made sense, but Xue Rong felt that there was a problem.

"And how do we stop those who join the family purely for their own benefit? Theres nothing stopping them from hiding what they gain after exploring these locations. I don't see how this could be profitable for us."

He Shen gave his chin a few carefull strokes. He also had these thoughts too, "I don't think this is feasible. This kind of risk, what is there to gain?"

Hu Tingfeng grunted, "Do I need to spell this out too?"

The pen graced the paper once more, this time, the strokes on the page were swift and vibrant. Previously he wrote at his leisure, but now he was practically spilling ink on the page.

'I've really had my eyes opened, the old man really spelled it out for us.' The fourth elder, Liao Bai, was really impressed with his guts. Only advisor Tingfeng could do something like this and not be reprimanded immediately.

Holding up the sheet of paper, the color of the white sheet appeared nearly black. The elders around the table all looked carefully at what he had written.

Xue Rong looked carefully, there was many divisions of labor written out but most notably, the organization was more akin to a sect. The difference between a sect and a family is slightly fuzzy but they do have their differences. The most prominent difference would be loyalty.

Although none of the people here knew of the Jin Family before joining it, they did know about each other. Their bonds may not have been so strong in the past, but after joining the Jin Family, they grew accustomed with each other. The same goes for their respective disciples as their loyalty follow their masters beliefs.

With the family heads being worldy kings and the elders as ascension realm demonic cultivators, this was a force that could make waves even outside the cave worlds. To keep the internal integrity, the forces recruited up until now had some relationships with each other. The rest were scattered or removed from the region. The fith elder, Gong Gui, was the disciple of the fourth elder, Liao Bai. Everyone knew about the eccentric Hu Tingfeng too, he had been in isolation in these fractured worlds for some time now.

The elders voices began to pick up again, each presenting their own opinios and arguements. It was not until an hour after had they finally settled on their objectives.

This entire time, Jin Guangli sat at the table and said nothing. In actuality, his only focus was making sure his wife and son had plenty of comforts in life. As for menial task such as managing the affairs of his surroundings, he could care less about such things. It was best to leave it to the people who actually lived in such a confined world.

Amidst the conversation, Jin Guangli felt the pressence of something familiar, 'Eh? is that my artifact I'm feeling? Did Hai'er exit seclusion...'

A smile made its way onto his face. This did not escape everyone's eyes. 

'The patriarch smiling? He must like these plans, then theres no need to continue deliberating' 

This thought crossed everyone's minds. Some felt sour at not being able to get their own plans introduced in time, But seeing the grin on the patriarchs face made them focus in at once. This was their task now.

He Shen was the first elder and leader of all the family elders. He presented a sheet of paper before formally explaining , "Family Head, these are the plans for the objectives we wish to accomplish, please have a look."

Jin Guangli took the paper while He Shen explained carefully, "The family hasn't grown much in size since the initial members joined. While we have a few thousand people between disciples and the family gaurds, there are not enough to cover any venture outside the city. At the moment, the most critical void fractures with valuable materials inside of them are being secured by our people however there are still tons of smaller spaces that we cannot grab ahold of. Instead of monopolizing these, we will focus on recruiting many younger people as disciples and tying down as many people to the city through responsibilities."

Eyes scoured the paper quickly. There were many more details such as benefits and manufactured goods that could be used to trade for raw materials that could be found around the lands. New proffessions and positions that would need to be opened and methods for large scale recruiting. It seemed like a good idea and Jin Guangli didn't think anymore about it. If he needed to make all the decisions, he would not have recruited these demons.

"Hmm. This seems good, then with this can conclude this meeting." 

The paper vanished into his sleeves as he dismissed the group, but before they could stand up, Jin Guangli's voice echoed out again, "But before you leave, I have someone I want you to meet. You've waited outside long enough, Jin Hai come in and introduce yourself."

'Jin Hai? It is young master jin?'

Everyone had wanted to see who the young master was. They knew where he was but as he was in seclusion they could not go and visit him. 

As their thoughts swirled, A small hand pushed open the grand door into the main hall. Yellow light filled the room as everyone turned to see who had stepped in.

With a sword by his waist and chest held high, Jin Hai stepped into the main hall. 

"I greet Father, Mother and the elders."

Jin Hai cuffed his hands as a greeting to the table. The elders nodded back in acknowledgement. 

Grabbing the sword at his side, Jin Hai presented it to the table with two hands. The sword gently floated into the air and returned to Jin Guangli's hands.

Jin Guangli Inspected the sword as it landed. He was in no rush as he ran his hands over the surface of the sword.

"Unbelievable, you've left scratches on the surface of my natal artifact. Although it is devoid of energy, this is not easy to do to my sword. It seems you have been working hard."

The elders erupted in clamor. Gong Hui and Liao Bai felt their horizons expand, "Scratches on a wordly kings artifact!? Isn't he only a flood demon? Is it possible to scratch an artifact at that level?!"

He Shen took a long glance at the sword in Jin Guangli's hands. Although human, Jin Guangli was not a weak sword immortal. His sword was a natal artifact-a heaven grade treasure that grows with the user. Such a thing could not be made, only nurtured over a great amount of time. 

Such a weapon could only be damaged by a strength greater than the user itself. If a wordly king natal treasure that was devoid of energy could be scratched by a flood demon, then pigs could fly faster than birds with four wings.

But seeing the stress marks on the weapon body proved otherwise. 

Xue Rong looked at the boy and declared outright, "There is no limit on young master Jin's future, I'm afraid none of his peers can hope to be a match. By the time he's an adult he will be peerless"

The others agreed with this assesment.

The smile on Jin Guangli face could not be hidden, but he had a question, as did everyone else. How did he do it?

Basking in the praise, Jin Hai felt he had ascended to heaven. His father had never praised him like this before and he felt the feeling to be wonderful. 

But when he noticed his mothers eyes had shrink to pin needles, his smile froze and his feelings plummetted back to the ground. An icy feeling washed over him and he felt like he had done something wrong.

"You've awakened your memories, haven't you?"

Jin Meixiu's voice was not loud but it seemed to drown out everyone else's voice. Although she asked, She was certain about this. The black hair on Jin Hai's head held a tinge of red. As he got stronger, it will dye his head crimson red, just like their eyes.

The once bustling crowd began to quiet. The attention turned to Jin Hai in an eerie silence.

Jin Hai began stumbling over his words, it seemed it was a bad thing and he did not know how to respond, "I-I-I-did. I awakened to our family's bloodline memories during my battle with father's sword."

The elders all breathed in a cold breath of air. Even Jin Guangli felt his mind waver. They all knew what it meant to be a red demon. The younger demons may not know in detail, but older demons are familliar with them. Variant demons normally have unique abilities but it is even more so for red demons. Both their strength and techniques are strong and unusual.

Even gray demons without a variant ability developed bloodline memories but such strength normally comes later in their develoment. Variant abilities are powerful but also very difficult to develop. Yet he was only in the flood demon stage and could already wield understood his techniques to this level.

They no longer wondered how he scratched the sword. The answer was obvious to everyone. Instead, another thought crossed their minds.

'This kid is not a prodigy but a monster.'

If before they thought his prospects were high, now they knew they were truly limitless. There won't be anything to stop him on his path.

Jin Guangli's body flashed from his seat, he was now standing in front of everyone at the table. His grand demeanor could not be missed as he stood impossingly infront of his son.

"Good son, it seems a test is in order. Show me what you have learned."

Hearing this, Jin Hai expressioned brightened, "Do I get to fight father now?"

"Haha, since you've completed the first challenge then I suppose its only right to offer a little reward."

Jin Guangli strode out into the fields confidently with his son right behind him. The elders followed suit, clamor chasing along with them.

Hua Song had already dismissed the outer disciples with her. She had been arguing with Yu Lin outside the hall. When they saw the formidable lineup exiting the building, sweat began building up on their necks. They immediately gave respectful bows as the family head lead the way with his son and elders in tow.

But their was someone Hua Song did not see walk out immediately. After the main group had passed, her eyes were curious. She looked into the hall they had come from only to see Her master, Sixth elder Lei talking with the Matriarch.

Out of all the elders, it could be said Lei Biya had the best relationship with the matriarch of the family. Hua Song could not make out what they were saying and did not dare to but the expression on the matriarchs face was different from normal. Her usual smile and placcidness was gone, instead, a certain darkness hung around her eyes.

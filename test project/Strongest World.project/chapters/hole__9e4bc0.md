Controlling the sensation, a bloody shin had begun to be infused with a special energy. She did not have a name for this feeling but she had been practicing controlling it. With a bit of will power, this energy could gather from all around her body and could repair her wounds. 

'hmm~ Internal vigor?'

she shook her head, she had been debating this for days now, she needed a name for this feeling.

'how about...Mending...Mending Flow? yea, that sounds good. Am I a genius or what?'

However it was not omnipotent. Still sitting with a bloody leg, she counted down the time it took before it healed. Meanwhile, her hand gracefully glided amongst the ground until it encountered a small river rock. Every so many seconds, she would move a another small stone a little further than the others beside her. She kept doing this before eventually she ran out of stones and began moving them back beside her.

Many passes back and fourth ellapsed. During this time, she would also wipe the dried blood from her body with a wet hand.

'2 hours for the mending flow to fix an injury like this. Its a bit faster than if I don't gather it togethor but not by much. Maybe its because I have several injuries elsewhere as well.'

She looked at her knuckles and knees, each scabbed over and peeling away to reveal the soft but scarred skin that covered them.

She contemplated. 

'I wanted to only heal the shin injury but it looks like my control over it isn't as great as I wanted. I'll have to keep practicing.'

With nothing specific to do other than wait and improve, she kept practicing. Time and time again, she struck the wall, healed and repeated. Periodically she would take time to rest and meditate. By now she had settled on a particular spot on the wall and if you rubbed your hand along it, you could tell where she had spent the longest time at.

At one point in her rest, her ears moved, almost like a forest animal.

splash!

'What was that? Did something fall into a puddle?'

Her ears listened for a while longer but there was no more noise aside from water dripping through some stone faces. She was very familiar with this space and in the long time she had been stuck there, nothing moved if she did not move. There were no bugs or anything else to bother her here. She was surrounded with dense stone. Occasionally, water dripped and flowed through the densely packed stones but this sound was different. 

Something fell.

In her mind, She could feel a detailed map of her surroundings. It was different from normal mental projections as only External stimulations made up this map. Recalling different areas, triggered her smell, touch, echoes and physical orientation. Despite being unable to see she had never felt so certain of her position.

She stood up, located the source of the one-off noise and walked slowly. After reaching the spot, a hand began scanning the wet ground, slicking water across the surface, bumping into a pile of gravel. She would never figure out which one had fell.

'Ha! Could it be?' She suddenly had a thought.

Darting to the center of the room, her body moved with great emphasis, she did not want to miss a second.

'The blue light! could it have arrived? But then I would see it. Then just what...' her sudden enthusiasm gone, but she was curious. What caused the stone to suddenly chip and drop. She knew her own strength was not sufficient, no matter how many times she had hit the wall. And this was the wrong spot regardless.

Shaking her head, she resumed her own meditation.

A few more days passed and while striking the wall, she stopped.

'How long have I been doing this? What, what am I doing here? Why, am I doing this?'

She suddenly felt herself loosing her purpose. She needed to do something new.

'I'm improving physically but what of it? This won't work. What if I could use a weapon? But there is none here. Maybe I could make one?'

In her mind she could feel the weight of several pieces of stone. Then the only material she had left would be the scraps of clothing she had. Most of her clothing was torn beyond use, bloody and messy so she did not wear any besides the most modest amount. But as she thought about it, the material was too fatigued and damaged, there was nothing she could make with just this and her thoughts on this path ceased to interest her.

Previously, she studied in Extreme Path city academy. It taught sword techniques to all its practictioners and she had studied a few with her sisters.

'I think eldest sister once mentioned that, some martial artist use their body to strike, mimicking beast and weapons alike. I Think an elder disciple also used a striking method from an insect.'

A stone found its way into her hands as she made her way back infront of the clean stone wall. 

'Maybe I could be like a sting beetle and wield something as I strike?'

Sting beetles were small insects that preyed on larger bugs. But contrary to their name, they have no stingers. Instead, they often take the body parts that other insects use to attack as their own using their front two claws. 

Holding a stone in her hand, she thought about the sting beetle and held the stone like a disc.

An overhead strike flowed in the darkness as it brushed against the wall.

kirshhshhh

Sounds of a broken river stone echoed as the rock in her hand shattered into glass like pieces.

It was a bust.

'It seems that this idea is no good. I guess these stones aren't on the wall for a reason. Forget it, I can just use my body can't I? What was it, a mantis, don't they attack with their hands?'

She thought about other methods of attack as well but all she had was her own body. She dug through her own memories, trying her hardest to recall all methods. Elbow strikes, knee strikes, grappling but none of these appealed to her explicity except one. 

'Sword strikes. It has to be a sword.'

There was nothing better than a bladded weapon. 

Making another move to strike the wall, she poised herself like she always did. Except this time, one of her hands rested well on the wall and the other fist was pulled back. Two finger stretched out from the balled fist, sharp and pointed like a sword's edge. 

For the first time in a while, she felt nervous. She only ever struck the wall with closed fist, focusing the force down her arm and into the rest of her body. Breaking bones was common, especially when she was too focused. As time went on, her sensitivity to her surroundings increased along with her awareness. Gradually she was more precise with her strength.

But that was about to change. She had always wanted to break this wall. But she couldn't do it with her fist but maybe her fingers could. But this could also break her.

'I don't know the limits of my own healing. I don't know if changing my attack method will work. But I can't stay like this. I must get better, I must.'

There was no reason to stop and she had begun believing in herself more than before. She took a deep breadth in, muscles along her abdomen began tensing as this reaction traveled simultaneosly across her entire body. The tightly packed rows began bulging as she pulled back even further, her one remaining hand on the wall barely reach, the other, palm to the sky.

'Strike!'

Three fingers came togethor rapidly, holding each other in lock as the other two bonded with each other, almost as if they were conjoined in the middle. A whistling sound briefly filled the stagnant cave as two fingers aproached a cold and unmoving stone face.

Kacha!

Countless cracks spriled out from the stone face as a hand implanted itself firmly into its surface. Several chunks of rock fell outright, revealing more stones behind it.

Taken aback, Lin Mei took a step away with her hand making its way out of its own devastation. Wincing for pain but as she inspected her hand, there was no damage, not even a scrap.

'What is this? I can do that?'

She was surprised herself. Even before she had been brought down, she had never been able to shatter stone like this. 

'Did my body fix itself? Am I absorbing energy using that barabric method? I can't believe its working.'

She sat down, to introspect. Inside her mind the connections within her body revealed themselves. But it was still bone dry, with not a single drop of energy swarming about.

She frowned. Maybe the stone had already been weaked over the last year she had been striking at it. She always thought that earth was always at full strength. How could it be weakened? A rock would only break when it gets hit with a certain amount of force. Could stones fatigue like the human body? She didn't know.

Splash!

Perking up, she heard another stone fall but oddly enough, it was behind her.

'Its that noise!'

Her ears twitched as she listened carefully. The sound this time seemed much closer but still faint. She could hear a scrapping sound, as if something was rubbing against stone. It continued for a period of time before stopping.

She wasn't sure what the sound was but she was certain of one thing.

'Something is coming.'

She didn't know weather to be nervous or excited but she was filled with energy. She could not tell much but the fact the sound was getting louder meant it was closer. 

Scurying over the wall, she felt around. Both hands rubbed over the stone wantonly. The surface of the wall sported several bulging pieces, nearly falling out themselves and breaking loose once she touched it.

'How did I not notice?'

If she hadn't felt it for herself, she would have never realized that the wall was falling apart. Whatever it was on the other side, must have been digging for a while. Did someone know she was inside? Or was something else happening? She didn't know but she must become ready.

She did not practice, she only listened.

Days passed without incident and as she grew weary of just sitting still, she had finally heard it.

Sh-Sh-Sh-Sh

Sh-Sh-Sh-Sh

Sh-Sh-Sh-Sh

'Something is digging through the stone wall!'

She could feel the vibrations make their way through the earth. Around her, rock began tumbling down, Occasionally hitting her but she paid it no mind.

The sound stopped suddenly and for a while, never picked back up. All the while, Lin Mei sat patiently.

A week later, the sounds resumed, only this time, she could feel each strike. Her sensitivity had been greatly increased during her time without sight and she could feel another map begginging to build itself on the other side of the stone that seperated the two. In her mind, a certain profile had begun taking shape.

'Claws, tall-maybe 2-3 meters, grunts, deep voice and...small chafs? I'm certain Its not human. Is there multiple beasts? Sometimes the rythm seems strange.'

She was most concerned about this part.

'If there are multiple beast, how should I fight? Is it possible to escape? Wait, I'll be blinded If I suddenly run outside wouldn't I? Thats not good.'

She suddenly thought about something, even if she manages to slip by the creatures and escape outside, it might take an entire incense stick to burn before her eyes would be able to see normally.

'This...This is a problem. I don't know how far from the surface I am.'

Forget escaping at that point, she would definitely be dead. Blinded in an unfamiliar settings, she would rather make her stance inside at that point.

Every so often the digging would start, stop and resume. Some times it was closer than others but it tended to flow in her direction. It was coming and as the time passed A plan began forming as she considered her options.

She could feel it now. The creature that was digging for her.

'Its not leaving. I Thought I could break out myself when it stopped digging but it doesn't feel like its leaving. There aren't enough steps. Its simply resting before digging again.'

One day she stood up from where she had begun resting. Today would be the day she regained her freedom and begin walking a new in the world.

Since the first time she had done this strike, she had never done it again but to her that did not matter. In her mind she had practiced this strike hundreds of times and with each passing day, she craved the feeling of freedom once more.

With on hand placed on the wall and another with its palm facing the sky, she held this position. In front of her was hundreds of pounds of broken rubble beneath her feet.

She felt giant steps clumsily walk towards her and just on the other side of the wall was a mountain of a beast. But she was relaxed. Its shape, she felt aware.

The moment the beast reached for the final push against the stone wall, Lin Mei's body roared to life.

Two finger folded across the palm with the thumb holding them back. The pointer and index finger slammed togethor and could not be pried apart. In a single motion the outstretched hand swung along its pivot and shot towards the wall. 

At the same time the stone facad came crashing down, her hand speared through it and into the belly of a soft creature. She pushed even further-almost Elbow deep as blood spurted out in large quantities. Her hand had ripped through countless soft tissues and was lodged deep inside.

Many hours had already passed since the family heads had entered the rift. Lei Biya stood patiently, waiting for them to come back. It was natural that the problem could not be resolved so easily. After all, they were dealing with some extra ordinary treasure. It would be strange if it took less time than it took for an incense stick to burn to handle the problem.

Lei Biya looked around the stone forest, the natural formation that formed intrigued her greatly. Glancing around, she had her own thoughts.

"I wonder what kind of treasure they are dealing with? It would be nice if they can acquire it for the family but that may not be likely."

Shaking her head in dismay, she continued speaking to herself, "A world that not even ascension realm demons can aproach casually. Although the family heads are stronger than them, its hard to say if they can remain unscathed against its unusual properties-they may have to destroy the treasure before they even lay hands on it."

Woosh-woosh-woosh!

The sound of air being cut caused her to turn her attention away from the rift. A small paper crane had appeared nearby out of thin air.

"this is...?" 

Grabbing the crane by the folds, she unfurled it into a long piece of paper and quickly perused the contents. She only needed a glance to understand what it was about.

"Oh, this isn't bad, it seems those little trainees are making progress. With a little more inspiration, they might grasp the topic well enough to advance. But where did they get this talisman? I don't remember giving them this kind of toy."

As creases began apperaing along the seemingly long piece of paper, she watched as the paper began folding itself back into a small crane. It was interesting to watch.

"Must be something a disciple of old fourth made. Its seems that the different parties are getting along."

She nodded her head, this was what the clan elders were hopping to see. The fewer incidences that occured, the better things would be. It wouldn't benefit anyone to have internal strife.

'Wait! how did this get here?'

Lei Biya suddenly realized a problem. Spreading out her spiritual sense, she could sense parts of the stone forest she could not feel before. Facing the rift, the intense paranoia and spiritual supression she had felt was almost gone from the surroundings. She was very relaxed around this area, where before she was tense with cold sweats. The natural formation seemed to have been broken apart on its own.

unknowingly, she released a sigh, 'The family heads must have made progress'

Inwardly, this brought immense relief. Although unlikely, there is always a possibility that something irreversible could have happened to the family heads. With the city just coming togethor, it would be extremely chaotic if something were to happen to them.

For this to have happened, she knew that they must have reached a turning point inside. Lei Biya put on a warm smile as she waited for them to arrive.

Time did not tick by slowly.

kirk-kr-kr-cr-crack-crackle

The sound of space being torn was echoing in the deepest part of the stone forest. From a tear in space emerged two figures.

"Welcome back, patriarch, matriarch, I congratulate you on your success!"

Lei biya's voice wafted into the ears of Jin Meixiu and Jin Guangli. Upon touching the ground, Jin Guangli nodded at her words. Lei Biya was not the strongest elder, however her words were always pleasant to the ears.

Jin Guangli waved it off with a big smile, "Its nothing, its nothing, my dear wife did all of the work. It seems she merely brought me to spectate."

Looking up after her greeting, Lei Biya noticed something peculiar in the hands of the family matriarch.

"Is that...the treasure that was causing the disturbances?"

Jin Meixiu wore a complicated look.

'Was it the treasure that caused the problems?'

She could not answer this clearly. The treasure that caused the problems turned into their hatchling. Wether or not the treasure was still inside, she had not seen it. What exactly happened in the moment she had been stunned, was a mystery.

But she couldn't respond with such vague words, "Its solved, but this is not the treasure inside since we had to destroy it. however this is a treasure. My treasure. Little Hai will have a sibling to look after in the future."

'eh?' Lei Biya did not know how to respond. She initially imagined a fierce fight lasting hours, but now the image had changed greatly. Many kinds of scenes began painting themselves in her head as she tried to rationalize what may have taken place inside the rift.

'Was it possible to go in with the intent to battle and come out with a kid? Does it work this way when your strong?' 

She wasn't sure, but keeping her smile warm, she did not flinch in the slightest, "Then congratulations, it seems that their will be much to look forward to in the future. Young master Jin will be happy to hear this news."

Togethor, their feet left the sandy ground, gradually taking to the skies. The group made their way over the tops of the stone forest and into the sandy fields. In the air, they were walking casually, however the space around them flickered and passed by rapidly.

As they were on the way back to their city, Jin Meixiu suddenly asked, "Have you thought about what challenge you will assign little hai next?"

Jin Guangli frowned at this question.

He gave his white beard a few thoughtful strokes, "I've thought about it but I'm not so sure. After gauging his power with our little battle, he has certainly made great progress. I'm concerned what I have in mind won't be enough."

Quisically, She continued to asked amidst their flight, "Well, what is it?"

Jin Guangli seemed to be squinting at something in a far away place, "Demon Beast hunting. For each beast he kills in his minor relm, he gets one point. One minor realm above, he gets three points and this doubles until he reaches the great barrier. Upon defeating a beast an entire realm above, he will get twenty points."

Lei Biya nodded, such a system is quite typical amongst smelting trials. It was something that touched upon the instincts of demonic nature and enabled one to refined the strength of oneself the most.

"oh, thats not a bad idea. How many points will be considered a passing score?"

As Jin Mexiu talked, Lei Biya ears were perked up as she trailed behind them. This was something she had been curious about. She didn't have any kids of her own, but always wondered what standard she should hold them to if she wanted them to be as successful as the young master.

"I think ten-thousand is a good number."

"ten-ten-thousand!?" Lei Biya gasped out loud, "Even if he killed several demons above his realm everyday for years on end, he would still not even aproach this number."

One should note that this estimate did not include breaks or the time it would take to heal and recover.

"haha! Just the way it should be! Little hai is still young so he must be ready to spend at-least this much time fighting!"

Hearing the astonishment in the elders' voice, Jin Guangli felt that this bold task was the correct choice.

But upon hearing these words, Jin Meixiu frowned, "No, that won't cut it."
 
Thoughts began swirling in the head of the sword immortal as he reconsidered it.

'Is it no good, was this too much after all?' he wondered.

Whatever decision Jin Meixiu came to, he would always consider it carefully.

"There needs to be some kind of emphasis on time. There is no point if he is just routinely hunting. It would be strenuous but not challenging. How about as he advances in realm, the creatures point he killed become adjusted accordingly, with the kills below his realm being nullified."

They all flew in silence. This was a bold thought. Without this stipulation, this would be a grind and a very long effort however, this extra rule changed the task entirely. If before, he only needed to kill and get stronger, with his points accumulating over time. Now he would need to always kill above his level. His realm would certainly increase in the time it took to complete this trial and if he killed within the same realm, he may find himself restarting all over constantly.

It was but a single rule but the resulting task became so much harder. Lei Biya thought about it and let out a sigh. This was why she devoted herself to the medicinal fields. Such effort would definitely overtax and burden the body to the point of breaking. Without any special abilities, this would simply be a death wish.

As they conversed more about the remaining smelting tirals, a fire deep in the sands begin to rise slowly out the ground. From in front, glowing crystals and fire-lights lit up the air as a city began manifesting within their sights. 

This was the Jin family city.

They left in a hurry but returned at their leisure. Upon spotting the border of the city, they began their descent.

"The family heads have returned! Quick, everyone come out!"

The city gaurds immediately spotted the trio, shop keepers, traders and wandering demons quickly gathered. As the group aproached, people began taking a curt bow in response.

Jin Guangli led the three man group through the crowds, offering a small nod to the passerby's before making his way deeper into the city. He had a regal temprement, neither overbearing nor humble, but full of a certain aura that made people admire his ability.

As they aproached the gates leading to the main compound, the entrance was already opened. After entering they settled into their responsibilities quickly.

Jin Hai was sitting in a sandstone room. The room was extremely spacious with many gems embedded into the walls and floor in a certain manner. This was a spirit gathering array setup for him to use as he cultivated.

With every breadth, the concentration of spirit energy would be enhance his demonic core and he would push his strength further beyond. However, he was not doing this purposefully. Instead, he was reviewing his fight with his father from earlier. Every strike he threw felt as if he was aiming at a wall that would not break. No matter how much he weakend his father or strenghened himself, there was no limit to the strength of this wall. The wall was too sturdy. There was nothing he could compare this too.

Jin Hai gruntled as he experimented with several possibilities in his mind. Different attack patters, different sword strikes and alternative methods. His body would twitch as he circulated this image, changing his reactions and engraining into himself what he should do differently. The fight had already ended yet Jin Hai still felt as if he was learning more about how weak he was and how strong his father was. 

'The difference between us is too big. Its, Its frustrating, I'm not strong at all.'

Opening his eyes, Jin Hai knew he had to resume working on his strength. The flow of energy was comparitively stagnant as he had not been actively absorbing it. 

Jin Hai felt his energy was far from full. At once, large amounts of demonic energy began pouring into his body from the surroundings. Although demons refine typically strengthen themselves by carefully refining energy from select sources such as demonic cores, plants and spirit stones; however the natural flowing energy in the air can be hammered away by the demonic core to strengthen onself.

knock-knock-knock.

A few taps rang on the heavy door in front of him.

Interrupting his focus, Jin Hai ceased his efforts but his eyes remained closed, "Come in."

Kirk-kir-kir-kirr

The stone door slid slowly but steadily and jin hai eye's creaked open slightly. Into his vision, the sight of two young men and a young lady appeared. These were the disciples of his caretaker he had seen before.

Stepping into the room, they all gave a respectful bow before speaking.

"Young master, the family heads has requested your presence. They are currently meeting with the rest of the elders in the main hall. We have been instructed to lead you there as soon as possible."

'something urgent?' 

Jin hai began to think but he couldn't think of anything that would require him to meet all the elder again so soon. Life with so many people felt unusually busy.

Setting himself up on his feet, he gave the trio a better look and spoke, "I understand. Lead me there."

After exiting the stone room, Jin Hai was once again surrounded by flowers. This was his personal training room and the scenary around it was quite nice. Tall stalks which seemed to contain a sun on the end of their stems brought out a certain boldness other plants he had seen could not compete with. 

As he walked, he had questions, "How do these plants handle the intense desert heat and energy? Wouldn't they wither away, like all trees do?" 

The male disciple in front stopped and pointed at the tips of a large leaf, "The gold crested sun flower is a plant that thrives in these conditions. If it was any colder, they would not be able to grow at all. Look carefully at the tips of the leaves. That sap is the condensed essense of heat and is very good for many medicines."

Jin Hai eyes opened wide as he took a look. At the very edge, what looked like golden liquid had formed, ready to drip off onto the ground.

"The other gardens seem to have a mix of tall and small flowers, how come these flower beds only have this one plant?"

This time, one of the female disciples responded, "The sap of the flower is too strong, even the leaves drip pure yang essense onto the ground. This usually poisons the other plants around it, leaving a kind of scorched earth around its base. The sun flowers don't even like growing near other sun flowers."

Even plants could fight! Jin Hai marveled at that fact. He asked a few more questions as they passed other areas but before long, a familiar sight entered his eyes once more.

The trio stopped, and bid their farewells as the young master kept going. Pushing open the halls, there was the six elders, his parents and his caretaker. 

"I greet Mother, Father and the elders!"

He offered a greeting upon his entrance. The group sitting at the large ornate table nodded in response.

"My good son, how has your rest been?" Jin guangli offered a question.

"Responding to father, I have learned much. I've seen my shortcomings and will definitely improve." 

Jin hai's voice was filled with sincerity as he began thinking quickly. He knew that in order to advance, he would need to fight. Seeing the family filled with so many powerful warriors stronger than he was, he had started to get an itch that made him want to fight. 

"Father, then, may I ask that I get a battling partner? I wish to continue to hone my skills through fighting."

Jin Guangli gave his beard a few thoughtful strokes, "Oh? Then how about participating with He Shen's gaurd camp training. I'm sure you will find something interesting there."

Cuffing his hands towards the first elder, Jin hai responded, "Then I must thank Elder Shen for taking care of me in the future."

He Shen responded in kind, "With Young master Jin Joining us, it would be our fortune. A young man with such drive will always be welcomed."

"Good! Now then, its time that I told you what your next trial shall be."

Jin Hai's ears had perked up, he was especially curious about his next trial. It had already taken him over a year to complete the first one, the second must be equally as challenging.

But as he heard the details, even the elders seemed to be suprised at the task at hand. The matter contained a new level of difficulty, in fact it was something that could go on indefinitely if one slacked off for even a day.

After he finished explaining, Jin Guangli's voiced echoed out once more, "How about it?"

Jin Hai sucked in a breadth of cold air, during the explanation, he had already begun calculating what he would need to do over the next few years. As he planned, he became excited.

"I won't fail your expectations, father!"

"Very good! Then I shall be awaiting your results...Now that we are all here, there is someone that you all have not met yet."

With that, the conversation shifted abruptly. From behind Jin Guangli an outreached hand stretched over his shoulder. It moved gently through the air and a layer of mist appeared amongst the table for everyone to see. The mist cleared away quickly, revealing the image of a large formation inside of a room. A white stone egg laid in the middle, adorning many shards of gems and plants surrounding it.

'A demonic beasts' egg!' Everyone recognized it right away but they had never seen such an appearance before.

The elders looked on in anticipation, it seemed as if the young master may be getting a beast mount. Not everyone was sure, but judging the arrays in the surrounding, they determined that its level would not be low.

This was the natural thought to have, as it was not uncommon for large families to raise beast.

'Which beasts' egg is it?' 

All the elders except for Lei Biya had this thought. They had not yet had time to disclose this information before they gathered. After returning, they immediately setup a favoritable environment for the egg and then summoned the elders from their post.

Just as Jin Hai was about to speak, His fathers voice began, "Son, you should greet her. Welcome your younger sister, Jin Dandan."

Clamor erupted around the table. Congratulations was in order and the elders hurried to pay their respects.

"Congratulations to the family heads for another promising child. The Jin Family will forever prosper!"

As everyone around congratulated Jin Hai and the clan heads on the arrival of the newest family member, Jin hai appeared slightly puzzled about something.

'But, this egg is pure white. I'm sure I hatched from a red egg. Did it change colors?'

He wasn't sure, but he was definitely sure that the egg he broken out from was as deep a red as his eyes.

Suprisingly, The family caretaker, Liu Guan, had spoken out, interrupting the joyous occasion, "Excuse me, clan heads, the egg is white. Will she be okay?"

There was great emphasis on her words. The elders at the table looked over to the mature lady sitting at the table and then took a better look at the egg in front.

Some of the elders didn't see the problem however those more knowleagable on the subject began having doubts.

Upon closer inspection, Advisor Tingfeng had thought of something and signaled towards Liu Guan and the others with more knowledge about the situation. "On second thought, there seemed to be more than just a spirit gathering array around this egg. Might you care to indulge me on this?"

"Where do i start?" Elder Biya took a breath of air between her words, "Once We arrived back in the city, it was apparent the egg did not have much vitality. Normally the mother would inbue the egg with this kind of energy however this egg is special. Not only does it absorb an intense amount of vitality to maintain structure, only the matriarch can observe what is inside the egg. This kind of situation, how do we treat it? Its very hard to do so."

As the others heard this, discussions began to break out. Different methodologies and opinions clashed as they decided what the next steps should be.

All the while, Jin Hai's eyes could not leave the image in front of him. His excitement apparent as he only cared for one thing.

'I'm going to be an elder brother, then...How should I act to be the coolest brother ever?'

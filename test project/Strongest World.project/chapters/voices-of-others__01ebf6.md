"Do yox uxdxrstand?"

It had asked this question Dozen of times already yet it seemed there was never a response. It would ask, watch, wait, yet there had never been a being that could fully comprehend. In contrast to outside, it was much easier to communicate with the beings inside the caves. Many times, it would reach out, nearly able to express a message before failure.

There seemed to be something that blocked it. What exactly, it did not know.

It began thinking about its past. Previously, it could not see as much as it did now. The things within sight was limited to only the sprawling cave system. Occasionally, it would catch glimpses of a vastly different space, and now it could directly peer into those spaces.

In those spaces, there was always something to marvel at. Upright creatures, ghastly sights and marvelous scenary. The views were far better than anything it had seen inside the caves.

The Sights it saw made it eager to see more, and it desperately tried to 'go' there but it seemed to posses no movement ability. It wanted to talk openly with those beings. Many a times it tried, but only few ever showed any reaction. Some of those things showed slight interests but others simply felt nothing.

It did not understand.

Why was it different?

It could only observe. It grasped the many languages in which these things talked to each other, the behavior they exibited, the exhuberance and feelings. It wanted to join them, to indulge in and experience yet it could not. The myriad shapes could speak, but it could not.

It introspected. 

'I am different, but how?'

A new thought appeared. There was indeed a difference. It had seen how they would periodically change. Sometimes they walked on fours, other times they walked on two. There were a few cases where they would not need to walk at all and could float through the air. They could interact with the everchanging land and shape others to its will. They seemed bounded to the world where in contrast, it was completely free but unable.

Such peculiarity, it found intriguing.

'Change. I can also change.'

Initially it could only see so much, but now it could freely look at anything within the caves and space cracks. 

But this was the wrong kind of change. Not the change that those creatures exhibited. It wanted to change like they had.

Although recently, it seemed they have changed less than before. The creatures generally had four limbs, fewer eyes and other features. It didn't know why they changed like so, but it must be better than its previous form, right?

'Form?'

'What Form am I?'

Immediately as the thought appeared, had its omniscience over the space concentrated on a certain area.

Its gaze carefully perused the area, only this area ever evoked something that was foreign to its senses. It didn't know what it was but the feeling was good. It didn't know why but 'it' must be here.

'This... I must be here, but this is not me'

It did not know why but it was sure of this. Its gaze focused again and decided to try a bit harder. Concentrating with more effort this time, even the view of the surrounding caves was removed from its sight, ending abruptly where the mist had started.

It was startled, its vision have never been abruptly cut off like this. As it gathered itself inside the mist filled area, it felt cramped. Since when it first began seeing, its vision had only grown in size. It had never been confined in such a small space like it had been now.

The feeling of familiarity began growing stronger, and by this point, it was certain that its true self was somewhere nearby.

But where?

It searched, but saw nothing.

Inside this field, there was only endless mist. From an outside point of view, a cloud seemed to have formed and was nearly fully condensed. If you inhaled a breath, you could drown with the amount of fog the field contained.

Time passed quickly but it had plenty of time. It might now know how long it has been around, but it felt it would not dissapear anytime soon. The cloud began leaching into nearby rocks, dirt and even gems as it began searching for itself. 

It didn't plan to leave a single spec of dust unturned, it was desperate to change.

"Jin Hai my son, my techniques are different from your mothers. You need to feel each change carefully and deeply understand what is taking place. With the slightest error, all of your effort would be for naught."

In a grassy plain, there was nothing but these two basking in the sunlight. One father sitting behind his son, guiding him through a specific scripture. 

"Mhmm!" The son managed a muffled response, each word his father spoke, he listened to carefully.

The father was deeply focused on his son's back, lines of red were squirming visibly. These were energy channels filled to the brim with power. They wished to break loose but the body held them back. The fathers eyes were focused intensely in serveral spots, while his hand tracing a path over the stressed channels. The channels straightened and eased under his gentle coaxing.

"Starting from your core, you must follow my guide carefully. This proccess cannot be rushed. Flow along these paths until you complete a minor circuit."

The son eyes were closed as he digested the words spoken. When he was ready, he nodded his head soundlessly.

The father traced a path between the many channels on the body. Togethor, they started and ended at the energy core, this was a scripture of his for gathering and tempering the surrounding energy once it was in the body.

Energy began flowing and followed the traced path carefully. At times when the energy channels began twisting, the father would softly trace over the spot, returning the channels to their original form.

The shadows of the two had moved slightly around them when the last of the energy returned to the core. Sweat had began to build up on the forehead of the son.

Thick fingers pressed into certain points along the body, each coinciding with a spot along the circuit.

"These spots, you must retain a portion of your energy. These are called deposits and its where you will form the spirit crystals that make up your circuit."

"Now do it, Again."

Once more, energy began to flow from the energy core, permeating the surrounding channels with a sparkling red energy. This energy sometimes distorted the channels it flowed through, requiring the father to assist. Other times it flowed flawlessly, without error or twisting. As the son practiced more, this process gradually got faster and faster.

Small nodules began to form in the body as small amounts of spirit lingered in places.

They repeated this proccess until their shadows made a full rotation around their bodies, eventually becoming one with the darkness that surrounded them.

At some point, they had stopped. The son had began meditating cross legged and focused on stabalizing himself. The sweat dripping from his forehead down to his back was a testament to the amount of effort he had put fourth. The father stood up and watched.

The father was named Jin Guangli and as he looked at his son, Jin Hai, he could not help but smile brightly. He had always dreamed of having a family of his own, but had never been experienced in these affairs. It was not until a handful of years ago that he met his wife that he finally realized all the things he had been missing.

From behind Jin Guangli, a pair of slender arms wrapped themselves around his shoulders. Long Red hair spilled itself along the left side of his body, forming a picturisque view of a mature lady leaning into his side, 

"How was it? Is he able to learn?"

Softly spoken words flowed into his ears and Jin Guangli could not help but savor the feeling. It was his wife, Jin Meixiu.

Grabbing hold of the hands around him, Jin Guangli turned to face his wife, "Able to? ha-ha, if I could learn as fast as he could, would I not be the elder of some Great sect? Its a waste that I only have these methods."

"Oh-ho? I thought you were undefeated across nine realms and ten worlds? What's this talk about a better method. Could it be you're teaching little Hai something improper?"

Jin Meixiu proded the side of the poor self-proclaimed sword immortal playfully. His blushing face had already turned as red as a cherry. Jin Guangli was quite shameless in the past. As all are when they are boasting during courtship, some words often came back to haunt him. Especially these words. Despite claiming to be unparralelled in his world, his wife was actually stronger than he was, and not by an insignificant amount.

Jin Guangli quickly recovered and put on a noble air, "Nonsense, What improper method? Many sects requested my teachings in the past. I'm an honorable guest elder in several prominent sects. Who would dare say so?" 

The wife chuckled at his response and her sights landed on Jin hai. Her crimson eyes were full of thoughts as they observed the changes happening inside of her son. He was already seven yet his entire life so far was spent in this small world, cultivating scriptures. It was unfortunate, but they had to find a suitable world to live in. They could not stay in the cave worlds for too long.

Jin Meixiu sights did not leave little hai as she spoke, "Do you hate me for this?"

"Certainly Not!"

His response was immediate. Pulling his wife further into his embrace, he comforted her directly, "There are many things to dislike about the world, but no decision of yours would I ever disagree with."

Jin Meixiu felt warm and fuzzy on the inside. As a red demon, her family followed authoritarian rules, as such, she had never felt such intense feelings from anyone else before. All of it was new to her but such things, she thought wasn't bad.

She did not leave her family willingly but even so, the things she began to experience changed her wordly views. She would never go back once she found how much the world had instore for her.

Jin Meixiu turned her gaze towards her son and said directly, "This fractured world has been showing signs of instability, sooner or later we will be forced to relocate."

"So soon? Its only been a few months since the last change." Jin guangli frowned and his eyes closed. A faint blue aura swelled up around him before dispersing. Deep in concentration his thoughts spread outward. In an instant, his mind was met with the edge of the grass plains. The edge of the plains was spotted with desert sand and receeding grasslands. The further into the sand he went, the more visible the changes to the grasslands.

Tall grasses wilted into brown stems and black dirt decayed into uniform grains. The once luscious grass was nowhere in sight and the trees left standing in these parts were already scorched as if they had spent decades without a single drop of water.

The imbalance of the world had been spreading, decay and structure had already flipped, currupting the nearby spiritual energy that dominated the world they were inside of.

"The erosion is spreading alarmingly fast!" Jin Guangli opened his eyes in shock, "In at most a week this place will become no different from a scorched paradise. This change is too fast, something else must be happening." 

Jin guangli stole a glance at his son, "Have you already found a suitable place to go to?"

Jin meixiu gave a shake of the head. There was no such thing.

She had already took a long look around, she was also at a loss. These spaces are normally connected to other spaces of similar size yet this one had no such thing. Although good for resting, it also meant they were stuck in this isolated world. The original void tunnel that connected here had already collapsed and stabilized.

"External energy has already begun flowing back into this fractured world in certain parts. Maybe there will be an opportunity when the changes fully take place. With the influx of energy, many demonic creatures will also arrive and fill the space. I believe it won't be long until the remaining world collapse under such conditions. Its possible that this space will become incomparably large soon.

Jin Guangli agreed, for the last decade, these worlds seemed stable but they frequently collapsed into a completely different setting. From wetlands to forest, densly populated habitats to deserted realms. After their son had hatched, they began looking for a world, one were their lives were stable.

Standing up, Jin Hai's frustration had already left his eyebrows. The sweat cooled and his mental state was in perfect condition. There was none of the strain left on his body from the earlier practice.

"How do you feel?" Jin Meixiu was slightly anxious, human techniques were inferior to demonic methods and completely incompatible with each others body. However if you start early enough, the demonic inheritance can't suppress the human one. And only in this way could you practice both.

A red glow began eminating from the surface of Jin Hai's Skin. The Red glow had a tinge of blue mixed into it. Just under the surface of his skin, his energy channels silently carried energy as he did a full revolution of the technique.

"I feel great!" Jin Hai let out an enthusiastic shout. His smile was big and bright. It filled both parents with energy as they watched. 

"Good son, rest tonight. Later we will practice the first movement of the Blue Mountain Sword Technique."

Behind Jin Hai's bright red eyes, was just a tinge of excitement. His true feelings lied with something else.

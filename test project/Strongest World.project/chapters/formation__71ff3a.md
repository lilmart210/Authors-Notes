"What a shame, If I had just a few more moments, I could have finished the battle properly. Such a feeling leaves a bad taste."

Jin guangli was shaking his head as he recounted his experiences. His eyes were now fully awake but his demeanor was poor. He did not want to leave his battle as it was.

Taking notice of his surroundings, his wife was deeper inside the cave. The previous animosity from the forest couldn't be felt inside this space. At his leisure he started forward, "Oh, it seems I must have been far too immersed. Looks like you already obtained the treasure that was to be had, haha, as expected. Nothing can escape your grasp!"

He was admiring the cave as he spoke, the glistening cave had diminished in quality but it was still a sight to behold. Cracked white stone filled the cave and precious gems were embedded into the cave walls. It was simply a luxerious pocket of space.

'hmm? is something the matter, why she's standing still, saying nothing?'

He was slightly worried so he put his musings to the side. Striding directly to Meixiu's side, he squinted carefully at her hands, "This is?"

"I'm...Not sure...the treasure had an attacking field around it, so I had blocked off my mind. But as I was refining the tablet, I somehow still fell under its assault. It was only a moment, but when I awoke, This was in my hands."

Jin Meixiu was puzzled. While refinning, the resistence from the book had took an unexpected turn but when she had finally thought she had a grasp, she slipped back under the illusions.

"It looks like a creatures egg of sorts. Maybe our son will have a companion beast, but I don't see any traces of animals here. Did you already kill it?"

A sudden feeling of wrongness began welling his stomach as soon he finished the words. He didn't know what was wrong but he felt slightly disgusted at his own words.

Flinching, Meixiu responded, "No, there was no spiritual beasts, only a floating stone book. The book vanished, turning into this. I'm not sure what to do with this."

Without the supression of the spiritual senses, Jin Meixiu spiritual awareness began spreading out. Concentrating on the egg in her hands, her mental powers began drilling into the shell to uncover what was underneath.

However upon coming into contact with the shell, it vanished outright. 

'Whats going on? Did the stone book develop an eggshell? Its resistence feels similar to the supression field.'

Trying again only yielded the same results. Guangli also tried however he was no better.

Jin Guangli frowned, "This is an odd egg, maybe we can try cracking it open, it doesn't seem like its made of anything durable. Perhaps we can figure out something then."

As soon as the words landed, he regretted it. A strong feeling of revulsion began festering in his stomach. This feeling was surreal as if he had eaten some poisonous concoction from a poison master and could taste each bitter ingrediant.

"No! Absolutely not!" 

Bringing the egg closer to her embrace, Jin Meixiu felt disgust at the words. She could not pin point where the feeling came from and she reacted purely out of instincts. She couldn't stomach the idea of breaking this egg.

"Now that I think about it, its better that we don't. This feeling, its strange, what is this?"

"You feel this too?"

Both of them began taking a better look at the egg. Besides the fact that they could not peer into it with their spirit, there was no indicator on the outside shell to indicate what it could be. Greeting their eyes was the pure white shell, the same white as the stone that engolfed the white stone walls.

"This is definitely some creatures egg, but the only creatures around are..."

Jin guangli trailed off. Although he was trapped inside of the illusions earlier, he was confident that his body was under his control. Although he could not move freely, he knew his situation better than anyone. But this was different when it came to his wife.

Maybe she...

Meixiu caught on to what he was trying to say and starting blushing.

"Such crude thinking, as if I would need to do something like that. Counting the time I spent unconsious after touching the book was only a few short seconds. Don't tell me this is all the time you need?"

"A-Ahem!" 

Jin Guangli decisively interjected, "Then what is this feeling? Its almost as if we are talking about little hai and not some treasure? Maybe this is the treasure's attempt to stop us from refinning it."

Meixiu thought about this. 

"The treasure was in the Spirit King realm and it could even confuse an Ascended demon with ease. Although its effects are greatly dimished on us, its not wrong to think that it could still have some sort of effect. At this level, it may even have some sentience. Feeling threatened, its likely done something to us that we cannot detect."

The more they thought about it, the more it seemed true.

"Lets try it again."

Raising his hand, Jin Guangli eyes focused as he declared, "I'm going to destroy this egg."

But he did not feel anything in response. Neither did Meixiu.

"Maybe you need to actually have the intent, otherwise its just a feint. If it truly messed with our minds then we have to have the intent in order to trigger its response."

Setting the egg on the floor, Jin Meixiu eyes deepend in color. Killing intent began to arise from under her gaze as energy welled from within. The already cracked stone walls began shattering under the rising intensity and the stalactites on the ceiling began shaking.

An outstretched hand made its way to surface of the egg, causing the egg to wobble and shake. But as the hand drew closer and closer, the trembling gradually slowed as her hand inched closer and closer. Immense guilt and pain began consumming her, eating away at her intent to kill. 

She realized this was not a simple trick of the mind, this feeling came from deep within. Her hand continued to travel closer towards the egg however the promised violence never arrived. With the gentlest of touches, Meixiu was crouching on the floor as she grasped the stone white egg and pulled it into her embrace once more.

Despite being almost at the peak of the Wordly King stage, tears threatened to form at the corners of her eyes.

"Why is it like this? I can't do it! I can't do it!"

crr-crrik-crack!

A crack that had formed on the surface of the stone roof had spread too far. Several dozen meter stone spikes began to loosen themselves from the roof of the cave.

Jin Guangli merely gave it a glance, the myriad stones turned into dust before scattering amongst the wind in cave.

"Then we should let the egg be, we shouldn't force it more than this." 

Helping his dear wife up, Jin Guangli felt terrible at their thoughts so far. They had only tried to open up the egg to see what was inside. It seems that somewhere they forgot about the most rational thing, to simply wait and see what pops out.

"We should wait, in this condition, the treasure can't escape now that its been reduced to this state. We also accomplished our original objective, that mental supression field is completely gone."

Jin Meixiu nodded but then shook her head, "I still have a way to see what is inside."

"You do?"

Jin Guangli looked inquisitively at his wife.

'Then why have we been trying to break the egg?'

He felt that they should have started with this method.

"But...its just, that this memory..." Jin Meixiu started talking but trailed off.

A light clicked on in guangli's mind, "You must have awakened during this trip! Very good, thats great! Power is power, no matter who teaches you, you must remember this!"

Jin Guangli knew what kind of succession demons possesed. It was different from humans who gained powers through the master and disciple ceremony. Memories passed on in blood from family is where their abilities come from. This is the source of variant techniques. 

"Your right, regardless of who teaches, it is mines to keep. I shouldn't keep on ignoring it."

She said so, but she still could not completely agree. Who did she learn from? It was the person she wanted to see the least. It wasn't something she could accept so easily.

These thoughts circled within her mind as she stared off towards the ends of the cave. Her eyes were open but Within her sight, she could only see her father speaking to her. The cliff she sat on while she listened had no limits and a mysterious feeling began permeating her body.

Jin Meixiu squinted and began speaking solemly, "I'm, not as talented as my son. Maybe without this journey I would have never even awaken. My father had such hopes for me and my brother, but neither of us could even make it to the patterned stage."

As she spoke, Cracks began appearing beneath the red scales and on the surface of her skin. The cracks appeared along the surface of her arms and legs as they traveled about carelessly.

In Meixiu's mind the teachings and words she had heard countless times began clicking togethor. 

"Even now, I'm unsure. Is becoming stronger such a great thing? Now, I only wish to raise our son, but if it means I need to do this and hold onto these teachings, then I will."

Each arm was covered in these small cracks. They wiggled and squirmed throughout the layers of skin as dark red lines began connecting with each other.

Jin Guangli was intrigued as he looked as his wife's transformation. The cracked layers of skin began opening slowly, as if waking from a long, dreadful nap.

'Eyes, these are all eyes! Although, in comparison to little hai, it looks like a very detailed tattoo, so this must be why its the first step.'

The transformation was different compared to his son. Jin Guangli was interested in what powers she would have. Would it be different from their son? He had his guesses.

After each eye opened in full, Jin Meixiu began, "I posses the queen's physique. little hai, the wisdom physique. It manifest across the arms, torso and legs. This hints that the technique I will gain will apply to many areas or people at once."

Looking at the egg, she continued, "My technique allows me to look at distant places. Its a bit different from spiritual will and the sixth sense. Without a void tunnel, I can still peer into hidden spaces. Even spacial rings could be looked into."

Sucking in a breadth of cold air, Jin guangli couldn't help but marvel at the technique, "So powerful? I can think of plenty of benefits to be gained from this."

Jin Meixiu's body shifted, the patern of eyes on her body began looking at the egg at once. Inside of Meixiu's mind, a certain image began to form.

As she took a look, she let out a gasp, "T-t-this is, how can this be!"

"Whats wrong?"

She didn't respond right away. Instead, the picture Guangli painted earlier appeared in her mind and a slight blush apppeared on her face.

'Am I really such a brute? No way! I don't believe I did anything out of the ordinary after falling into the illusion. But, this is...clearly...'

Noticing Meixiu's weird expression, Jin Guangli grew even more confused, "Did you figure out the egg? Is it some kind of demonic beast?"

She gave Guangli a strange look, "Yea-it is..."

Cutting a small slit on her hand and position her hand above the egg. Jin Guangli gave her a quizical look and some of the cloudiness in his mind began to go away. He began having a certain thought.

Plat.

The egg shook slightly, the red blood dripping onto its surface was absorbed into the shell immediately.

Silence.

Jin Guangli didn't say a word, he cut a similarly sized cut on his palm and dripped it carefully.

Plat, plat, plat.

Each drop, that dripped vanished amongst the white exterior of the shell.

Taking out a small vial, Jin Mexiu poured some of its contents onto the egg as well. 

kr-kr-crak-cracle!

The falling liquid was vaporized immediately. It was unable to get close to the exterior.

a single phrase appeared withing their minds, 'This is Blood resonance'

Jin Guangli gave a small response, "It seems, that we were looking to far, the demonic beast in the caves were very close at hand."

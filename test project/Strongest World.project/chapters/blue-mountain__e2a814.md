"The Blue Mountain Sword Technique has an emphasis on dominating the opponent. Each strike should be unyielding, never backing down from direct confrontation..." Jin guangli stood with his sword stabbed directly into the earth. His mouth was the only thing moving as his hands rested heavily on his sword. Back straight, eyes bright, he narrated the principles of the sword technique to his son, Jin Hai.

Jin Hai moved to copy his fathers stance, sticking his sword into the hard ground, he was always a prudent student. As the words fell onto his ears, he would listen with intent as he moved accordingly.

"The first form of the Blue Mountain sword technique is simple to grasp. With the world on your shoulders, the weight of the sword becomes immeasurable!"

Jin Guangli paused, the bright green grass beneath his feet showed signs of cracking as he focused sternly on his sword. Blue mist oozed from all over his body as he shouted, "Thousand Catty Stance!"

Crik-k-k-k

Thousands of cracks began sprawling out from his feet into the shape of a spider web into the surroundings. Waves of blue energy rolled from his body onto his sword, which was being lifted slowly from its sword shaped hole in the ground. The sound of metal clashing could be heard as the sword made its way to his chest

Waves of formless pressure was emitted, Jin Hai found it hard to keep himself steady under this force and nearly buckled under its weight. He held his sword tightly. His blood red eyes could not help but be amazed. 

Jin Hai stared in wonder, 'So Fierce? Yet its only a stance!'

He did not show it, but he had been curious about human techniques for a long time. Until recently, he had been strictly training his demonic techniques which he inherited by being the son of his mother. It was tedius and boring, requiring constant inner reflection and mental cultivation. Despite asking previously, neither of them allowed him to learn from his father. 

It was not until they discovered he could mimick the aura of a human sword master that they let him practice.

Gradually, the pressure eased and Jin hai was able to steady his stance. He took a long look around. The original vibrant field had been over turned for hundreds of feet. Rocks had been pushed up to the surface, exposing the dark soil far beneath the surface. Besides those spiritual beasts whose weight could leave person-sized imprints on the ground, he had never seen such a demonstration of strength like this before. In this small peaceful world, It was all new...

Jin Hai stared intensely at his father, "Human Techniques are so cool! How come mom thinks they are so weak and useless?"

"What a question you've asked."

The father took a second to choose his words. The son waited in return, eyes wide and observant.

"You as a demon, do not know what you are capable of, yet you are not weak nor useless. Humans will never advance unless they are taught. Occasionally, we can make some interesting things however its normally in response to nature."

The father thought about his words carefully again before responding.

"Physically, there isn't really a difference between a human like myself and your mother, a demon. But there is a difference and its not something that can be made up for so easily."

Jin Guangli hands pointed towards the center of his body as he spoke, "The human method of internalizing energy has been around since the beginning of time. It didn't start with humans and it also didn't start with the intention of fighting others. It evolved as a simple way to be stronger in a hostile environment. To adapt to circumstances, you could say. Other creatures knew this but humans are not ones to give in so easily. They mimicked, and stole from others until they could stand on their own in the world. Thus began the cycle of resisting the world until they could claim it for their own."

His gaze then made its way towards his son chest, "But demons? Your true nature is intact. There is no need to fight for every scrap of energy. Just by existing your body is strenghened and reinforced. You only need to feel for your true nature, act according to their instincts, growing ever more powerful. Yet humans must defy their nature and the world. But to obtain what is not theirs, could such a thing be easy?"

Jin hai grasp on his sword loosened as he pondered these words. His father said nothing more and only watched. At times like this, a smile would etch itself onto the father's face. When Jin Hai recovered, the smile would be gone, long replaced by a stern look and voice.

Jin Hai manuevered to an area of the fields with less damage. There seemed to be sand interlaced into the dirt in places. He stabbed his sword into the ground and recited the scripture.

"Strike Unyielding, Confront Directly"

His hands gripped his sword tightly. Closing his red eyes, a faint blue energy began travelling through his energy channels. They would leave and take energy from dense deposits of spirit within his body. As it made a full revolution around his body, blue mist began emerging from the surface of his body.

"With the world on your shoulders"

The originally lush grass began to show signs of wavering and the sword began sinking deeper into the ground.

"The weight of the sword...becomes immeasurable, Thousand Catty Stance!"

Jin Hai's energy channels had begun to swell, veins were visible to the naked eye as he managed to stop the sword from sinking further. The sword seemed to hold all the burdensome things in the world, no matter his leverage, it took all his strength and more to stop it from sinking. To say less of lifting, it would be a blessing to even get it back to where it was.

Struggling to lift the sword from the ground, he had a new found respect for his father. The sword becomes heavier the more advanced the user was. He did not know how heavy his father sword was when he excecuted this technique, but he did know he had mastered the sword. Such a sword would be akin to lifting thousands-maybe millions of the one in his hand.

'I can't dissapoint father's expectations!'

Jin Hai groaned under the pressure. He refussed to give in so easily.

"The Blue Mountain Butterfly was sealed for a hundred years!"

Through pursed lips, Jin Hai etched out words of self-encouragement, "There was no despiar or desperation. There was only joy in the challenge"

He held the sword guard in a vice clamp, blue waves boiled off his skin into the air and his back that caved from the weight, gradually straightened under the pressure.

"With the weight of a mountain above it, the Butterfly soared to new heights"

Shing!

Jin Hai stood up directly, sword steady in front of his chest, gaze firmly looking ahead. The sound of the unsheathing could be heard for miles. The blood under his skin began to permeate through as it flowed freely from his strained body.

There was hardly a single part of him that was untouched. 

As soon as the blood began to flow, the blue aura faded and translucent red scales began to form over parts of his body that was injured, the blood that was flowing stopped immediately and the bright red crystaline scales faded from sight. 

This never ceased to amaze the father. His son's skin was now flawless, as if he had never practiced a single day in his life. This was apart of the red demons inheritance, a variant ability passed down in their clan. After hatching, their shell hardens, condensing into scales that protects them from any and everything. Although they can control it, it is always active and never needs to be consciously used.

Actually, he was quite reluctant to teach his son these sword moves. They are extremely taxing on the body and while powerful, extremely dentrimental to the bones and tissues. For humans, hundreds of precious medicines needed to be used in order to practice successfully. Even he himself needed spiritual baths before and after practice or he would be unable to cultivate it.

But seeing his son's eager gaze and his mother's aproval, there was nothing he could do. He could only teach and supervise.

This was the difference between humans and demons. Humans were shallow in the face of demons, unable to cope with even the smallest difficulties without external aid.

"Again"

The words were flat, eyes that were firm and without emotion oversaw as they oversaw Jin Hai's training. He repeatedly lifted the sword from the ground. Behind the facade was an excited old man, he had never been as happy as he was this day. He realized there was nothing more he needed in this life and was content with doing this each day.

The Sky darkened, brightened and darkened countless times...

"Ha! Mountain Uplift!"

Swoosh!

An underhanded sword strike kicked up the dirt from the ground. In these few weeks, Jin Hai had learned six sword moves of the Blue Mountain sword technique. His father watched him off to the side, commenting on his movements periodically. The dirt had a fine layer of sand mixed into it now, which was lifted into the air whenever Jin Hai practised his swings.

"Third Move, Retrieval!"

Jin Hai's hand shook, The rising sword ascent slowed, trembling in his hands. The sword that appeared lighter than a feather became thousands of pounds heavier in an instant. With both hands on the hilt, the sword descended violently. There was a distinct whistling as the blade's edge neared the ground before stopping suddenly.

Woosh!

Sand, dirt and wind flew out from the ground. Despite not being hit, the sandy dirt had a distinct line below where the sword hovered sligthly.

Feeling his fathers eyes staring at him intensely, Jin Hai followed through with the rest of the sword moves he had learned. The last three moves were a set and needed to be executed swiftly one after the other.

Xing-Xing-Xing

the sound of air being cut echoed in the clearing. Once the last move had landed, Jin Hai brought the sword back to the starting stance, Thousand Catty Stance.

"Mhmm, stop here for now." Jin Guangli's voice sounded into the clearing. "You've grasped these moves very well, it is time we begin battling."

Jin Hai's eyes lit up, he had been wanting to battle for some time now. Between practicing with his mother and father, he had never truly fought before. "Really? We can fight!? he-he, I get to fight father now!" 

His enthusiasm was infectious, even Jin Guangli had a rare slight smile.

"Settle down, we won't be doing that so soon. Instead, you will focus on a different fight..."

Jin Guangli stood up from the stone he took as a seat. 

"Humans must go through three temperings before they can begin cultivating in earnest. Qi Gathering, Spirit building and finally bloodline awakening. This process is known as crystalization. Demonic creatures like yourself are all born above these levels. Humans must toil for years, maybe even decades before they even reach your starting point. A great amount of trainees never see the end of this path. Humans could be considered quite pitiful."

There was an expression of nostalgia amidst his face, there was untold hardships and effort. No one could know exactly what he was thinking but he continued his thoughts, "Although these are things humans don't have naturally, each human cultivator who wishes to reach the peak has a certain drive. There is understanding in the suffering. For Demons, such complexity does not exist. You were born at the Flood Demon Level, which is roughly equaivalent to a peak Bloodline awakened human. In my eyes, you have missed too much."

On Jin Guangli face, there was a serious expression written over it. Unlike during training, where he was stone faced and cold, right now He had a certain passion. These feelings leached into the air and could not be ignored.

"Since there are three temperings for humans, there must be three for you as well. This is the first."

A hand swiped amidst the air and a Vibrant Blue Sword appeared. The air around it seemed to warp as a formless pressure built up in the air. Several straps attatched to the hilt floated freely like the wings of a butterfly around the sword. It hovered in the air silently.

Just by looking at the sword, Jin Hai felt his breathing stop for a moment.

"This is my natal artifact, Swallow-Tail Sword. It has been with me every step of my journey and holds my conviction to the arts. Right now its devoid of Energy and unable to attack. Your job is simple, you just need to grab it."

Jin hai stared hard at the floating weapon. His father was a Wordly King and a natal artifact would have the same level of cultivation as he. Forget grabbing the weapon, he was not sure he could survive walking near it.

The blue mist around him faded and the red in his eyes deepened further. Transluscent Red Scales began to emerge on his skin as he slowly reached out to the sword in front of him.

Sensing him neering, the sword shivered, In in instant it brightened.

Bang!

Jin hai felt an immense pressure land in the center of his chest, he coughed up a mouthful of blood before being flung back a thousand feet, crashing into overturned dirt and weeds. He didn't stop until a wilting tree bore the impact of him crashing into it. The broken Red Scales around his chest reformed immediately. The red aura around him flared and began moving rapidly across his body to repair the damage he had just recieved.

"D-dad, Wha-what is thi-" amidst the blood from his lips he began to stammer out but was immediately interrupted.

"That was the will of the Swallow-tail Sword. The Blue Mountain aura naturally exhibits itself in its surrounding. The stronger your will, the closer you can get. The closer you get, the stronger the rebound. This could be as simple as a few days or as long as a few years. As long as you wish to learn more, you must not return unless it is with this sword."

Jin Guangli appeared nonchalant as he continued, "Oh and there are a few tricks, I would be careful If I were you."

With these words Jin Guangli vanished from the clearing, his energy could not be felt from anywhere nearby. Jin hai red eyes opened wide as he struggled to breathe, his father had already left.

Jin Hai expression turned cold, "I was doing so well in front of father yet this sword wishes to make a fool of me!"

Seeing that his father was no longer around, his child like demeanor had vanished. Anger swelled from the bottom of his heart, forming a new goal in his mind as he slowly sat up.

Thump! Thump! Thump!

Muted First landed on a stone facade, they crackled and shook before another well timed strike hit against the wall. There was a steady rythym as Lin Mei made sure not to use too much strength but just enough that she could feel a certain weakness permeate throughout her tendons, up her arm and through her body. The feeling made her want to retract her hand, but she held it there, until the time for the other first landed.

It was methodical and consistent. This was her only path forward.

'Strike. Wait. Hit. Wait. Strike. wait...'

she repeated these words endlessly, until she could no longer keep the pace. Both hands withrawn from the wall, she honed her intent and struck with both hands at the same time, 

"Break!"

Bang!

Teeth gnashed at the sensation, as if pillars of fire had lurched out at her arms, grabbing hold of them tighly, burning away the flesh and blood beneath them.

'Hold it....Hold it in...You can't flinch from just this much pain.'

At this point blood had begun seeping from her lips as her canines were mercilessly clamped on any part of her tongue and gums that happened to be in the way. But she held it in, for as long as possible, her arms swollen, knuckles raw with the rest of her hand damaged greatly. In the cave with little sensory, pain had become one of the fewest vivid stimulations she had left to experience.

Sweat dripped down her body, as a cool sensation began rushing around, with it came a released breadth and mental relief, 'It so subtle that I didn't feel this at first but now I am sure of it now. Its this feeling, isn't it?'

Pulling her hand off the wall, she brought them to her side and sat down, back straight against the wall. She gave herself a chance to catch up on the breathing she had missed and introspected, 'Today should be around the fourth month since i've woken up. What to do, What to do~'

Despite the bleak outlook, she was actually feeling quite swell at the moment but suddenly she began feeling something irrational bubbling up, scrapping her nails across the damp stone floor, "Worthless Bastard! when I find you, I'll personally boil your bones into nourishing broth!" 

Her il-wishes continued for a few more mouth fulls before she suddenly stopped and closed her eyes, 'calm, be calm, there is no reason to snap. Remember why you are here. Remember your goal'

A deep, cold breadth in and a warm breadth out. 'Right, know what your goal is, you must always strive towards it. You must complete it. No matter what.'

Her eyes opened but despite being unable to make out any shapes in the darkness, she felt her vision was brighter. In a previous incident, she had been too frustrated and hurt herself in the proccess. It took days to recover, the longest period of time she had spent recovering yet. She didn't know how long this would last and if she needed to stay for years here, she would. She couldn't let such a feeling control her or she would never last long enough to leave.

'Its that clensing feeling thats healing me isn't it. Its also been several weeks since I last drunk that water. It seems I didn't need it after all. I've never healed fast like this before, so this must means I've awakened some kind of healing factor.'

Her fingers traced over hands once more and the raw exterior had already begun to scab over. She was very familiar with this, within the day the exterior would shed and before she would realize it later, they would be soft and delicate. It was as if nothing had ever happened to them.

'Its curious, very curious. But why does it heal with all of my scars?'

Her fingers had been running over sections on her hands and arms where deep scars had been present. Underneath the white skin was deep, dark lines, clearly tracing her energy channels, and above them were healed but deep groves in her skin that showed tremendous past trauma. The reason why they didn't heal fully, she didn't quite understand. Now that she had this ability, she thought that maybe certain types of damage could not be healed. Certainly, her previous injuries were still there.

'ha~ these thoughts are useless, theres only one thing to do.' 

Behind her, the stone wall stood unchanged this entire time.

After a short rest, she finally stood and and faced the wall once more. This time, she poised her legs into a striking position. If she wanted to improve, she would have to train all of her body.

"Its either you, or me!"

A Shout cut through a dense forest, "Don't Let that bear get back to its cave, we can't stop it inside of there!"

Figures shaded by the thick foliage above raced through the undergrowth with their weapons raised. All of them were equiped with a stunning hilt-less long sword.

Moving swiftly through the forest, they were desperately trying to catch up however they were not as desperate as the bear. The beast knew the area better than they did and was intelligent enough to know that it was in dire straits. The blood falling from its shoulder, back and neck warmed its body and functioned as a stern reminder what will happen if it even thinks to slow down even slighly.

A hand was gripped tighly around a long sword as he projected his thoughts, "You're not getting away!"

'Distant Cloud Steps!'

His body seemed almost illusiory as he took great leaps forward, the distance between the running bear and the young man was crossed in an instant.

A sword strike, appeared from the corner of the beast eye, hurriedly, it turned its body immediately as the strike sank directly into its large flank-barely missing its neck.

Roar!

The beast let out a sharp cry, as the young man quickly withdrew its sword but as it did so, gnarly teeth, the same size as his hand, filled his vision.

'Not good!'

With the same thought his body suddenly became illusionary and vanished from that spot, only to reappear a short distance away. Sword in hand, ready for his defense, It wasn't until he laid his eyes back on the running bear that he realized his mistake.

Collapsing to one knee, he pounded the ground, "Ah! It was a feint, it had no intention of fighting to begin with!"

Heavy Foot steps aproached quickly as a rather large individual arrived. His exasperated words rang out, "You let it escape?"

The young man looked sharply at the one who spoke, "Let? Do you think I let it escape after hunting it for days? Nonsense! This is so infuriating! Now we have to find another creature to hunt, if we can even find one! And why are you all so slow!?"

He eyed 4 others as they finally made their appearance. Out of breadth and panting heavily, they had only heard the tail of the shouting.

One of the four responded in between breadths, "Senior brother, we, we only recently joined the sect, how could we be a match for you?"

The senior brother calmed his nerves sligthly, if the elders hadn't insisted on pairing the old with the new, he would already be done with this field excersize. He didn't understand the purpose of mixing them all togethor like this but it wasn't like he could complain to them about it.

"Whatever, regardless, start tracking that bear, you can do that right? And whatever you do, don't follow a stone clawing bear into its cave. I can't tell you how many disciples make that fatal mistake."

The other five disciples shouted in unison, "Yes Senior!" 

The senior disciple thrusted his sword into the ground and started leaning against a tree.

'Hmm, thats not right, we didn't discuss stone bears did we?'

As the other disciples began to split off, a voice slipped back into their ear, "Wait"

The others stopped and turned around.

"We never covered what makes stone clawing bears dangerous, did we?"

The other disciples looked at each other before making their way back to their senior.

Picking up a stick the size of his forarm, he swung it around half-hazardly, narrowly avoiding his fellow disciples. 

"What do you all think is the most dangerous aspect of a stone clawing bear? Is it the jaws and teeth? Maybe even the paws?"

As several disciples thought about it for a second, the tallest of all the people there spoke out, "Isn't it obvious? Its their claws. How else would a stone clawing bear get their name? Its from digging through stone to build their dens."

Snap!

The stick that was being swung around had been broken in the midpoint, dangling lamely as if its mimicking a broken bone.

"Oh, your quite knowledgable alright, however you just cost yourself an arm"

The tall disciple frowned, but showed no other emotion. His eyes wandered to the other disciples around him.

"Its their tenacity! right? how else could it escape senior brother Yan?"

Crack!

Another section of the stick seemed to have lost its strength to stay straight, folding in on itself once more.

"I appreciate the flattery, however that won't save you in a fight. Your leg has sadly been mauled."

Senior disciple Yan Looked at the remaining three. His eyes sharp as he inspected them.

'Humph, they are too nervous, unable to answer even a simple question like this. Its not like its a real assignment.'

"You've all hunted before but I doubt you have ever hunted a beast inside of its house. Why? Because instincively, you know a place where a prey hides, is where it believes to be safe from us, the hunter. But you probably think you could go in if you really wanted too."

Interjecting in, the tall disciple refuted, "What difference does it make? Inside or outside of its shell, only strength matters."

Senior brother Yan thew the stick at the tallest disciple Without warning. The tall and muscular disciple was also fast and nimble, hands reached to intercept the stick in the air when the unasuming stick burst into pieces, shattering into countless splinters.

The tall disciple yelled out in pain, "Agh! What are you doing!" 

He began carefully picked out countless tiny pieces of wood from his face. He was turning red all over as each splinter was carefully plucked one by one. 

"After a desperate fight with the bear, you finally died to its machinations. How unfortunate for you Zhengshang."

Increased blood flow created great pressure in the ears of the bear. The bear had not stopped running, not even for a second. It never looked backed once and only kept running. grunts filled the air on every stride but it never stopped. Survival had possesed it and as of right now, it couldn't stop as fear drove it forward. A distinct lack of overgrowth and a gravel clearing appeared in its sights and it dove head first into an opening a few times the size of its body.

Its desperation carried over as it ran further and further into the cave until only dim reflections of light was visible. This deep in the cave, the moss that grew on the walls became the primary source for sight as any hope for natural sunlight was lost early into the journey inside.

In front of it, two cubs had been wrestling with each other and upon hearing the stumbling steps of the large momma bear, they immeditely turned their attention towards her.

But just as they turned their attention, had the momma bear collapsed from exhaustion. The cubs saw no sustenance in the momma bears mouth, only deep wounds and blood begginning to pool around them. Whimpers escaped the mouths of the cubs as they began climbing over the slumped beast.

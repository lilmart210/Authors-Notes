"You fool! How could you let them escape!?" A tall man with an ugly expression yelled at a man on the floor.

Slamming his hand into the table, there was more words he wanted to say but none of them expressed his anger correctly.

There was a long sigh before the tall man gradually took his seat.

"After chasing those damn rats for years, I've finally trapped them in my greatest scheme! but not only have I discovered that they escaped from certain imprisonment, your saying they also took the most important possessions as well!?"

The man on the floor was drenched in sweat, these possessions were far more important than his small life and they needed to be here for the auction in the coming weeks.

And It shouldn't need to be said what would happen to the mayor if even one of those items could not be recovered.
 
"Mayor Qin, do not worry, I have already sent a few people after them. There is even a diviner amongst those people and I promise that he is also quite powerful." 

to be a diviner was a rare skill to have and being acquianted with one brought many benefits. People skilled in their proffessions are often aloof existence but having the ability to command one showed that the man shivering on the floor was not a small existence.

But Mayor Qin didn't even bother to look at the him, looking otherwise untinterested in whatever the man had to say. The man's face was pressed far into the floorboards, his fear was evident.

Mayor Qin grunted. If he hadn't already sent people after them then he really didn't need this manager, there would be more competant people to take his place. After all, there were many underlings, each vying for a chance that the rubbish did not have.

Actually, the man on the ground already knew his role as the Manager was over, Mayor Qin was a vicious man but he treated those in his circle with immense benefits. It could be said everything he had was given by the mayor.

But he knew that what had happened today was far below Mayor Qins bottom line. If he couldn't provide a satisfactory answer then his life would certainly be over.

"Mayor Qin, I happened to have a favor from a powerful warrior outside the city, They can definitely send some people to help search on account of a past favor"

But Mayor Qin still didn't respond.

Mayor Qin seemed to be thinking of something, much to the expense of the managers well being. He had already said several more important words but the mayor hadn't spared him any attention. If he could see that the mayor was currently thinking deeply about something, he wouldn't have said any words at all.

Mayor qin seemed to frown before asking his manager a question, "Which items did those people take exactly?"

"Sir, the World Revealing stone, 4 spritiual beasts hide and the locked storage ring."

Seemingly understanding something, A grin appeared on Mayor Qins face, Mayor Qin reached into space and an old rock appeared in his hands. 

Looking at the rock, several edges glowed brightly and if the man on the floor was looking up, he would become confused because this was clearly the World Revealing Stone. Despite being made of stone, its surfaced rippled like the waves on the sea and formed an arrow that pointed in a certain direction.

Just now, the direction of the arrow suddenly changed and Mayor Qin grinned. The World Revealing stone vanished and he immediately issued commands.

"Let that diviner and his team keep pursuing those fellas, shoushan you will follow me, theres something that we have to go do."

"Yes...a-Yes!" hearing that means that he gets to keep his little life, if only for a few minutes more. Manager Shoushan got up quickly and bowed while Mayor Qin walked past before following Mayor Qin outside his office...

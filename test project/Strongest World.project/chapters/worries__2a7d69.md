Jin Guangli was holding a thick blanket, wrapping it around the shoulders of his wife, the cover drapped down to the floor. He took a knee in front of her, looking deep into her eyes. The fire behind him could be clearly seen reflecting from within as the smoke from the burning wood rose into the air. The burning embers almost as deep a red as the gems they reflected from.

"You've been holding yourself back all this time, my dear, you should have already advanced past this. I've felt for a long time that you've been afraid of something, but i've ignored it. Maybe I shouldn't have, because now I see fear wearing itself on your face. With me here, what could you be worried about?"

Jin Meixiu thought about those words for a moment. There was nothing but the splintering of wood to break the silence. The more she thought, the more she felt that she should not run from it any longer.

Taking a deep breadth, she let out some words, "It's my father. After seeing little hai, I know he will be after our son. I-I don't know what we should do. I don't know what we can do."

"Your Father?" Jin Guangli let out a gasp of surprise, for as long he knew her, she never mentioned her family much, if at all. He had thought that maybe they had...so he did not ask, but seeing as 'that' is not the case he let out a sigh of relief. "Is that not a joyous thing, why would he be a cause of such trouble?"

There was nothing but pain flashing in her eyes as she spoke, "It seems, I never properly introduced where I am from."

Jin Guangli grasped her hand gently as she talked, never once wavering in his support. She felt at ease in his presence, her nervousness diminishing as she continued to speak.

"My Father-he is the patriarch of the Red Demon Clan and I am his first born child. He is not a particularly warm person, holding neither empathy or compassion. He only believes in strength and nothing else. Under his hands, the clan has never known respect for those that had poor abilities. And when I failed to meet his expectations, he turned to my younger brother. When he failed as well, he turned to the rest of the clan. Not many could live up to what he wanted leaving him greatly unsated. He made Everyone under a certain level of strength embark on a thousand year journey. It was a specific journey where you must achieve one of his objectives, such as concuring a world before returning." 

"Concur a world!?" Jin Guangli felt a shock run through his body. What kind of rule was that? Feeling that there was more, he decided not to interrupt her words further.

"Mhmm, although, not in the literal sense. Father sent most of the younger generation to worlds dominated by human methods and techniques. There, we were told to climb to the peak of that realms cultivation. Once we had the strength to return, we were promised many things, even the seat of the patriarch if we survived."

Jin Guangli's eyes met her's, he could feel the emotions of those words. What did it mean to be the inheritor of a clan like hers? Such a thing would arose the interest of many who were ambitous, but he knew his wife. She definitely did not want to indulge in those matters. From her tone, he could sense where this story was going, "Is this where I found you in my world?"

Suprisingly, Jin Meixiu shook her head, "I landed in a particularly weak world compared to yours. Your world has all kinds of beasts, demons and humans. How would I hope to surive with my meager power at the time?"

Jin Guangli felt that this made sense.

"The strongest in this world was a stage weaker than our elders. It took a century before I had enough strength to travel that world freely. Wehn I accomplished that, The first thing I did was search for my brother, There was no way I would allow him to return to the clan. But, by the time I found him, it was already too late. There was nothing left for me to find. Sometime during my search, his Soul Light had extinguished."

Deeply red eyes were starting to become moist. Jin Guangli felt a rock fall in his heart, for his soul light to extinguish, meant that he either got rid of the mark imprinted on himself or died. But looking at his wife's words and feelings, he knew that it was not so simple as an imprinted mark vanishing, she must know he had died.

"Father was too arrogant, he believed that Red Demons were vastly superior to any other creature, that we were special and closer in tune to nature and cannot be felled at any level. But there is another clan of variant demons called blue demons, just as strong as red demons in their own arts. There is also the common grey demon, who can devlop their own variant abilities at any point in time. Humans are just demons who can't form spiritual cores but can increase their strength rapidly. Is this balance he spoke of so stable? Who can say they cannot surpass demons one day? Its not as if we are anything significant! He sent all of us to die, just to relish in his own self satisfaction."

There as anger burning in Jin Meixiu's eyes. The more she spoke, the angrier she became, there was nothing more that she hated than her father in this world.

But her anger was shortlived, sinking into her chair. She pulled the soft blanket up to her eyes as her voice dimminishing. She continued, "Now, little hai awakened a treasure in hid body at such a young age, when father finds out, I'm sure he will take him away in a heartbeat. There is nothing he craves more than talent."

Jin Guangli snorted as he stood up, "Humph! And why would we let him? I'm only afraid he won't accept my challenge if he dares come."

Amidst his brovado, he noticed his wife giving him a strange look, "Is something wrong with what I said?"

Jin Meixiu's voice was flat, "We can't."

"Can't what?"

"We can't stop him. Unless he decides not to, there is simply no way to stop him from taking little hai. He is inconceivably powerful, so much so, it would be easier to abolish our cultivation and restart than to continue and reach his realm. Even after 300 years, I'm only at this level."

Jin Guangli's eyes squinted, he himself had spent almost 200 years to reach his current cultivation yet he was still a ways off from his wife in strength. Such a statement meant that her father was truly that strong.

"Then, he's in the Spirit King Realm?"

Jin Meixiu shook her head, "Its meaningless to figure out what realm he is in. He is one of the world defenders for my home world, making him atleast on par with the distraction tier. As for his exact realm, I'm not too sure. Our relationship was not so close where I dared ask about those things."

Sweat began dripping down his face as he realized what kind of trouble they may be in. The distraction realm had absolute control over their internal structure and decay. It was not a stretch to consider them immortal gods at that point. Forget fighting, he was certain they could not damage the skin on his body even if he stood still and did nothing.

Jin Guangli entered into deep thought about this.

Amidst his trouble he realized something, "Your father is so strong, then why has he not come to find you after all these years? You say our son awakened a treasure, but your strength is not small. With his abilities he would definitely know about this and would be able to contact you in a heartbeat."

"I-I'm not sure why..."

Jin Meixiu pondered about this question. Truthfully, she had always been worried about this. She only wished to live quietly by herself, it was not until she met Guangli that she experienced the things that the world had to offer. But wandering the world like this came with a risk. She had always been subtly afraid that her father would appear after hearing about her somehow and taking her back to a place she felt she did not belong.

Jin Guangli got up and took a few paces around the room. He had been in deep thought when an idea began taking form in his head. The more he thought about this, the more he felt that this made the most sense.

A smile made its way onto his face as he spoke, "Its not that he doesn't want to, but I believe that he can't! After sending all the young family members to their deaths, I'm sure the other family heads could no longer stand his tyranny, removing him from the seat of the patriarch would be the most reasonable choice. After all, he would have single handedly crippled his own clan."

But Jin Meixiu was not convinced, "Nonsense, The leader of the clan word's are heavier than gold. To begin with, my clan hardly has even a few dozen living members. Even if others don't agree, who would say no? They would never oppose him."

"And he would, never..."

Jin Meixiu felt a frog began to rise in her throat as the words came out,

"relinquish his...power." 

Jin Meixiu stumbled over her own words. She knew her father's personality very well, he would never give in, no matter who it was or what appeared. He had the strength to back it up. It was not a stretch to say that their was nothing anyone could do about him.

But then why was she still free? Not only this but there had been no communication ever since she left the clan. In her eyes, her father was a bastion of strength, there was never something that he could not do.

He would also have their soul lights. She removed the mark on her soul through tremendous effort and her brother had died so his mark would have faded, yet her father had not made a single move about this, leaving her to find her brothers body first. 

This was unusual.

Her father was not a kind person but he wanted them to become powerhouses of the clan. The training, the expectations, it could be said that he cared about them but not in a lovingly way. He was a demon supremacist to a fault and he would certainly be there to retrieve their bodies if nothing else.

But he had not.

Seeing the downtrodden look on his mothers face, Jin Hai could not hold it in any longer. He pushed the door in and declared out loud, "I'm going to beat that evil old man! He will know not to bully my family!"

Jin Hai's stern declaration was met with the laughter of his father, "Ha-ha, thats my son, so what if he's strong? Then it only means we need to get stronger."

Jin Meixiu felt her eyes roll at this statement. She didn't know if she should laugh with them or cry at their misfortune. Either way, she could not refute this as the tense atmosphere slipped away entirely.

"We only need to get stronger..." Jin Meixiu repeated this line back, rubbing Jin Hai's hair as she did. For so long now, she had been living with her fear. Refusing to become better, refusing to advance and becoming complacent. Now that she seemed to have something to fight for, she did not want to fall so easily.

Feeling her finger flow through his hair, Jin Meixiu knew what she wanted to protect more than anything else. She steeled her heart, 'I don't know whats keeping father busy for so long, but since I have this chance, then I must use it properly. I must become stronger. Strong enough to decide my own fate.'

She continued to play with his hair for a while more before her voice fell onto the boy's ears, "Jin Hai, eavesdropping is not something good kids do. Even if we know you arethere, you should pretend to have heard nothing and walked away."

Jin Hai's felt he had done something wrong but strangely, he did not regret his words.

"visiting so late, you must have wanted something?"

"Ah!" The boy shrieked, "I nearly forgot again!"

Jin Hai ring finger glew and several demonic cores began falling from the air into his hands. The demonic cores were cut crudely in various places but they held its own charm. Not perfect, but not an eyesore, they held the faces that Jin Hai loved the most.

"I have a gift for you, mom, dad, I carved some stones as a gift."

Placing each figure into the hands of their respective likeness, Jin Hai was grinning from ear to ear. He had spent the entire morning carving these out under the instruction of Yu Lin. He had wanted to give this to them when he first arive but became busy as the day wore on.

The pair accepted their gifts. What started as a solemn conversation, had ended with new found joy. With more than a year seperating their last meeting, they had plenty to talk about with each other. The trio continued their talks long into the night.

But Amidst their reunion, Jin Meixiu and Jin Guangli suddenly stopped, their expressions becoming unnatural.

Immediately, They shared a thought, 'Someone is watching us!'

A dreadful sensation had washed over the burning hot sands. Had they been sligthly weaker, their spirits would have missed it entirely. It only lingered for a split second before moving, with the powerful couple nearly missing it themselves. It seem they could only hold its interest for a little moment  before its attention had shifted elsewhere.

Jin Hai noticed their estranged reactions, 'eh, why did father stop talking? Maybe he wants me to figure out that attack from the sword myself. But i've already thought for so long about it.'

He began brainstorming some more, but noticed that his mother also stopped and seemed to be spacing out as well. Another thought appeared, "Maybe they didn't like those carvings after all?"

He took a few more glances between them both, it did not seem like that was the case either.

Jin hai was nervous, What was wrong?

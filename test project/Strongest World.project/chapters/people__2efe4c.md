Outside a particular room, was a courtyard with a variety of flowers. There were a few tough looking men standing around, each briming with power and sporting hard faces. No one would dare aproach with them outside of it. Even the servants found it hard moving around, shivering at the thought of bringing more food to the room.

"Its been a pleasure speaking with you, ShouShan! ha-ha, then as you said, I'll go with that!"

Inside the room, a cheery voice was relishing many kinds of fine wine and meat. On the small table between two people, was specially prepared cuts of nearly every native animal. For the young master Lei, the most delicious food by far, was the benefits he had gained.

Looking across the table, Shoushan smiled and responded in kind, "I still need to make some preparations for these things. Young master Lei, I'll be taking my leave then. Since you've come from so far, how about staying the night?"

"Then I'll have to trouble you some more for today!"

Shoushan gave a small bow before retreating from the room.

'These few hours have been quite eventful...It should be enough'

Shoushan was still very frightful of city lord Qin's return. Although he gave him some tasks to take care of, it was far from redeeeming him of his earlier failures. 

He had been lost within his thoughts when a young boy intercepted him as he made his way past the door to the courtyard, "Reporting, a message from the border patrol and another addressed directly to senior."

Picking up a scroll and a envelope, Shoushan dismiseed the young one before inspecting the two messages carefully. 

Unfurling the scroll, was a message addressed from the gaurds who stood at the border 'No signs of suspects located near city border. No signs in the city. An individual was captured for questioning. Not a wanted suspect and released after a brief interview. All is normal.'

Shoushan began to sweat as he finished reading through the scroll. How could things be normal? All of it was irrelevant and so far, he made no headway into his investigation. It seemed to be more accurate to say that those three simply vanished from their custody rather than escaped. How else could there simply be no signs at all?

'Reports of activity in the immediate forest. Gaurds have been dispatched in order to identify a cause'

Shoushan squinted before these words at the bottom. He was aprehensive if this was what he was after, but at this point he wanted something, anything that could be used to explain things to Lord Qin.

Shoushan turned his attention to an envelope addressed to himself. Ripping the corners off the envelope, revealed the envelope unfolded into a letter.

There was nothing written at all, only blood splattered over the paper.

Shoushan immediately glanced up to see the messenger nearly out of his sight and shouted, "Stop!"

The young man froze. Frightened, he hurried back before the manager. His face was slightly ashen. He had done no wrong yet it seemed the manager would soon be taking his anger out on him.

How unfair~!

"How was this letter recieved?"

The boy responded plainly, "Answering Senior, This envelope was delivered by canister from a carrying bird."

Shoushan knew that already, his interests lied in something else, "And the bird? What of it?"

The boys' head was still bowed and he once again answered plainly, "Senior, seeing as to how the bird was not the kind our office would use, the office had let it go. It was also very reluctant to stay."

Shoushan eyes narrowed to pin needles, he was certain of his next answer but hoped it not to be true, "When did this happen? What about the birds' species?"

The young boy was uncertain about this, he reached into his long robes and pulled out a tablet. On it was some notes, categorized neatly into columns and rows. He scanned briefly before responding.

"We recieved it aproximately two hours ago. This description seems to paint the bird as some kind of hawk. It has a note that its exact species was hard to tell"

Shoushan felt a ripple of emotion go through his body. He was certain that the hawk was the personal spiritual beast of the diviner he had commissioned. He remember that old man in particular liked to boast how that bird was special. He saw for himself that the bird had advanced reasoning and could do more than just understand words. He would often use it as a means to show off his abilities to his clients.

Shoushan dismissed the boy and he headed straight to city lord's office. The day had gone from hell to a nightmarish-hell! So far he had achieved nothing yet it got worse every minute. Even the things he had hope for turned up as wasted effort. He wish he could wake up from this dream but it seemed he was not asleep in the first place.

Pushing open the door, he was greeted with a familiar room. Only a few hours prior had he been negotiating for his life. Unlike then, Lord Qin was nowhere to be seen.

Shoushan frowned, as officials landlocked between so many giants, it was rare for them to leave the building for such a long time. Their movements was observed at all times during the day, so its was better to not be in the open. Even more so now that they were in possesion of those special items. 

He hesitated to return home at times like these.

Fumbling through his clothes, he pulled out a small wooden box. It was carved with exquisite shapes and patterns. Opening it revealed several strips of yellow paper, embezled with precious minerals and colored with distinctive markings.

Shoushan found a chair to sit and began eying each talisman. These were the thousand mile voice talismans and they came in pairs. These could be used to communicate with anyone a thousand miles away discretely. They cost him a fortune to obtain and he would never use them unlesss it was his last resort. 

'I could still wait for the lord to return...'

Shoushan tried to convince himself, but time was something he did not have a lot of. After his initial split from lord Qin, a few hours had already passed but the search was still on. At this rate, it was unkown just when those brats would be found.

'With lord Qin being a decisive man, its precisely why I should let him know now rather than later. The longer I wait, the worse the result could be'

Shoushan shook away his qualms and grabbed a talisman. He did not resist as his energy was sucked into the paper. It glowed faintly as it caught fire and Shoushan thoughts took shape.

'My search team has failed. They have encountered difficulties, maybe even foreign interferance. The border has seen and heard nothing. Please advice my next course of action'

The talisman had burned completely. Now he only needed to wait. He set a few incense sticks on fire, to help ease his nerves.

Thus he waited.

Some people arrived with their own matters, to them, seeing Shoushan was just as good as seeing the city lord. He dealt with them as they came.

A dozen more sticks were lit sequentially after each other as he waited some more and by now, all of the sticks were ashes in a tray. The smell lingered for a while before eventually, he couldn't even smell them anymore.

There had been no response. By now, Shoushan was once again nervous but thought about what this meant. As government officials, they could not leave the city easily. If they could not leave then they had to stay within the city and that was well within the boundaries of the talisman. Even if he was within the surrounding forest or past it, he would have still have recieved his message.

This meant that the talisman was delivered safely and the city lord could respond at will. But no such thing had happened.

There was only a few situations left where one would be unable to recieve a talisman. They were either dead, unable to respond or so far away that the talisman could not reach.

But how could lord Qin go so far so suddenly? He suddenly recalled that he had left out earlier with the city lord, as he was under intense pressure, he had forgetten to ask where they were initially headed.

"Thats strange, what matter did he need my help with? Now that I think about it, he seemed reluctant to part at first"

He tried to recall the direction the city lord walked away in, yet he failed to remember.

'why-why can't I remember anything?'

There was nothing to recall, he seemed to have forgotten even what color the clothes the lord had worn today. Everything seemed grayish.

Shoushan called out, "Bring me someone now!"

A servant lady, stepped into the room. She was dressed in formal attire. She gave a curt bow, "This servant is here"

"I need a response from young master Lei about what he remembers seeing from lord Qin earlier in the day."

The servant nodded, "This one understands"

Shoushan watched as the lady left in a hurry and began thinking to himself. There was simply too much to think about, but it was not long before he had recieved a response.

Young master lei had not seen lord Qin at all today.

Shoushan felt his heart jump, 'This is impossible, didn't he greet him first?'

He wracked his brain but still failed to understand. Putting this matter aside, he began thinking about the worst possible outcomes. The search team he had sent out already encountered failure. Facing those three girls, Shoushan would never believe that they would loose to some brats. In this case, foreign inteferance was a high possibility. There was reports of a scuffle, but after investigation, it had nothing to do with his team. Maybe other outside forces could have deliberately intervened quietly.

But this was also unlikely. Some things just did not add up. Shoushan began racking his mind, trying to imagine what sort of plot he had been thrown into.

'The hawk, it only had a piece of bloody paper attatched to it. Its probable the diviner simply died as he began writing his notes. But the timing...that timing is too strange'

The timing was strange indeed.

The hawk was recieved not too long after he had parted with lord Qin. This being the case, he ruled out foreign interferance. They would gain nothing by killing a search party before they had the chance to find something, So it could not have been the surrounding clans' doing. Those brats would also have still been in the city. Logically, they would be unable to organize something themselves after being trapped in the city, they would have been too focused on escaping first and foremost.

Shoushans thoughts stirred some more.

This meant that someone else outside the city had simply killed his party. Either someone else who he hired and wanted less competition or simply an unfortunate encounter with a madman. His only other outside contacts were an information broker and a warrior who owed him a favor. Neither of which he had the time to contact before his meeting with the young master.

He had ruled them out.

'Was it simply a stroke of bad luck?'

Shoushan thought about city lord Qin. He could not contact him, and he was sure he left out with the lord yet the young master had no recollection of this event.

"How odd, how could he simply vanish? just like...just like those brats!"

"Damn it!" Shoushan yelled in frustration.

He got up from his chair and walked past the desk that the lord of the city would sit at. In front of him was a glass window. It was quite thick, so the image he saw was poor, but he could still make out what was happening underneath his gaze.

People moved to and from places, some fast, some slow, but they never stayed within his sights for long.

He had a realization that city lord Qin had initially included him in his plan. He would disapear, just like those brats, just like he did. However he stayed and thus his judgement was passed.

He would die, as a scapegoat. The clans would soon realize that the items are missing and they will demand to know why. Lord Qin was a thorough man, he would never be caught, Shoushan was sure of his methods.

but did he want to die?

Shoushan balled his fist in anger. In theory, he still had a week left to come up with an explanation. Unlike lord Qin, he had a family in Extreme Path City, a wife, a daughter, a son. Unless he wanted to see them hanging from the city gates, he would need to escape soon or fool everyone.

He began writing and talking to himself. In the blink of an eye, the moon was hanging. Hours went by as he sat and devised a plausible plan. He deemed It impossible to escape. He had no abilities to speak of in this department. In that case, he would need to fool everyone.

A plan? He couldn't simply tell everyone that city lord betrayed everyone's trust and trust that they would understand. He also could not cancel the auction as some people were highly interested in what they had to sell. Doing so would invite far too much suspician.

He needed a new narrative. One which combined reality with his interests. Shoushans eyes were already red, he had been struggling for so long to come up with a plan yet he still fell short. 

'Not this...'

'Not this either...'

'This is plausible, but the outcome... Too risky!'

Nothing he planned would work.

Shoushans writing became messier and his eyes were no longer as bright as before. He was aging visibly as the night wore on, with even some of his hair being thrown into the trash along with an abundance of failed plans.

But he never stopped. For stopping meant the end of him and what he fought for.

Sizzle~

A fire began burning in front of him and from the fire emerged a yellow paper. Shoushan was stunned, it was a talisman. His fatigued eyes shot open, grabbing the paper as it began burning away in his hands. In his mind, words began to appear.

"Dear Shoushan, I have thought it over, but You must hate this old man. Unfortunately, I was never destined to stay. In a way, this can be seen as your promotion"

Shoushan grimaced, 'as if! all you have left me with is a ceremony with death!'

"I hxve a lixlx axvixxe, xux...."

Thoughts continued to form but the words became blurred, illusionary almost. He had never seen such a phenomenom before, what had happened to the message?

As he struggled to understand, something else began to unfurl in his mind. He felt his understanding become stronger. The words in his mind trailed off into the silence and what replaced it was a heightened sense of understanding.

All around shoushan was his plans he had devised and as he looked at each paper, he felt his initial inspiration, each emotion he etched into the paper as he thought of his future and the effort it took to write them.

Stolen goods? A plan? nonsense, at the current moment there was not anything he could not comprehend. The dots he needed connected immediately.

'To think it was such a simple task...'

He began writing his final plan, he was afraid such a state would not last forever, in this moment of clarity, understanding came from within and inspiration came from his thrown away plans. All he needed to do now was pass the blame onto someone else. A silent auction, to respect lord Qin who had sadly died during his duty. A silent auction, where no one knew who bought what. Those that obtained something good would naturally have their belongings coveted by the others. He could simply point in anyone's direction and the crowd would lunge over. This was not pulling the blinds over a wolfs eyes, but showing them were the sheep had hidden.

The thoughts flowed easily onto his paper and once the details were finished, drowsiness began to overwhelm him. He was spent.

In his final moments of clarity, He swallowed back all his critique and hatred for the old man, in its place, was nothing but respect and awe. He had not understood what he was given but he knew that the city lord still cared deeply about him. Had he not cared about his little assistent, he would not have helped him like this. It seemed he still needed such help even after spending so much time with such a great man.

Shoushans eyes gradually closed as the final sparks from the message faded away. The light in his mind was growing brighter and brighter and he even dreamed of lording over the city. As long as he lives past the next two weeks, he would be cemented as the city lord. He already began formulating who to blame and in his dreams he felt like an emperor.

Amidst his chuckling from his sleep, there seemed to be something, something that had been watching for a long time now.

It waited, and waited but its gaze moved on as it seemed it had not gotten an answer to what it had wanted...

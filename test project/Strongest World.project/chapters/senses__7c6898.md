"Agh!" Lin Mei exited her lotus position, grabbing her stomach as she shouted in a fit, "Damn that bastard, if I could boil him alive I would! I'm Completely crippled, dammit!"

For the last few days, she had been trying to gather energy, with it, should would be able to see in the dark and start her journey once more but every single energy channel in her body was either broken, mangled, completely unable to contain energy much less form a circuit neccesary for cultivation. 

She was furious at this problem but it wasn't something she could solve Immediately. Turning to the next problem, she began to think.

'Its been almost a week, yet I have not eaten anything. This wouldn't be a problem if I could circulate energy within my body but i'm currently a cripple. I should be nearly starved to death by now. Whats going on?'

She didn't understand this at all. Before being crippled, she was merely in the Energy gathering stage, unable to go a couple days without eating something containing energy. She should be feeling extremely lethargic and keeling over around this time. Energy was taken into the body to replinish and strenghen itself. But she could barely feel the wild energy and the energy she tried to intake only harmed her and escaped anyways. If things were like this, she was effectively a mortal-no worse than a mortal. Even without cultivating, a body would absorb energy, but as it stands, she was a furnace with a dwindling flame, burning the fuel it did have but getting weaker with every passing moment.

This had her worried, but she couldn't feel her strength diminishing. In fact, besides the fact that she was unable to cultivate, each passing day, she felt more and more alive. This was not normal. 

'Something is wrong but I don't know what. But maybe, this is a blessing in disguise? Although I can't use energy to lighten the surroundings, it doesn't seem my situation is getting worse. Now, how do I escape?'

She had already traced out the cave with hand. There were many cracks along the wall and even some parts that spewed water, but she really had no idea how she could leave this pocket of rocks.

'Would there be a moment like before to take me out? But, this is clearly a different cave from when I had entered. Not only that, but I definitely won't be able to figure out the position I had been in to have been brought to this cave.'

There was nothing she could see but her eyes still furrowed togethor, before suddenly she shouted, "Ah! thats right!"

once again, she walked around the wall, but this time, she stopped when she felt familiar strokes on the tips of her fingers and water beneath her feet. She took several large steps away from the wall and stopped just short of a fourth step. 

'This should be about where I woke up, but now, do i wait?'

She didn't really understand the way the transportation worked. How often does it occur? Is the stone neccesary? She really did not know.

She squated, taking a seat in the lotus position on the ground. Regardless of how or when it occured, she would have to wait and see.

'Even if I can't cultivate my body, I can still meditate. Maybe, I can figure out whats going on with my body.'

Her fist were open and relaxed but they tightened as she re-affirmed her commitment.

"I will not die a second time."

...

Hours turned into days and days into weeks. It had been an entire month yet her situation inside the cave had not changed. She rested in the lotus position in a familiar spot and practiced moving around in the cave. 

She could not see due to the darkness and she was unable to control her inner energy to see clearly but she practiced anyways. Her commitment was not so weak that she simply stop.

"Ha! Ha! Ha!"

Sweat poured down the side of her face as several punches cut into the air, she was practicing the movements she learned at the academy. At first it was awkward, she would loose her sense of balance after a single swing. She hadn't realized how much she needed sight in order to stay upright. But after practicing several stances and punches over the last few weeks, things began to settle in place.

'If I stayed in here for years, would that make me a hermit?'

She chuckled at the thought and sat down on the ground again. Entering into a meditative state, she did her usual contemplation.

'At some point I even stopped drinking water. If I had no internal energy then I should have perished a long time ago. I dwell on this everyday but I can't make sense of it. Do I have internal energy or not? Something has happened to me, but what?'

She was stumped on this. Her body felt pretty good, but how long this state would last was of big concern to her.

'Have I been cursed?'

the thought appeared suddenly, but she shot this down immediately.

'What kind of curse would have positive benefits? A blessing then? Nah, There must be something special about this cave that allow me to live this way. If thats the case, then leaving would probably cause problems...No-I must leave, I definitely won't stay here forever. I can't be complacent.'

She let the thoughts roam around in her mind for a while before nothing but the drips of water filled her brain. She exhausted that topic and began thinking of the next.

'Its been several days since I last tried, it's time to try again.'

Her back straight and her mind focused on her body, she tried once again to sense the pathway that energy took in her body, beckoning for the wild energy around her for stimulus as she tried to trace a path. 

A drip of energy, no bigger than the drip of water that could be seen gathering along the walls began traveling in her channels. It traveled slowly as it made its way into a single channel and it did not take long until it came across a broken segment in her body.

A burning sensation began to build up below around her stomach area. The searing heat as the droplet of energy flowed past the first chokepoint.

'Come on-I got this' she thought aloud in her mind. She was familiar with the pain by now but there were countless twist and turns. This sensation would only intensify as she continued.

The droplet of energy traveled slowly, several incense sticks worth of time passed as she deeply concentrated on controlling it. The heat she felt had begun turning some parts of her body red and swelling up. It was painful, like poking at an already bruised gash but she persisted. This was The first step in cultivation - energy gathering. She needed to flood her body with energy. Once energy is aplenty in the body, she could begin enhancing her basic movements.

Previously her body had a decent amount of energy inside of it, within a few years she would likely break through but now, she had nothing.

Silence~

In her mind, she had finally made some progress, but she had yet to travel the entire channel when she discovered something very unusual ahead.

'A forked path in my channels? I'm-I'm not familiar with this way.' 

She was sligthly hesitant but this lapse in concentration had caused the single droplet of energy to pop immediately into mist.

Her eyes shot wide open, "Guwagh!" She shouted as she keeled over, face first into the cold damp rock. Her hands had been frantically grabbing at her stomach as the feeling of something melting her insides had taken over. Squirming on the ground, she spat and hissed as she recoiled in excruciating agony.

Speaking through gritted teeth and saliva, she muttered, "Why-Why is there a split in my energy channels? Agh!"

She let out a yelp, as she questioned her own sanity. Although her energy channels were broken, that only meant they were unable to pass energy through them, not that they should be different. In fact, energy channels couldn't change, thats why aptitude was determined at birth. 

Why were they different?

Between heaves of deep breaths and grunting, she didn't want to think anymore. Curling up into a ball, she felt cold, and extremely lethargic. Her vision was always dark but now she felt that it was darker than before. It wasn't long before darkness covered the rest of her senses as she coughed her way to sleep.

...

"What happened?" Mind groggy despite the rest, reminded her of when she had first woken up in this place. As she laid there, nothing made sense for a long period of time.

Sitting upright into the lotus position, she was about to begin introspection when she remembered what put her to sleep to begin with.

Her eyes jetted around, looking at nothing before a long breath escaped her lips, "Forget it."

She laid on her side for a period of time.

Currently her body felt quite good-a conundrum from its actual state. She didn't know why it was like this but even after harming herself earlier, she felt fine after some rest.

But that didn't bring her any comfort, she started twitching a bit as an unnerving thought began to spread, 'Something is wrong, even my body is different.'

'Is this the after effects-but no matter what, energy channels don't change like that, Is this even my body?"

She could not help but recall when she had first awakened. Who was she reaching for and why did she think of them?

She felt a certain alertness that she had not felt before.

A hand reached for her back immediately after the thought had arisen. She could feel the countless scares that littered her body, that coincided with her destroyed channels but more importantly, she could feel large, somewhat healed scars that should belong to that spiritual beast that attacked her.

The beating sound of her heart calmed down a bit, but there were still so many things that were wrong that she didn't know where to start. Anger began to show through gritted teeth and her fist tightened. Sitting up on her knees, her anger only intensified. "Screw You! That damned mayor and that smiling bastard. You should have killed me or left me alone, why leave me in this state! I can't gain strength like this!"

"How am I to survive!?"

Her shy and timidness had dissipaited long ago, leaving something different overnight. Her hands had begun gripping the ground unconsiously as her nails began leaving scratches on the rock. Her anger was flowing into a fine river of emotions that began pushing out all her other emotions. She felt, strange, impulsive and extremely bothered.

Lin Mei couldn't put a finger on what this was but she needed an outlet. Taking a proper seat this time, she began again, her resolve was not so weak that she would give up after only after a few tries.

Constant coughing and fits of pain echoed in the cave, this was already her twelfth attempt, "I can't-I can't hurt myself more than I have now, even if I have to check every path, if I can complete even a single circuit, I will win!"

A mist condensed into a solid drop of pale energy, this time, with practiced efficiency, it traveled through a narrow channel, carefully avoiding the damaged edges of her energy channels and imperfect blemishes in its path. It soon arrived to a split end. She had already explored one path and it was completely severed. The pain down that road was too intense-she couldn't take this path. 

There was no hesitation as the small droplet of energy made its way through. Normally, a teacher would point out the best path a student should take to circulate their energy,according to a specific technique, but she did not have that luxury and could only experiment.

The drop of energy needed to move quickly or it would dissipate completely. Energy channels were like rivers and once they began to flow, they could not be stopped or stalled. They would bend, fracture or at worst break.

This channel the energy was flowing through was particularly narrow, it was difficult to pass the droplet through without searing heat forming amongst her abdomen. She powered through it and began anticipating her next move. In her memories, after the first channel was a small split but that was no longer true. In her mind, the energy channel she was traveling through was frayed along the edges, with countless splits and turns. It was a giant interconnected network. She tried her best to only travel in the most complete channels because if she wasn't careful, this energy could eject itself into her body, destroying her internal organs in the process.

Sweat piled up in droves as she was thouroughly soaked in her concentration, 'I need to make sure I don't travel in circles around my body, that could be bad'

At some point, the narrow channels began to widen and she grew in anticipation, as this meant she was aproaching the first gathering point inside her body. These are usually large section of channel that could store energy. The first steps would be to fill these with energy as you navigated your circulation technique. But upon reaching this point the energy channel began to narrow tremendously. Countless holes in the surrounding material could be seen and although this was the furthest she made it in, she knew she couldn't continue further. The droplet of energy began to be absorbed into its surroundings and gradually turn into mist.

Her eyes opened, and for a time, they only stared.

'After trying several times, I made it to the first point however...It seems it won't work after all. Even If I can gather more energy, I would still be unable to cultivate without damaging myself.'

She would have to change goals. As far as she knew, there were no way to heal broken channels and if she wanted to cultivate in her current state, she would have to strenghen her body instead. But forget about body cultivation techniques, she only had third rate martial arts from the cities academy, a far cry from anything that could strengthen her body to break through any meaningful level.

Standing up, she reached out into space and walked until an unmovable force stopped her hand. An idea had begun to form but she had not yet thought about it fully, 'My body seems to still be able to recover even without energy, what if I...'

At an arms length of away from the wall, she stood in a striking pose. A certain queasiness began to take over as she had slight issues standing straight up, "Ah! This is so stupid!"

Despite being unable to see anything in the darkness, she closed her eyes in anticipation as she threw out a strike.

Bam!

Knuckles collided and bounced off the stone wall as a bit of blood began dripping onto the stone below.

"hisssssssss", Pushing air through her teeth as she gritted her lips, she let out a warm breath and took in a breath of cold, damp air.

'It hurts, It hurts, It hurts...'

She repeated over in her head but externally she did not voice it. She knew this was going to be a mental game. If she couldn't absorb energy to strengthen herself, then this would be the only way to become stronger until she could escape.

Oddly enough, not even a full moment had passed and the blood that dripped had stopped. She could no longer feel a warm feeling dripping away and the pain that had been present had vanished completely.

Although she couldn't see these changes, she could feel it quite vividly, 'Whats going on? It doesn't feel that bad?'

Standing poise for another strike, she decided she would need to test. Despite striking with a lot of power, she had still held out just a bit. But not this time...

krr-cha!

Lin Mei let out a yelp "Ah!"

A strike landed into the unshakable wall and her hand that made contact with the wall recoiled in despair, unable to leave a solid mark on the stone surface. But the same could not be said about the fist itself.

The hand dangled lamely from the wrist, unable to muster much strength of any kind. This was not the result she had been expecting at all, and hoped it wasn't completely broken but she knew it was atleast fractured. She didn't know what color her skin was but she was certain it had turned a bright red and would darken soon. Her lack of sight did not help but make her more accutely aware of the pain that had begun coursing through her hand.

Holding it close to her body, she shook but did not speak further. If she complained now, then she would complain later and she would not let that happen. 

Through eyes clenched shut and a rapidly beating heart, her mind spoke a small mantra, 'I resolved myself, I must face this pain so I can to improve. I will not compain, I will never yell.'

Several hours passed and she felt just like the rock that was sputtering out water, cold and soaked. As she mellowed in her own reflection of thoughts, the pain had subsided greatly. She had been careful to not move her hand carelessly after that strike and only now did she let go of her hand.

"Wha, how is it like this?"

Giving her hand a small flex, her hand felt almost okay. Giving her wrist a shake and pull only confirmed something.

'I definitely broke something in my wrist just then, but now it doesn't feel as if I did anything but a strain. Theres' something in this cave that is aiding my recovery.'

She wish she could see, there was likely something present in the cave that is quickly recovering her physical body. Things such as injuries and even hunger.

'Could it be the water? Was it special?' She did not know for sure but she made a mental note. She would make sure she kept on drinking it. There was no point in not. As long as she could train she was satisfied.

She got up and walked until she hit something that could not be moved. The rock wall was undeniably solid but she only smirked. She had a goal.

"This, this is such a dumb method but. I've heard that repeated strikes can strengthen the body. I have nothing but time, I won't leave until you break!"

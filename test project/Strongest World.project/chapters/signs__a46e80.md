"Not just the disiples, but even the first elder He Shen is unable to aproach this void crack?" Jin Guangli could not believe his ears. "He's close to the peak of the Acension realm, yet he's unable to enter?"

They had been dealing with this new void crack for a while now. This sudden news was most unwelcome.

Lei biya nodded. She began mulling over the reports she had so far, "They cannot. I just finished treating the first elder before reporting directly to you. The disciples do not remember aproaching the void crack and some parts of the first elder memories are out of order. Although, he does remember stepping foot into the light of the corridor before falling into a daze and fainting. He does not know how he escaped. Besides the loss of memory, there doesn't seem to be any lasting damage."

Jin Guangli and Jin Meixiu exchanged glances. A week ago, they had felt the presence of what seemed like a supreme expert wash over their city, now a troublesome void crack appeared just outside their city's domain. They didn't like how things were progressing.

On its own, this was not so bad, theres many void cracks like it litering the desert sands. The cave worlds in particular. They eventually stabalize, merging its interior space into the larger world. Fixing them into place before they did this could provide a monopoly of rare resources, but they could also spell trouble-just like this one.

"Dear, it seems we will have to go check this one out ourselves. On its own, its not so bad but if we leave this be, I'm afraid once whatever world this is descends into our vicinity, we will only be able to watch as our domain turn into a fire-or rather a sleepy silence."

Jin meixiu chuckled at his analogy. 

Standing up, Guangli looked at Lei Biya, "I'm not one to wait, We will deal with this now."

Calling out for someone to take a message to the other heads, he alerted them for some task they would need to do while they were gone. Once that was taken care of, his voice fell once more, "Alright, lead the way"

Lei biya nodded, "As you wish."

As they walked out the main compound, they each felt their feet grasp the air as they took to the skies quickly. From below, people could see them stepping above their heads and bowed out of respect. Normally, one would not sky walk within the city as it was looked down upon, but no one would stop the clan heads as they made their way to the outskirts of the sprawling city. 

After exiting, it would not take long for them to reach their destination. For experts such as themselves, they would not need long to get there as they sped up their travels.

Dozens of miles were crossed every instant. After some time, A stone forest appeared in front of them. Stalactites dozens of meters tall poked up from deep underneath the ground. Their canopies spreading numerous stone branches into the sky. Here, a special living stone could be harvested that was several times denser than normal desert stone. This is what many of the buildings were repaired with when the construction of the city went underway.

The group spied the sparse forest, landing just outside of it.

As they set foot underneath the start of the forest, immediately, everyone had the same thought, 'This place feels extremely strange.'

Their senses seemed to be dulled amidst the surroundings, unable to distinguish the minute details of their surroundings.

Jin Meixiu took a single step forward and in response, red scales begin emerging on her body, forcing her to stop.

"It seems not even my body wants me to step foot into this place. This-What do you want to do about it? I feel this could be much more dangerous than we thought."

Seeing their inaction, Lei Biya decided to take a few steps past Jin Meixiu. Lei Biya was a bit confused as to why the matriarch had stopped, "Although I do feel this place is a bit ominous, I don't feel anything in particular. The disciples and elder made it past this point. Why not continue further before coming to any specific decision?"

Jin Guangli also took a single step, suddenly his gaze sharpened as his natal artifact appeared in his hands, almost by instinct. like his wife, He could directly feel the enmity from the stone forest. It seemed as if his mortal enemy was lying just beyond the next tree, waiting for him to make the mistake of coming too close.

Jin Guangli snorted, "Bah! Mere paranoia! It seems there is something playing mind games with the spirit, not wanting us to come too close." 

Despite forest dapening his spirit, his senses sharpened as his gaze peered amid their surroundings. 

"It seems the feeling is stronger the higher your cultivation is. Its likely whatever this is doesn't want us to come too close."

Stalled, they began inspecting what they could see. Jin Meixiu blood red eyes eyed the forest keenly, not a single dust of sand or rock could escape her view.

After a period of time, she broke the silence, "It really is just a form of mental supression. We should continue."

A period of time ellapsed but as the trio moved further in, the paranoia of their surroundings only grew. The supression on their minds caused them to be deeply focused during the entire trip, and their supressed spirit meant that they had to observe with their eyes the things they couldn't see.

It had been a long time since they felt this way.

In front of them, a two meter crack floated in space. A pale white dust seemingly spread from the crack into its surroundings. The land nearby was breathtakingly pale and sparkly. Gradually at the edges of the void crack, splinters were forming, indicating that it was becoming bigger quickly. 

Jin Meixiu took a glance at it, "Although ominous, it doesn't appear the space surrounding it is dangerous. Due to the fracture, it seems to have formed a natural formation, restricting the use of spiritual senses."

As she was more knowledgable in this subject, Jin meixiu gave out some more of her thoughts, "I'm not too sure about what is inside, but from the aura that is leaking out, it should be a treasure that deals with spritual awareness. Since it can interfere with even a Wordly King's spiritual senses, it is probably a sky tier treasure. Even for us,Going inside would be very dangerous if we can't resist the effects properly."

Jin Guangli eyes sharped and a frown came over his face. If they were a Spirit King, he would not have much to consider, his soul would be protected by his undying spirit, allowing him to battle the effects of the unnease and paranoia caused by the natural formation.

They all wore heavy looks. This was not something they wanted to deal with.

"Should we make this a forbidden area then? I don't see an easy way of supressing this. Maybe... Elder Gong can seal the entire area away?" Lei Biya shook her head as she spoke, she didn't have many ideas besides this. The last thing she wanted was to try and aproach this void crack. She thought He Shen was a fool for jumping into a space that restricts the use of the spirit so half hazardly.

Jin Meixiu refuted immediately, "This treasure can affect us even while seperated by a crack in space, although a seal would hold it temporarily, the barrier would eventually fail once the space stabilized. When that happens, every faction around would begin scrambling once they sense the aura of a sky treasure. These lands are too new and divided. We would be pressed having to fight everyone."

"Then we must head in." Jin Guangli didn't think about it any further. There was no point in doing so, not when they were being forced like this. With his weapon in hand, he walked directly towards the void crack. He was a sword immortal, if he could not resolve it with a single strike, then he would use two. If two did not work, then he would use three. His eyes opened and aura primed for anything that may come his way.

His wife spoke no further and followed behind his lead.

Lei Biya knew her limits, wishing them a safe journey she stayed at the entrance. They walked until the light coming from the space covered them both.

One after the other, they vanished past the horizon.

The moment Jin Meixiu's feet landed into the new space, she felt the world shift, landing hard against the ground.

Bam!

She felt her side burning from pain and found it very difficult to breath. Blood was pooling underneath her as she gasped for air. Each breath brought searing heat with it and her red scales that healed and protected her, laid fractured across her body, receeding back underneath her skin.

"wha-what is-"

"Just this much and your already broken. This generation is too weak! Too Fragile! Whats a little pain when your life is on the line? If you want to become stronger than this, this much should be tolerable."

'Father!'

'That was fathers' voice!'

She grabbed her side and struggled upwards, she heard groaning come from her side. It was her younger brother, also beaten and bloody, not moving from his lack of conscious. Panic began setting in as a feeling of sadness permeated throughout her. She needed to get up and divert their fathers attention, les' he resumes his harsh lessons on him.

Meixiu spat through gritted teeth, "I-I'm not finished yet, I can still fight."

'Get up!'

Pushing up with what remained of her strength, mustering up the scraps of energy that was left in her core. 

'Get up Now! I can't fall here, not until I prove myself for the day. If I don't father won't stop until both of us are completely broken!'

The burning sensation on her side began to be soothed as if a cool breeze had been blown over it. The red scales that had hidden themselves had begun forming, stabalizing her breadth and emotions.

Standing to face her father head on, she rushed back into the fight. Her first landed amongst the body of the imposing figure in front of her.

pa-pa-pa!

Strikes resounded crisply through the air, Meixiu had been stricken several times. Falling back to the ground, before standing back up, grunting through the pain, repeatedly rushing back. She had no plan to stop here. 

Eyes the color of bad blood looked at Meixiu with a bit of interests, "hmm? You're moving better than before, it seems you've finally gained some tenacity."

The patriarch had seen Meixiu collapse to the ground repeated over these last few hours. Yet this was the fastest she had stood up so far.

He could feel meixiu's power rising, the bottom of the awakening stage to the peak before surpassing even that. Her power was rising quickly.

Such a thing, His interests was peaked, "What is this?"

Meixiu began gathering her strength, her strength was rising too fast, she had begun to feel that something was wrong.

'my strength is returning-this doesn't make sense!'

She was struggling mentally, torn between familiarity and vague foreigness, but as she thought about it, she felt that this was natural, as if in her mind, this was the way the world should have been.

But she knew this was wrong.

The patriarch only watched, his smile growing bigger and bigger. Seeing her power cross pass the manifestation realm and into ascension realm with no signs of stopping, he couldn't control himself any longer.

"Finally! It has finally happened! Ha-ha, with this, I want to see who would dare say my blood cannot vie for the seat of the next clan head!"

As her power rose, Meixiu mind began clearing up. Looking at her father, the previous emotions had since faded, replaced by calm and serenity. Her strength continued to rise as she focused on this feeling until it placated around the mid-level of the Wordly King realm, her strength had been fully restored along with her mind.

Looking at her father with clear eyes, all her injuries vanished as her strength had come back.

Meixiu looked around with meaning, the redness of her eyes deepend as she observed.

kir-kir-kirr~

"Break!"

bang!

The world around her began to collapse. The sky, the broken training ground and even her brother began fading into a white obscurity. The landscape around her became discolored, loosing its vibrant colors before fading into an obscure speckled whiteness.

"This is an illusion, but the last few hours felt too real. If I hadn't realized that something was wrong, I may have been lost in that world forev-'

"An illusion? What are you murmuring about. It seems you've had some kind of fortuitous encounter, look at you. Your strength now is not too shaby."

A voice split the silence, Jin Meixiu looked surprised. Her father was somehow still here after being dispelled.

'This is an illusion right?' turning to face her father, Jin Meixiu wasn't so sure anymore. She should have freed herself after breaking the illusion and her instincts did not react in the slightest. Yet, feeling the atmosphere, it still felt dream-like. Looking at the patriarch, he seemed quite a bit older than previously. He stood slightly titled, eyes hollowed and a face burned with age.

'This...has he always looked so weary? His long beard has turned grey-white and his speech doesn't hold the same power as it used to.'

An old skinny hand reached out, "You've grasped the Plane of Illusions technique. I see,very good. This space isn't so simple, so this would be the only way you could break free so early. Come closer, let me have a better look at how you've been."

She could not explain what was happening, The world should have returned to normal after being dispelled yet her father was still here. His appearance was slightly different but she could recognize him right away. Something was wrong here.

Slightly sweating, Meixiu had some thoughts, 'A sky treasures spiritual attacks are too strong! Not even breaking the technique can free ones' mind. Do i, fight?'

Looking at her father being so at eased, she felt fear invade her body, 'can I win?'

With a wave of an old bony hand, the discolored world began changing. The sky that had initially began to fade away had regained its vitality while the ground rushed back into existence. The scenary had changed, instead of a training field, it was the peak of a mountain. Looking down the edges would reveal nothing but an intense vertical slope that plunged into clouds.

Her doubts only intensified as she observed this act. "Thats my technique, plane of illusions! Father, you knew it too? But thats..."


He shook his head, "Whether or not I know it, its easy to replicate when you only understand it at this level."

Gearing up to fight once more, Meixiu had set her resolve. If she couldn't break the illusion with her technique, then she would have to beat the illusion itself. Regardless, the power inside the illusion could not be too overbearing. The treasure would certainly have its limits.

But to her surprise Her father had no such resolve.

"There's no need to fight, you've already won. Seeing your progress, I wished to help you along your path."

"I've already won? What do you mean?"

'Was this some new tactic?' Meixiu didn't understand his words.

"Come, sit. While you're here, you should ponder about this. Here, your comprehension is the highest, it would be ashame to awaken so quickly."

Walking carefully to the area he pointed out, Meixiu followed his words. She didn't know what he was trying to do, but seeing as they won't be fighting, it wouldn't hurt to keep it that way for now.

"As demons, we gain inspiration from within, awaken memories of our forefathers and advance the technique. However, there is nothing stopping this process from skipping a few steps. Little Hai demonstrated this, awakening directly to a treasure body. I'm truly proud of that boy, its his fortune to be that gifted. But for you, you'll have to start with the patterned skin."

Twitching at his words, she felt it odd that he knew about her son in such detail. As her father kept talking, she began to feel a subtle feeling arise. Sinking further into the feeling, past experiences began to emerge. There was several battles she had lost and she didn't understand how. The mist around those fights began to unveil themselves and her deficiences were pointed out.

As her father talked more and more, she felt the shackles on her cultivation loosen gradually and layers on her core began to build up. It was a soft feeling that permeated her body, slowly promoting her stagnate realm towards the high level of Wordly King. 

"The spirit king Realm is very mysterious. This is the beginning of eternal life, or its often considered to be so. In the wordly king realm, your body becomes eternal, unable to corode or rust. In the spirit king realm, your spirit becomes eternal, neither wanning with time or dimishing in capacity. Some say without refining the spirit, even if you have the vitality, you would be unable to live for thousands of years without loosing your sanity."

Theses words were all things she had heard before and as each topic was spoken, at some point she found it difficult to hear the words correctly. No matter how hard she tried to listen. She could not open her eyes or keep her senses primed fully. She began to struggle but her body was relaxing gradually, as if a deep sleep had began to take over. The space around her darkened as she became more and more relaxed.

At some point she gave up, the feeling was too peaceful.

Startled awake, Jin Meixiu opened her eyes. She was met with precious gemstones that littered a peculiar speckled white rock cave. The ceiling, floor and walls were all a stunning marble with streaks of minerals flowing over the walls.

"What an odd occurance. Who knew I would have awakened a blood memory here of all places. I've never had an experience like this one before. If I could have stayed in there a bit longer, I felt like I could have grasped something spectacular before awakening. This treasure must have made this space special, it seems It has the capacity to induce comprehension. What a strange ability."

Her eyes panned to a singular book in front of her. It was made out of the same white stone that permeated the cave. The pages were constantly flapping and there seemed to be writing on it, but her senses were unable to reach the stone paper. In fact, her senses were restrained heavily indie the realm, unable to cover anything her eyes could not see. Her spirit was completely useless.

"It seems the reason why they collapsed before entering was that they became overwhelmed. If you fail to break free, then you will suffer. Fortunately my skills are somewhat similar. Allowing me to break through quickly. But just what happened in my mind?"

The more she thought about it, the more odd it became. Her dream seemed as if it started with a nightmare, the illusion of this world. But after she broke free, she was launched into a blood memory. There, she learned more about herself, advancing her cultivation an entire minor realm. Is that the reward for breaking free?

As her eyes wandered around the cave, they came accross Guangli. His eyes were wide open but dulled greatly. He was trembling intensely with his sword in hand. A slight wind was pacing periodically around him, filled with a certain aura of battle. It seemed as if he was in the greatest fight in his life, unable to take a break for even a second.

Looking at him, she decide to leave him alone. This was a once in a lifetime opportunity for them, as it could directly help them comprehend apart of their path. If she forcefully awakened him, while he would likely forgive her, she would feel eternally guilty.

This realm was quite small, with nothing but her, her husband, and the book. Setting her gaze once more on the floating white-stone book, she began advancing towards it.

Immediately, she felt pressure with each step. With each step she took, she felt the weight on her mind increase until it became almost unbearable. The red scales that materialized on her body began glowing incessantly on her as she stopped just a few meters short of the floating book.

"I can't sit here for long, the pressure is too great. I'll have to confine the treasure and leave in the shortest possible time."

With this in mind, she began reaching towards the book. Her outstretched hand was consumed by power as her energy flared.

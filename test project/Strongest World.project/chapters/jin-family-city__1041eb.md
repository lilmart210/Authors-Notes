"Woah, there's so many different people, what are they doing on the side of the road?" Jin Hai was big eyed, staring at all the sights along the road inside the desert stone city. There were thousands of people, just in his sights, he could hardly see anything else.

Yu Lin followed Jin Hai's gaze, "The people returning with treasures and goods, normally sell them here. The edge of the city is the best place to catch things as they come in. Often times, these are workers for large shops, each competing for the best prices."

Jin Hai looked but didn't understand. There were many shops, some with sparkling jewelry, and others with specialty items. The many choices were overwhelming. Outside of the things his parents showed him on occasion, he had never seen so much stuff before. 

A smell wafted over diverting his attention, "Something smells, sweet..." Taking a few deep breaths, Jin Hai began walking further into the city. He stoped outside a small sandstone shop with a line formed at the door. Eying a particular sign, the words 'Sand Crusted Dragon Sticks' stood out to him.

Several sticks of golden, crisp bread with sand like dust sat behind a glass display window. Demons did not need to eat or sleep but Jin Hai felt a longing for the savory smelling snack.

Jin Hai raced to the entrance, but as he was about to open the door, a shout came from behind him.

"hey! you can't cut the line, thats not fair, I'm next!" A boy no older than Jin Hai was pointing at him and Yu Lin.

Yu Lin payed it no attention, pushing to door further for the eager boy, however Jin Hai stopped.

"waiting in line?" Jin Hai's dark red eyes pivoted and a peer of his filled his sights. He had grey eyes, grey hair, was well-toned and slightly taller than he was, "Why would you be waiting in line? Theres hardly anyone inside."

As the boy was about to respond, a man behind him abruptly covered his mouth, "Excuse my son, naturally, brother Lin and his company, do not need to wait in line. Its only to keep us folks organized."

Eyes darted between Yu Lin and Jin Hai, the boy's father had wide eyes. He did not know who the boy was but everyone recognized Yu Lin, he was not strong but he was a subordinate of the Jin family's matriarch, Jin Meixiu. Not anyone could become a subordinate of the Jin Family. Currently, they only recruited the best talent. Seeing as Yu Lin was respectfully following the boy, his status was obviously not ordinary.

Yu Lin's bearing was regal as he held a hand up, "Then I must express my thanks, since I'm accompanying the young master, I won't be polite. It seems he has taken a liking to this bakery."

Eyes darted between Yu Lin and Jin Hai, his son struggled for a few seconds before placcating.

'Young master? He's the young master?' 

Curious eyes began to observe the duo. Some demons that were walking past overheard and began looking on carefully. All of them knew about the Jin family, they were the powerhouse of this desert, fighting off the competition to secure this place for themselves.

Clamor eruted around them, their could only be one young master in Jin family city. some had speculated long ago that couple must have a successor but as to who that may be, no-one had seen him or knew who he was.

The boy was no longer rebelling, but he also had no intentions of yielding further, "Humph! I don't see what makes him so special. So what if your parents are strong? without personal strength, it means nothing."

The boy's father felt his heart stop, he had not known what had gotten into his son today, but he felt years of his life leave his body.

Pa!

A smack rang across the streets, the boy felt his cheek burning red as his father withdrew his hand.

"It cost nothing for you to be quiet!" The father reprimand his son, his mood was visibly sour. The boy felt wronged but said nothing.

Seeing the crowd grow bigger, Yu Lin quickly urged Jin Hai forward, and as they headed into the bakery, Jin Hai had many questions.

'Do I not look strong? Why did he dislike me so much?'

He was most pressed about these few questions. He wanted to be an impressive figure, not ride the fame of his parents or be hated by everyone around him. He wanted to be a proud sword immortal, like his dad.

His thoughts was interrupted when the aroma of freshly baked sweets hit his nose. Putting aside his thoughts, he began taking a look around. There was more than just sand crusted bread on display and he could hardly believe just how many different kinds of snacks was inside.

"Welcome to the sand crusted bakery, we have the best softly baked bread in the lands."

A lady in the prime of her life was serving as the receptionist behind the counter. Further past her, the kitchen was clearly visible. An old man was busy striking bread prepared on the counter.

Yu Lin had not visited this store often, but he suddenly understood why there were so many young men outside. Some of them must be here to try their luck.

"Young master, you should pick what you like the most. I've had their sand cakes, its quite good."

Jin Hai eyes gazed across the variety of baked goods. There was a 'pa-ta-pa-ta' sound as his gaze wandered towards the back. The only man was striking bread repeatedly. Every so often he would stop and change his technique, from wide palm strikes to poking and folding.

"I want that one."

Although there were plenty to marvel at, Jin Hai gaze had never wandered from his original objective. He knew what he wanted.

As Jin Hai walked out with golden sticks of bread, a voice from the line called out, "I'm called Yao Kang! You best remember me!"

Jin Hai gave a side glance but only caught the boy's rear end heading into the store. He no longer paid him any attention. Taking a bite out of the sweet bread made him forget about everything around him.

"So good~"

The sweetness overwhelmed him, he had never had something so good before. Jin Hai had never seen his mother eat outside of what his father cooked occasionally. However his father did not care for anything specific, often times lunch consisted of whatever they found growing along the ways. His eyes looked over the other few sticks in his hand and he felt like he did not grab enough. His greed took over as he ravenously at the rest.

He made a mental note of this location, he knew he would be back to grab some more.

As the pair walked through the city, Jin Hai's big red eyes glanced all around as the plethora of shops slowly turned into housing districts, "Are there many shops that sell food like that one?"

Yu Lin Shook his head, "Most stores in the city focus on artifact refining and trading. Very few make things that can be eaten for pleasure. Most are bland and function as medicine. What use would such sweets have anyways?"

Jin Hai had a dejected look. Yu Lin was slightly frantic as he added on, "Young master, there are definitely more than enough places in the city to go around and visit. In the future, there will be even more. Once you're a bit older, I'll introduce you to some better locations, how about it?"

"Really?" Jin Hai felt his mood improve but he thought about the previous snack he had eaten, "No, just bring me back to the bakery, I'll definitely eat everything else that the shop had!"

"Haha, then I'll take you back there then."

The two chatted while they walked. As they walked closer to the center of the city, the center-most building became ever so clearer. Once they had aproached the steps of the pavillion gates.

"If it isn't brother Lin! Its been quite a long time since I had seen you. I had started to think you had forgotten about us!"

A man dressed in full uniform stood outside the gates. He was amidst several other guards, each wearing red robes, the signature clothing of Jin family warriors.

"ha-ha brother Feng, waiting for such a long time, It seems I owe you a drink. I'm sure everyone here wouldn't mind a small outing."

"A friend of captain, naturally we can't be polite."

"Brother Lin is very generous!"

Nearby guards joined in on the cheer. Yu Lin knew Fu Feng, he was one of his peers when they were reconstructing Jin family city. Fu Feng also became one of the first people to have joined the Jin Family guards. Seeing as he was in charge of a small group of people he did not recognize, it seems Fu Feng must have become a captain. 

Only Awakened demons could become captains. He must have advanced sometime while he was gone. This was a connection he could not let go of.

Amidst his musings, Fu Feng asked directly, "Brother Lin, What can I help you with today?"

Pulling out a red token, Yu Lin spoke freely, "I'm here to meet with the family heads. The young master has excited seclusion."

'The young master?'

Eyes began turning to the boy that had been quiet the entire time. They were greeted with big red eyes that looked at them carefully. Some of the guards found that they couldn't see through him.

What did that mean? It meant that he was actually stronger than they were.

'This boy is too fierce!'

Fu Feng analyzed the token, he did not need to since he already knew Yu Lin had it, but he did it to keep up appearances as a city guard.

As his eyes inspected the token, Fu Feng replied, "This is a bit tricky. The clan heads are currently in a meeting with the family elders. I'm not sure what they are discussing about but I was told to refrain from interrupting them."

Fu Feng glanced at the boy standing next to Yu Lin, "Although its different when it comes to the young master. They are currently in the Main hall, wait around in the surrounding garden. I'm sure they will be done soon enough."

Yu Lin And Jin Hai made their way without any further troubles. Inside, the clearing was massive. Desert Stone path ways crossed many times, accentuating its beauty to the beholder.

After walking in, Jin hai stopped, "There's so many flowers here, how is this possible? How could there be such pretty plants growing in this hot sand? I have not seen these growing anywhere earlier."

Since the trees and grasses turned into sand, he had not seen a single flower. But now there was an entire garden in front of him. Dozens of servants were carefully tending to the plants in the ground.

"haha, quite the sight isn't it?" Yu Lin had a big smile on his face. Being able to show the young master around has been somewhat pleasarable.

"The main compound has dozens of homes inside of it. Further along this path is the main hall. This area is the flower garden, although it may not look like it, these plants require lots of energy. They are just some of the ingrediants used in pill making. The esteemed sixth elder Lei is the Jin families master pill refiner. She controls all the gardens in here, most contain precious plants and herbs they need for refinement."

Jin Hai walked close to one and took a deep breadth, demonic energy flowed into his body from the air. He could feel his spirit uplifting from a single breath. This was different from the natural energy present in the air. It was not as refined as the energy in his core, but it was easy to gather. It melded with his body seemlessly.

Before he could fully savor the feeling a shout rang out.

"Hey, you are ruining the garden! Don't you know you cannot recklessly absorb the plants spirit!?"

A lady followed by several people who were carrying boxes appeared amidst the landscape.

'I'm ruining the garden?'

Taking a closer look at the flowers in front of him, Jin Hai noticed they were starting to wilt and the leaves began to brown. He stood up but as he was about to speak, Yu Lin interjected.

"So what if these little flowers die? Can the young master not enjoy his own garden? I want to see who wants to stop him."

"The young master?"

Eyes began eying the duo curiously, before focusing on the boy amidst the flowers. No one had seen the young master before and yet he was suddenly here? Such a thing, Who would believe it so easily?

But seeing was believing. Dark red eyes locked eyes with theirs and the lady leading the group swallowed any further criticism. There was only one person, no two now, who had that kind of gaze.

"Even still, as the maintainer of this garden, Elder Lei won't let me off if I let these flowers wilt. The spirit arrays in the training rooms would be a much better fit than these plants. How about using those instead?"

Although his appearance was calm, Jin Hai mind was slightly panicked,

'Is it really that bad? I shouldn't cause too much trouble, what kind of young master would I be if I bothered everyone?' 

Jin hai stood up, it seemed the lady was at odds with Yu Lin because of him. His red eyes began taking in everything about the group. They were wearing the Jin family attire but there was a pot imbued onto the garments. The lady infront had two streams of smoke coming from the pot while the others only had one. 

They seemed to all be around Yu Lin in strength, however he could not grasp the strength of the lady in front of him.

'This feeling of vagueness reminds me of the guard outside, it seems they both must be a lot stronger than I am.'

The scents of herbs eminated from her clothing, Jin Hai took note of it, the smell was fragrent but pleasant.

'If she has Such strength, why would she be playing with pots and grass? Wouldn't it be better to fight in battles?'

He wondered but didn't voice it, "I didn't know that these were so valuable. The energy was very soothing that I hadn't realized I was affecting the garden. I'm sorry about this big sis."

The lady had a slight smile, "There's no need to apologize, this is your garden after all."

Yu Lin snorted, "Of course he can do what he wants."

The lady found Jin Hai to be pleasing to the ears and decided to outright ignore Yu Lin. Clasping her hands togethor she took a small bow. The others behind her followed suite.

"Although late, let me introduce myself. I'm Song, a core discple of the sixth elder. The people behind me are the newer family disciples I am in charge of."

Jin Hai's eyes visited each person carefully. He had thought of what it meant to be the young master before, but seeing the look in their eyes made him think it over again. The city, the people, everything was new to him. The change was too fast from his previous lifestyle and now he had no idea of how he should act.

He decided he should learn more before meeting his parents. So far he has been dragging Yu Lin around with him, He had not actually spent much time thinking about anything else.

"A core disciple? Whats that? I've only just arrived, so there is much I don't know. Can you explain this some more?"

Song smiled sweetly in response, "Of course, how about a stroll through the gardens as we speak?"

Jin Hai nodded his head. Togethor the small group began wandering the paths. Yu Lin followed closely behind the talking pair. Occasionally speaking a few words in between their conversation.

Song didn't want to admit it, but Yu Lin knew a lot more than he let on. His thuggish attitude when they first met annoyed her but he was more intelligent than he showed.

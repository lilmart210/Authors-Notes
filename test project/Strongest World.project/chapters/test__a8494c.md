"Father, We're going to fight here?"

Jin Hai looked across the gardens. He had strode past here earlier, the densly packed foilage was all around him. Their surroundings was backed with a few dozen people. Some important figures had heard of the young master returning and rushed to meet him.

Even the elders were sligthly puzzled, were they really going to fight here?

This...Didn't seem apropriate.

Jin Meixiu had caught up to the crowd and stepped out, her downcast face from before was gone but her smile had still not returned. 

She looked at Jin Guangli and asked, "Do you have a specific place in mind?"

"Hmm..." Jin Guangli gave his beard a few strokes.

He thought for a few more seconds before a suitable place came to mind, "It has to be there, the clearing were we used to train. Take us there."

Her red eyes darkened in response. Crystaline glass hands raised and pointed at the duo. Mist began appearing in the air around Jin Guangli and Jin hai. In just a few seconds the mist had grown thick enough that nothing could be seen out of it. 

After a few more seconds, the mist gradually dissapeared. 

Jin Hai recognized this place immediately, "This...is the place were we used to train? But didn't it turn into a desert! Why are there trees? And where is everyone else?"

He thought about it but couldn't come up with a reasonable answer.

Jin Guangli smile had grown even bigger, "Its an incredible illusion isn't it? No formation disk or array in sight. Because humans cultivate against nature, we don't awaken variant abilities. I can't help but be amazed everytime I see it."

His eyes landed on his son, "I wonder what yours is like?"

vhoom!

An intense feeling of dread landed over the former field. It caused Jin Hai to shudder and his throat to seize. He struggled to even take a deep breath.

He dropped to his knees as his hands caught the ground before his face could hit it.

'What is this feeling? Is this dad's real presence? I can't, I can't fall here!'

Jin Hai closed his eyes as he relaxed his nerves. Finally swalling down a mouthful, he began circling his energy according to his revised Blue Mountain scripture. His energy swirled slowly at first, but accelerated rapidly.

Although interested in the technique his mother used, he had no time to think about it. He had a bigger threat at the moment, his father. 

Once fully relaxed, his eyes began to open as he stood up. Tucked away in the corner of both his eyes, a second bloody pupil gradually emerged. They traveled across his eyes until it bumped into the iris, merging to form an eye with two deep pits of red.

Red swords began forming in his surroundings as scales began appearing on his body.

Jin Guangli noticed the changes in Jin Hai's body. His energy was circulating in a manor different from what he had taught him.

'The blue aura of the sword scripture is nowhere to be seen. During his retreat it seems he completely evolved this human technique, turning it into a demonic technique.'

"Interesting! Lets see what you can do!"

Jin Hai felt his blood boil at these words, he wasted no more time and began the assault first.

Outside of the illusionary world, the surrounding demons had burst into discussion.

"Haha, the patriarch is fierce but so is the son. What technique is that, anyone know?"

Another demon chimed in, "Its a human technique, who knew they had something like this up there sleeves? It seems I've been wrong about them this entire time."

"Although he is supressing his energy, you can see how skilled the patriarch is, look at how he's carefully treading those strikes! There isn't a single wasted movement-not even his clothes are flapping unnecesarily."

There was alot of clamour now that the battle had started. From When the mist had formed, it started to fade away and what replaced it was a mirage. Inside the mirage was a clearing in a forest, where the Patriarch and young master had reappered. 

Not many knew about the matriarchs abilities in full, but seeing this made them reavaluate her strength. Such a feat was not minor, atleast not without some kind of artifact or treasure.

As the fight grew more intense, Jin meixiu's eyes saw less and less. After seeing Jin Hai's transformation, she had grown restless. Time seemed to go in reverse as the fight grew on. The longer she watched, the more memories of her past would rush through her mind. It filled her with unnease and dread, she couldn't help but frown at these thoughts.

Elder Lei was by her side, noticing her state had not improved from earlier. She did not speak on what plagued her but Lei Biya figured it must be with the young master's future.

"Miss Jin, it seems your worried about the young master. Is he not splendid? I don't think there is anything you need to worry about. If nothing else, you must believe that I'll support you with raising him properly."

Jin Meixiu's complexion improved somewhat, it was nice having people to support you around, but looking at Jin Hai's conjoined pupils, the words "Treasure" echoed in her mind endlessly.

The voices in her head were loud and she could not keep those feelings away. She, did not want to see Jin Hai continue to improve.

"The Patriarch did not take it easy on you, young master. Your body is showing signs of fatigue from being pushed too far. Even as a variant with the ability to regenerate, you must be aware of your limits or you will risk your body failing when you need it most."

A sweet and caring voice flowed into the ears of Jin Hai. At the moment, He was resting inside a large basin, His body bruised and tender from his long fight with his father. Beside him, Several disciples of the family were around, constantly mixing precious herbs and rocks into the water.

Jin Hai was all smiles as he responded, "Oh, I will keep that in mind auntie."

But he was distracted with something else. He was watching as all the ingrediants disolved into the water.

'How could all of this disolve into the water without a trace? Even metal and rocks seem to vanish in the water, its so odd.'

The lady was patient, seemingly used to such behavior. Jin hai's head was sinking lower into the water, trying to see exactly when the ingrediants disolved into its pieces. As the materials was lowered into the water, the disciples around him made several hand signs as there arms glowed. Following which, the minerals in the water began shrinking before vanishing completely in the water.

Jin Hai let out a yelp, 'Ouch! Ah-I will stop, I will stop!'

A hand was pulling his ear, forcing him mostly out of the water. A sweet voice once again resonated in his ear, "Young master, you must focus on absorbing the energy otherwise all of our efforts will go to waste."

Sinking back into the water, Jin Hai wanted to continue watching but knew he could not. Aunt Liu words were soft but they could not be ignored. Don't look at the fact that she was watching after the clan's young master. Liu Guan's power was not inferior to the sixth Elder Lei and Jin Hai had personally experienced her devilish hands several times throughout his bath already.

Liu Guan was once apart of a wandering group of physicians, but seeing as the sixth elder, Lei Biya, already occupied the position of family pill refiner, Liu Guan opted to not care about it. Liu Guan was not one to fight over meaningless titles. Instead, she deflected to a minor position, becoming the young master's caretaker.

As Jin Hai settled back into the large stone basin, eyes began settling on his body. Liu Guan looked carefully at the boy, curious about his methods.

Blood red scales began emerging along Jin Hai's skin, covering all the sensitive areas of his body in a sharp protective covering. The scales resembled crystal formations and was slightly transparent. As he circled his energy according to his revised scripture, he felt the energy in the water be tugged in his direction.

Liu Guan watched intently as several streams of energy made its way into the boy's body and thought to herself, 'The body tempering solution is very good at restoring the body's fatigue and energy, however there is one downside, there is too much energy and can be detrimental when consumed in a weakened state, such as after battle.'

Her eyes scanned the murky water eying the flow of energy in the tub. As a physician, her spiritual sense was trained on the body since young and could interperate things that others may not see. Jin Hai's inner core had begun forming new layers over itself and had entered a state of expansion. The energy flowed into his body, traveling to a single place as he taking in more and more energy wantonly.

Liu Guan nodded, 'It seems the young master is exceptional'

A hand submerged itself into the water while a voice echoed to the disciples around the tub, "Put a piece of folded iron and some hundred year herbs in the water. hmm, oh and throw a Peak flood demon core in here as well"

The small group of disciples answered in unison, "Yes Master."

Materials were dropped into the water one by one, Liu Guan hands began moving and making signs under the water and the materials began shining. The water began to form undercurrents the longer this went on. This was a form of the Medicinal Hand technique and when mastered, could turn anything into the most desirable of panaceas. The water was immediately enriched in spirit energy. The swollen channels in Jin Hai's body began moving even more energy throughout his body. His demonic core was greedily absorbing all of it, strenghening itself after a long days worth of fighting.

Jin Hai sat like this for a few hours, unmoving. The others only sat around and watched. With Liu Guan being strong as an elder and the disciples being core disciples, they were all stronger than Jin Hai. In the flood demon stage, the body and demonic core needed to be flooded with energy to advance further. But the sheer volume of energy that Jin Hai could absorb freely was astonishing to everyone. 

This was much easier said than done as the rate of energy could not be too fast. The body could only store so much and the rest must be used to temper the body. Such a thing was not easy to do without a great amount of time, pain and effort. But here, Jin Hai was doing so freely, strenghening his bones and organs while converting the rest into his energy. Such control was unpreccedented, some of the disciples here had doubts if they could do it like this.

A disciple murmurred, "This is a flood demon? Is their family all like this?"

Liu Guan stared menacingly towards the disciple who spoke. Words soundlessly made its way into his ears. The disciple froze up for a while before hanging his head low. 

They were here specifically to help the young master and gain experience while doing so, nothing else. Liu Guan was strict, things such as question and talking were definitely not allowed. Especially, not during a time of meditation. 

"Ah!" Jin Hai stood up abruptly and yelled, "I forgot, I forgot!"

Liu Guan was puzzled, "You forgot? is there something you need to do, young master?"

Jin Hai was completely outside the water, with only his scaled appearance protecting him from everyone's eyes. He scanned the surroundings for his spatial ring that Yu Lin had given him. "Mhmm! I need to bring something to father and mother!"

He vaulted out of the basin, grabbed his clothes skipping by everyone before running out.

"Do you not want to wait before...you..." but Jin hai was already gone from the room. 

Letting out a sigh of remorse, Liu Guan could see just how rowdy the young master is. 

"oh, to be young again"

Liu Guan did not know what it was that was so urgent but seeing the enthusiastic boy, she let him go free.

looking at the basin, she could not help but make a remark, "What a waste although its mostly gone, the energy in the bucket is still substantial. Every bath should be taken with care as it will be slightly less effective after every use. You all will practice the Medical Hand extraction technique. I want to see a 60% purity rate on the extracted materials or higher, anything lower is failure."

Jin Hai rushed through several buildings as he reached the very center of the compound. By now the Celestial stars in the sky had all fallen and only several dim celestial bodies remained floating in the sky. Here, he could feel a slight burning effect begin to take root in his body, but he ignored it and pressed onwards. Demons saw him as he rushed past but payed it little attention as it was the young master running in his own house.

"Mother, Father, I have something!" Jin Hai yelled out enthusiastically as he felt two familiar auras appear in his senses. Slowing down, he calmed his emotions as he entered a courtyard drowning in flowers. They towered over him by many meters and were very peculiar. With a single, giant flower on a very thick stalk, Jin Hai had never seen something like that before. he prepared himself as he stepped foot into the courtyard but as he was about to enter the main room, he stopped.

Inside, voices could be easily heard. Through the cracks between doors, he could see a flame flickering as the only source of light inside. His father was making an expression he had never seen before. He was normally stoic and unbothers but, something about his facial tone and words seemed solemn and full of emotion. 

'Huh? What is this?'

Jin Hai hesitated, he was not sure if he should stay or leave.

'Such a city, who would have thought this desert would have this kind of thing.' Jin Meixiu was standing in the sky looking down at a Desert Stone City. There was a lot to think about here, as there were many kinds of peculiarities.

'Over the east, the main celestial star rises, but as the day wears on, the burning sensation that permeats the city begins to increase, forming another celestial body in the sky... there must be some kind of secret down there.' Jin Meixiu was sure of it. Now that the world was shifting, things she was once familiar with was changing rapidly. Before others arrived, she had the idea to monopolize as much as she could.

Jin Meixiu gaze fell onto the city below her as she started her descent. Her dark red eyes eyed every grain of sand that filled the city below her. 

"Eh? How come its like this?" Jin Meixiu audibly gasped. Landing amongst the buildings, fine architecture had withered away under the burning effect of the desert heat.

'Desert stone can't be burned or molded using the heat of the desert. It would only be strenghened in the presence of such heat. The fact that these buildings fell due to time is impossible...' 

Putting her hand on a nearby building, the rigidness of the buildings was felt immediately. Although it would be easier to climb onto the roof than find an operatable door in these ruins, the remaining structure was abnormally tuff. Her hand rested on a portion of the orange sand wall and gave a little push. Without cycling her energy, Her natural bodily strength was unable to shake the remaining foundations of the failing house. It wasn't until she gave a strong shove that a cracked formed in the wall. 

Jin Meixiu sunk into thought. After sometime, her red eyes flashed and her divine sense flooded the city.

Hundreds of miles was scanned in an instant, shock was written all over her face. Looking up at the fastly rising second sun, the burning sensation slowly began melding its way into her body. The effect was minor but she felt a small rigor invade her bones and muscles, nourishing the body in a small but noticeable amount.

Such a thing, who would build a city like this? Just by living here the residents would have their body washed of impurities and tempered like steel. Not anyone would have the resources to do something like this. As the sun rose, she also felt the density of the surrounding spirit increase by several folds. If all this energy was gathered in a central area, it could easily become a cultivation paradise which only the strongest demons could enjoy.

Her gaze turned towards the center of the city, despite being dozens of miles out, a pavillion centered there was easily seen from any corner of the land. A grin made its way onto her face, "There's a treasure here!"

Jin Meixiu's figure flashed, the distance between the two areas was covered in an instant. Reappearing in front of the pavilion, a courtyard untouched by time was present.

"Such Dense yang energy, there could be no mistake" A fiery aura flowed slowly from the inside into the surrounding city. Jin Meixiu's excitement was further stroked. 

Casually strolling into the interior, the furnishings of the building was exquisite and grand. Whatever person lived here was worshiped heavily as items of ceremony were everywhere. 

The pavilion had many rooms and several courtyards. This seemed to be a family compound. Whoever lived here was well protected by the surrounding city. 

"This is it." Inside a main hall, the yang energy was especially violent. Just by being in the room, Jin Meixiu clothes became slightly singed. The feeling of burning was more vivid here than anywhere else in the city, with the main hall being decorated with sand in many places where find decorations once stood. Jin Meixiu laid eyes on a Formation. 

"Naturally formed Golden Crow Celestial Fire, how rare. What a profit I've made this time!" In Jin Meixiu hands was a wisp of flame, no bigger than her finger but the fire was overwhelmingly powerful. Its color was just as deep a red as her eyes. A reddish energy flowed from her hands, surrounding the flame. The heat in the room was stifled immediately and the flame landed inside a jade box. 

Jin Meixiu body flashed and she reappeared outside the ruined city. The desert stone streets that was flooded with yang energy had decreased greatly but had yet to vanish completely.

"The second celestial body is still High in the sky, it seems that the fire wasn't formed on accident. If I am not wrong then the city was built here with the intention of being tempered by the heat. Occasionally, it might make these kinds of things."

This made sense. She began re-evaluating her plans. Initially she wanted to go find a void crack and leave this world, however all of the cracks she found where highly unstable. She was not sure of where she would land if they were to travel. With many people, creatures and the like arriving, it would be difficult to live carefree here like they had been the last decade. Jin Meixiu furrowed her eyebrows as she thought deeply about this problem. How to solve it? She started forming some ideas...

"Oh, who knew this hidden world had such a beauty? Brothers, I must thank you for leaving this one for me." A voice could be heard from afar, a group of people appeared some ways away in the desert. Although distant, such distances were nothing in the eyes of experts They were headed straight towards the ruins.

Another voice responded swiftly, a bit of mirth was hidden in his voice, "Haha-you jest! Naturally the one who gets to her first gets to keep her!"

"Maybe if the others joined in, I'll have to forfeit, but Just you? I'd like to see how you will stop me!?"

Several laughs echoed across the yellow sands. The captain and vice captain always bantered like this. The rest of the crew found it intertaining, often throwing in their own jabs every now and then.

The visage of the people was soon apparent. All of them had on mixed garments of different colors. Once outside the city, they all paused. In front of them was the object of their taunts.

Jin Meixiu was unaware of their appearance, looking at the city, her mind was filled with her own plans.

A short male with ash-grey eyes and torn clothes stepped out from behind the captains and spoke, "Not even turning around? Humph! When the captain speaks, the world shall answer. I wish to see where a girl like yourself grew the balls to disobey!"

The group was met with no response. The Captain looked at the vice captain and sent a soundless tranmission between them.

'The world shifted and we've been here only a short while. We should be inside a neighboring cave world. There are still too many unknowns here, we shouldn't be too overbearing'

The vice captain nodded, soundlessly responding,

'We should send Yu Lin to take a look. The fool would be willing to throw himself into the fire if it meant he could taste the soup.'

Grinning at the man who had just spoken out, the vice captain spoke carelessly, "Yu Lin, take several of our brothers there to have a look. I'm curious what kind of entertainment we will have tonight!"

Yu Lin gave a big smile and called upon a few people next to him. Togethor they marched directly to Jin Meixiu, with Yu Lin Leading the way.

Yu Lin stared in disdain at the people around him, none of them really wanted to come. They were always too cautious about these things, with the captains in the Manifestation Realm, what problems could they not take care of? Unless an old late-staged demon appeared, nothing would happen.

A salacious tongue swiped slowly over Yu Lins lips, 'Looking at it now, just the view from behind is quite the marvel to look at, he-he if not for the captains I wouldn't dare give up this kind of treasure.'

As the small group aproached, Jin Meixiu thoughts concluded. Turning around, she was greeted with the sight of a group of people. Her eyes bathed over the swathe of dull colors. 

"A bunch of grey demons?" Jin Meixiu eyes took everything in with a glance.

Yu Lin who had taken the initiative to come over was stunned silent in place, 'Too beautiful, simply too beautiful'. If before he only had thoughts of fighting his captain for this lady, his courage had now risen sharply. 

His eyes wandered from her long slender legs up to her waist. Despite being hidden under red robes his imagination filled in the the curves he could see. each passing moment he felt a burning passion and longing. Even the others who had no interest in such thuggish work had begun showing an interests. 

As their eyes made it past her waist and up to her face, Yu Lin had a good look at her face.

"Red eyes?" Yu Lin felt troubled. He could feel the demonic energy flowing around her, and as far as he knew, all demons had grey eyes and aura. "Why would it be red?" His mind focused as a sense of alert began echoing in his mind.

"She's Mines!" 

One of the subordinates next to Yu Lin appearance flashed, his hands became a blur as he pulled out several artifacts from his sleeves. One was a Beast Tamming net, the other a black talisman.

He had yet to deploy his artifacts when his body lagged, stumbling to the ground while dropping his treasures. His body collapsed and slid until he stopped just inches away from Jin Meixiu's foot.

Thump! 

The other man snickered, "Ha! Trying to get an early start on us, Look at how you slipped. This is why I told you to practice your movement techniques more often." He made a knowing smirk towards Yu Lin but his smile froze when he saw Yu Lins' face

'Killing with intent!' 

Alarm Bells were ringing in Yu Lins head. Although his partner had not realized it, their friend on the ground was definitely dead. What slipped and fell? Could a demon fall on accident? He had never heard of such a thing.

He was definitely not any stronger than the man on the ground. Yu Lins' sweaty hands clasped togethor as he forced out a trembling smile, "My friend here has made a small mistake and introduced us poorly. His death was well deserved. Please, accept this as an apology"

Yu Lin's heart bled as a ring was slipped from his finger as he presented it as a gift to Jin Meixiu. He did not want to do this but keeping his life was more important.

"hmm?"

Jin Meixiu's eyes narrowed as her eyes focused on the ring that had slipped of a finger.

"Not bad. I have no idea how someone like you have something like this, seems like you have some interesting methods. This will be a nice gift for my son." 

As the ring fell into her palm, a light glow was briefly emitted as her spirit quickly perused the contents inside. The ring was a special one, inside of it contained a space no larger than a travel bag. Something like this was very costly, and the more she thought about, the stranger it seemed that this nobody could posses one. In fact, most people would assume it to be vain jewelry at first glance.

But for people with strength such as herself, such things we not worth much. But the resources inside could help her son who was in early stages of cultivation.

Yu Lin's companion was eying his friend on the ground. Earlier, he was confused but seeing as his friend had not yet stood back up, things began clicking one by one in his mind. He tacitly shut his mouth and let Yu Lin continue talking.

"Our group has recently arrived in this world, but wish to know more. If I may ask, our captains are willing to discuss this further. Would senior be willing?" Yu Lin words were short and sweet. His face all smiles, and he gestured kindly. His previous thoughts and intentions seemingly never settled in his mind. 

"Bring them here." Jin Meixiu responded nonchalantly.

Nearby in the yellow sands, the captains soundlessly cursed their luck, 'Damn demon, if he wanted to go to hell, he shouldn't have dragged us down with him!'

The captains were horrified, they had clearly seen the entire interaction but they didn't dare claim they could kill an Awakened demon without even touching them. In fact, they have no idea how he died, and at this distance, they couldn't pretend they didn't hear her words and run. Without further prompting, they quickly arrived in front of Jin Meixiu.

"Greetings to Miss, our group has-"

Jin Meixiu waved her hand, "Enough of that, this group is disbanded."

The captain went quiet and listened.

"This city is mines, as well as the surrounding for ten thousand miles. Its a little small, but that is how big this desert will eventually grow. Currently, this fractured world is still expanding and soon it will absorb many small worlds such as yours."

"The world was expanding?" 

There was clamor amongst themselves as this revelation was too shocking. The group was used to the sudden changes in then environment but not one to such an extent. It seemed that the cave worlds were undergoing some kind of drastic change.

When Jin Meixiu opened her lips, the rest quieted down, "Your group must have dived into this realm fairly recent to not know this. These cave worlds are only branches of space. They are being attracted to this area before being absorbed into the land. This desert is one such space."

She paused for a moment, taking in their gazes before continuing, "Oh and anyone who lands in the desert from now on belongs to the Jin family, you people included."

The captains felt their scalps turn numb and gave each other a glance. Although the lady spoke as if this was some trivial matter, this was too a big change. And being inducted into a family was akin to kidnapping. Although they had no choice, such things could not be decided on a whim.

The vice captain stepped forward and spoke, "Senior, you mentioned your family own these lands but ten thousand miles is the size of small to medium sized clans. I don't doubt your power but if we are to patrol such a large space, we would not be of much use. Is your clan also here in this world?"

Jin Meixiu nodded at this response, it might seem like he was talking back but these are questions a subordinate would need to ask. Simply agreeing would be too unreasonable. Naturally they would need to understand the circumstance.

"mhmm. My husband and son will be moving here with me. You all will be rebuilding the city, along with anyone else who falls into my territory. As for claiming the area..."

A crimson glow began to surface from deep within her body. Her eyes glowed as they seemed to reflect the color of bad blood. As they stood, the group grew wobbly as they lowered their bodies closer to the ground to steady themselves. It seemed as if the ground would fracture at any point and the sky visibly darkened as it took a reddish hue.

kr-kr-ca-ca-ca!

The sky was murmering admidst the yellow sands, across the entire desert, demonic beasts began looking up into the sky. The originally clear sky and bright celestial light had become several tones darker, as if a storm would soon begin raining down. 

Detailed Scales emerged on Jin Meixiu's skin, red demonic energy flowed endlessly from her body and expanded across the desert skies. Torrential red rain begun to fall everywhere, every spot it touched would become drenched with a slightly red aura as it permeated into the surroundings.

"Atleast an ascension realm demon!" The captain felt the blood draining from his face as he gazed at the endless rain. Was this still something a demon could do? Just standing her she can affect the world around her. The ascension realm was only a single realm above his, while he was in the beginning stages of the manifestation realm. It would be centuries before he even progressed to the peak of his realm. Maybe he could never reach the next realm in his entire life.

The wind was beating across his body, causing him to shake, his densely packed spirit core flared, protecting him from further harm. It made him unsteady, but it was not able to shake him down completely. However his weaker subordinates was not as lucky. Their strength was insuficient to defend, collapsing to the ground amidst the overbearing pressure.

He remembered that he initially considered running. 

'Running? Even if I was ten times faster, I'm afraid I would be unable to reach a place her eyes could not see or her power could not reach. Ha, if this pressure was directed at me, I would die without even knowing how'

Had he actually tried that he knew what kind of fate awaited him. Nearby, a lacky of his, still laying warmly on the ground, was already the perfect demonstration.

The rain lasted for a few moments. The winds began to die down across the sands and the reddish aura seeped away from the air. When it ended, everything seemed to contain a permanent red glaze as if to resemble the peculiarities of certain demon.

"Forgive me for my earlier words, it was me who failed to see seniors' strength." The captain had a pale complexion but he kept a warm demeanor as he made his remarks.

Sensing the surrounding changes, the group noticed a particular energy in the air. The moment their spirit made contact with it, they could feel a warding presence. It was Jin Meixiu's territorial mark. 

All demons knew about this as its usually meant as a warning to others as they declare a territory. But doing it in such a fashion... No one dared to imagine such a thing. This manuever was far too extravagant and marks like these were normally developed over a great period of time.

Jin Meixiu scanned the surrounding, "Who here is a fox-demon?" 

Yu Lin stepped forward, "This lowly one here is one."

The rest of the group looked at Yu Lin strangely as a small disk made its way into Yu Lins hands. Some of them wondered what Yu Lin gave her previously to warrant such comments and now this. 

"This is a communication array, when I need you it will shake. You should find the inner city to find me."

Jin Meixiu issued a few more commands for them and headed off into the city.

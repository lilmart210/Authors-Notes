Sand dunes stretched far in every direction. What was once a lush verdant forest had resembled nothing from its past. The sand spread rampantly over the surrounding area, corrupting the lands with its death grip over life's neck. The greenery that surrounded Jin Hai's training spot appeared to have vanished overnight.

xi-xing, xi-xi-xing!

The sound of cuts could be heard before settling down. A single sword, a single boy, each stood facing each other with several hundred feet between them. Jin Hai stared down the sword, he felt this sword had some intelligence of its own. It could respond to his attacks in ways that were subtly different, disrupting his own plans of attack and ruining his countermeasures.

"Dumb sword! Today will be the last day you float there freely!" Jin Hai had a matter of fact expression and contiued in his speech, "humph! You have been a formidable opponent these last few months. I'm feeling a bit generous and will listen to your last words, before I return you to father."

The sword wasn't a living being, it could not speak back. It continued to hover, occasional red sand would wisk up infront of it but fall to the ground due to the swords' surrounding gravity field.

"Very well then, since those are your final wishes, I will make sure to destroy those places once I capture you. What? You were not expecting this? haha! You should have thought of that before telling me!"

Jin Hai's aura exploded, it was many folds greater than what it had been a few months ago. His energy was smooth and seemless, the blue energy traveling underneath his skin marched through his body with practiced effeciency. If his father saw this he would be frightened by the pace at which his son has progressed. This kind of skill would take a human decades to accomplish.

The blue energy dyed the surrounding sand as he shot fourth, closing the gap between them immediately. Brandishing his sword, he observed every change carefully within his sight. Feeling his intentions, the sword's arua sharpened greatly, emitting its own field. As Jin Hai aproached within ten feet of the sword, narrow bands of formless pressure was emitted outwards, like fast moving birds, they shot towards him.

'The first wave of three, formless attacks'

Jin Hai's red eyes narrowed to slits as the attack aproached him.

'Swallowtail Sword Dance!'

Jin hai manuevered carefully, he executed the last several sword moves from the Blue Mountain Sword technique. These moves combined and complimented each other, turning into a sword dance.

Pa-Pa-Pa-Pa!

Each band was struck accurately and with his momentum at its peak, Jin Hai's figure moved and he appeared three feet closer to the floating sword.

The sword shook, as if retaliating in anger, thousands of bands were emitted all at once. Each one aiming for the boy's vitals.

'These are weaker than the first attack, Thousand Catty Stance'

Jin hai's body never stopped moving, activating Thousand Catty Stance does not require one to be standing still, the attacks landed on his skin but dispersed upon hitting him. He advanced another three feet.

"two of three, Hehe, its over for you! Third Move, Retrieval!"

With his sword in the air, its weight became unbearably as it crashed down, he would force the sword into defensiveness with this attack, and with the momentum of battle on his side, the sword would have no choice but to respond, he would close the distance between them and grab the sword.

Creeee~!

The sword wailed, it was in an overwhelmingly disadvantagous situation, the desert sand whipped up, speckling the sky as it responded with a strike of its own, formless pressure ascended into the air as they met with Jin Hai's strike!

Bang!

The sound of metal clashing took over the air. The ground underneath the sword and boy cracked and gaveway under the impact. The surrounding one hundred feet fractured and collapsed, revealing the dense desert stone underneath the sand.

A hand appeared inches from the sword hilt and grabbed it, no matter how the sword struggled it was unable to break free.

"I've already told you today was your last!"

Jin hai released his own weapon and was holding down the sword with two hands, no matter how it struggled, he would never let it go. He spent these three months figuring out counters for all off the sword attacks, although they were different each time, he was also evolving.

'Eh? Blood?'

Blood dripped onto the ground, Jin hai felt his hair raise. 'Why would I be bleeding?'

He hadn't gotten hit by the stronger attacks nor did the weaker attacks break his defenses. What was wrong with him?

Jin Hai felt his strength waning, his aura started turning a faint red as scales began covering his body. The pressure Jin Hai felt from the sword increased greatly. The sword, sensing its opponent weakness, seized the opportunity.

'You-'

Bang!

Confused, Jin Hai's body ragdolled across the ruined desert dune. Deep cuts enough to see bone littered his body as he flew, blood pooled out as he crashed thousands of feet away from his earlier battle.

The flesh and bone in his arms were visible and his body only stopping after several thousand feet. 

Jin Hai lay flat on the ground, silent. The red in his eyes deepend as his blue aura vanished completely. Strength began filling his body as his body repaired itself. 

He began thinking about those words his father had said.

'This...Must be what he meant...But what was the method of attack?'

More than the pain, Jin Hai was stuck in his own mind. The clearing was silent for a period of time. 

While the red scales repaired his broken body, Jin Hai savored the defeat. Every time his body repaired itself, he could feel his body being tempered and so he would choose to lay flat like this. It was pretty comfortable outside of the initial pain.

"Poison! The sword actually poisoned me!" Jin Hai eyes flashed open as he sprung up and glared at the sword across the sands, "You-You actually dare use poison in our rightous battle!? I had already grabbed you, I won, how could you still fight back knowing this!?"

Jin Hai had already forgot he was playing the villain earlier. His anger reached his peak, forgoing his earlier formalities.

"Screw training! human techniques? demonic inhertance? who cares anymore! I. Want. You. Destroyed!"

Red scales covered his body as his sword flew over to his hand. When he grasped it, his body flashed and he reappeared a thousand feet in the air. Killing intent flowed freely from the boys body, even his sword had become soaked in the aura. He was told to never mix demonic energy and human energy as they were not able to mix. Demonic energy corroded human energy and human techniques required conflicting paths. Yet in his anger, he did not consider these things. He wanted to use all of his strength.

A murky, reddish Blue aura began appearing in his surroundings, powered by his demonic energy, it was tens of times stronger than before.

The sword felt the threat, unbeknownst to Jin Hai, the sword had begun secretly accumulating energy despite being told not to. Around it, a formless aura rippled accross the sands. Beneath it, sand turned to desert stone and the desert stone became so dense that the cuts and scratches soothed themselves out.

Jin Hai spat through clenched teeth, "Humph, lets see how arrogant you'll be when I return you in pieces!"

But before he could attack, Jin Hai's energy channels began breaking and cracking from the stress. Even with a strenghened body, Demonic energy did not like to flow in the shape of human techniques. Human techniques required a delicate and constant flow, it was unable to handle the sheer volume of demonic energy.

"T-th-this can't happen!" Floating in the air, he tried to control it however under his skin, the cycle of energy began twisting and warping. He was on the verge of loosing his control. His mind started to cool off from his earlier outburst but he was unable to stop his momentum. He needed to find a way out fast.

The sword had nearly formed its attack, it decided to launch a single decisive strike to end the fight. A blue aura began making its way out from the sword with a trace of killing intent. This natal artifact responded in kind to Jin Hai's provocations.

Seeing his power grow weaker and out of control, Jin Hai felt for the first time, his red demon physique fail to keep up with his intentions. The burst vessels were not healing as fast as they should be and the pain began taking root into his bones. A needle like sensation had begun prying into spaces of his body he had never known about. 

But he didn't quit just yet. He delved deep into thought. The changes in his body, the soothing of his channels, the differences between human and demon. He understood these things well. The past few months he would get beaten as a human, heal as a demon and fight as a human once more. This cycle repeated endlessly, every single day. It was too the point he felt it pointless to differentiate between the two sides of his.

Figures began appearing in Jin Hai's mind.

Jin hai let out a small gasp of shock, "Mom talked about these, These are bloodline memories!"

Unlike humans, Demons pass on techniques through blood. Simply being born apart of the family is enough.

Time slowed to a crawl as scenes flashed one by one in his mind. Jin Hai began understanding a certain truth in the world. While deep in thought, His body was nearing its breaking point, the energy that pooled under his skin began breaking loose, causing him to bleed and damage his body further.

Seeing the boy in the sky was unresponsive, the sword decided to make its move. Bands of energy that were floating around it, began coellescing into a single point of energy. 

Voom!

A bright blue sword light swept out, and leaped towards Jin Hai.

Jin Hai only managed to open his eyes slightly before the attack was upon him. His entire vision was dyed multiple shades of blue. But he was strangely calm. Waves of pressure descended upon him but before it could hit, its power began to diminish.

In the corner of Jin Hai's left eye, a tattoo like marking of a blood red pupil began to engrave itself slowly into the sclera. Despite looking like art in the eyes, it seemed to gaze intensely at the incoming light.

The sword attack which was initially powerful and intimidating shriveled in form, from dying the sky blue, it shrunk to almost nothing. By the time it hit Jin Hai, it scattered like ashes in the wind, unable to leave a mark.

"This is... My bloodline ability? Its too strong isn't it?"

Jin Hai was ecstatic, He quickly assumed a medititative positioning and the rampant power quelled under the gaze of his own eyes. He felt the strength of his rampant powers weaken as it dissipated into nothing. Red scales and energy seaped into his energy channels, repairing the enlarged and damaged vessels as his appearance returned to normal. Although recovered, Jin Hai felt that his body greatly needed to rest for a period of time before he could fight again.

"My ability seems to allow me to scatter energy, but those ancestors I saw, both of their eyes had two real pupils."

He could feel the etching seemingly carved into the side of his eyes move freely about.

Thinking about it some more, Jin Hai pondered, 

"And their eye didn't float around like mines. Is something wrong?"

Jin Hai delved back into his thoughts, ignoring the sword that floated mindlessly below him. As long as he doesn't try to attack it first, it will not try to attack him again.

Inside his mind, he could see red demons fighting amongst each other. Each one had polycoria and wielded peculiar techniques. On a closer inspection, the demons looked somewhat like himself, albeit older.

Jin Hai became entranced, by the time he woke up, the sky had already turned dark. 

Staring at the sword below him, Jin Hai's smile grew. Jin Hai's aura flared, in his hands, a red sword convincingly carved from stone began condensing itself from nothing as a second pupil formed and rose from the corner of his eye. He energy traveled along the paths of the Blue Mountain technique and he yelled out full of brovado.

"We are not finished yet!"

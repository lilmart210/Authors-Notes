"So they escaped after all..."

These words were spoken very lightly. Standing up from his wooden desk, the man made his way to the arched window behind him. It held a certain beauty, one that he apreciated greatly.

Underneath his gaze, people could be seen moving to and from places, some faster than others but never lingering within his sights for long.

He was Qin Peizhi, city lord of Extreme Path City.

He looked hard at the outdoors before sighing. His loosely hanging sleeves whipped behind him quickly, his palms slamming hard into his desk.

"After chasing those damn rats for years, I've finally trapped them in my greatest scheme! But not only are you telling me they escaped from certain imprisonment, they also took those important possessions as well!?"

There was a figure with their head pressed deep into the floor, if not out of respect, then out of fear as sweat covered his entire body.

"Lord-I, I-"

"You Fool! what do I need excuses for? Tell me, what plans do you have to fix this?"

The man on the floor felt wronged, but he kept those thoughts to himself. The merchandise that was missing was far more important than his little life and those possessions needed to be here as the auction list has already been handed out many weeks in advance.

And it should not be mentioned what would happen to him should they not be found in time...

The man on the floor gritted his teeth and said, "Lord Qin, do not worry, I have already sent a few people after them. There is even a diviner amongst those people, they will certainly return shortly"

To be a diviner was a rare skill to have and being acquianted with one brought many benefits. People skilled in these professions were often aloof figures but having the ability to command one showed that the man shivering on the floor was not a small existence.

But city lord Qin couldn't be bothered to look at him, instead he was uninterested in whatever the man had to say. Qin Peizhi gave his groomed beard a few thoughtful strokes. Things were already in motion so he returned his gaze to outside the window. It seemed the busy bodies beyond the glass held more worth to him now than ever. In comparison to outside, the loudest sound indoors was the wind passing by the walls.

Amidst his thoughts, city lord Qin grunted, If he hadn't already sent people after them, then he really didn't need such a manager. After all, the next person would certainly be more competent. To not make the same mistakes that got their predecessor killed could be said to be the duty of all his underlings.

The man on the ground knew his role as the city lord's manager was over. Lord Qin was a vicious man but treated those in his circle with immense benefits. Almost everything he had accomplished in his career was but a gift from the city lord.

But he knew that what happened today was far below lord Qins bottom line. He was angry at the world for conspiring against him but more so at himself for such a large oversight. Throughout these years, he made sure to manage affairs properly, but if he could not provide a satisfactory answer then his retirement from office would also be his yearly aniversary.

Anyone who knew him, could only bring him flowers.

"Lord Qin, I happened to have a favor from a powerful Earth Realm warrior outside the city, he can definitely help search on account of past favors"

Qin Peizhi's eyebrows twitched, but the silence continued, slowly becoming deafening to the ears.

Lord Qin seemed to be thinking of something, much to the expense of the managers well being. He had already said so much but the city lord had not spared him a single response. If he could see that the city lord was currently thinking deeply about something, then the little manager would not have said anything at all.

City lord Qin frowned before asking his manager, "Which items did those fools take exactly?"

"Sir, the World Revealing Stone, 4 mid spirit-building spiritual beast cores and the unapraised ring."

Seeming understanding something, a grin appeared on lord Qins face.

Reaching into space, Qins Peizhi's hands flashed and a rock no bigger than his palms appeared in the middle of his hand.

Looking at the rock, several edges glowed brightly and if the man on the floor was looking up, he would become confused, because this was clearly the World Revealing Stone that was taken. Despite being made of stone, its surfaced rippled endlessly like waves deep in the sea. Togethor, the waves formed an array that pointed towards a certain direction.

Just now, Symbols began to appear and the direction of the arrow rippled before changing. 

Qin Peizhi grinned, his face revealed a hideous smile before swiping his hands once more across space. As if a trick, the peculiar stone could not be found and he immediately issued commands.

"Let that diviner and his team keep pursuing those fellas, ShouShan, you will follow me, theres something that we have to go do."

"Yes... a-Yes!", hearing that means that he gets to keep his little life, if only for a few minutes more. Manager Shoushan got up quickly and bowed while lord Qin walked past. His nervousness gone as he followed Lord Qin outside of his office.

The buzz of the city soon came into their eyes, ShouShan and Qin Peizhi were currently exiting the town hall. In front of them was Extreme Path City. Situated in the heart of the city was the administrative building from where they had just left. It occupied the most prominent position due to the many factions occupying the surroundings. As the only nuetral party amongst all of them, government officials could be said to be the only people who could occupy such a space without any rejection from other forces.

Atleast on paper this seemed good, but Qin Peizhi was under a great amount of pressure. While he could not overtly favor a single side, it also meant that every party was watching him closely. In the event anyone was displeased, it would become a headache for him.

Glancing at the bustling city, there was some self-satisfaction in the city lords eyes. Although he considered this position as a mere stepping stone for his future success, the effort he put into it was real.

"It seems that it's my luck today, that we can meet today without much trouble."

A voice called from afar.

Qin Peizhi squinted, many faces appeared within his mind as the figure of a tall man with flowing black hair aproached. The city lord was not one to respond so quickly. Without a good idea with who he was talking to, he would never blatantly respond without much thought.

Eying a long sword whose handle was odly dark, even during the day, and the enchanting figure of the man, the city lord concluded his thoughts promptly.

"So its young master Lei, I must have troubled you much for you to have come here personally."

Outwardly, he had the biggest smiles but inwardly the Qin Peizhi was troubled...

Although young, this Lei clan heir was the sole inheritor of the current clan patriarch. With both strength, looks and the intelligence to match, the Lei clan was the only Prominent family whose succession was already decided. He had no choice but to pay adequate attention to him.

"haha! Not at all, but I'm intrigued about a piece of news I heard recently, I rushed here to hear a little more about it..."

Young master Lei was now face to face with the city lord and his manager. Five imposing gaurds dressed in similar fashion was also following the young master. Each with a muscular frame and a long sword at their sides that shimmered in the light.

Lord Qin's head shook and his hand waived as he responded immediately, 

"Then it must be fate that we met like this, ShouShan would be happy to explain these things."

ShouShan, who had been silent during this time, sprung forward to face the young master. He was truly thankful for him appearing at this crucial moment, being useful was the thing he needed the most right now.

As Young master Lei and ShouShan began their talks and started walking towards the buildings entrance, Peizhi gave Shoushan a hard stare. In truth, he was very satisfied with Shoushan. Lord Qin planned on bringing ShouShan with him, but now he had some doubts. He had never been more hesitant than now but gazing at the streets and the people in this crowded city, his worries slowly eased.

This city and its inhabitants was truly nothing to him. Although he spent nearly two decades over-seeing this place, his time to leave had finally come. What was fate? This was fate. He shook his head once more and began his journey again.

Odly enough, despite being a well known and respected figure, no one seemed to give him a second glance while he made his way through the crowded streets and away from the heart of the city. As if he was another unremarkable citizen in the vast areas that surrounded, Qin Peizhi made his way-albeit slowly-to the edge of Extreme path city. Occasionally, he would stop and take a look at the wet markets, shops and people that he would pass by. It was not long before he had aproached large stone walls that marked the entrance to the bustling state.

When he encountered the gates, there was already people queuing for the exit. The gaurds did not check in particular for residency but rather to keep a log of who has entered and exited. This was something he had put in place himself, as it allowed him to collect tarifs from the merchants and taxes from the people. But more importantly, have finer control over the neighboring sects or alert him of some figure sudden visit.

It was a busy place at the borders but no matter what the people were doing, Qin Peizhi walked past them all and even the gaurds at the wall did not stop him. Actually, they seemed to not have seen him at all.

By now, Qin Peizhi's nostalgia and worries had vanished completely. Once outside of the city, a stone appeared in his hands, as if by magic.

Waves ripled on the stones surface, the arrow unchanging from before.

"It seems its still a bit too early..."

A smile slowly etched itself onto the face of the city lord.

"B-b-big sis ther-"

"Dodge!"

A paw filled with long, sharp claws struck accurately into the smallest figure's back. Tearing through the cloak and into flesh, five daggers dug deep enough to tear into bone.

"Ahh! keugh, he..lp..m..e..." A voice yelled out but was muffled as a six foot tall panther-like beast was now hunched over the body as it stood gaurd, pressing its body weight further against its prey.

"Little Mei!" The tallest of the hooded three called out, her voice filled with grief. She wanted nothing more than to tear this wild beast to shreds.

Seperate figures dashed directly at the big cat, A series of sword strikes lashed out but landed amongst the air. 

Frantically looking around, they spotted the beast quite a few paces away. The beast sat on its rear hunches as it began taking several steps back slowly.

'How Fast!'

They just had an exchange and the big cat was already on the other side of the cave. Eying the big cat, they finally had a chance to look around. It seems they appeared in a monsters den. Underfoot was heaps of dried grass covering rocky gravel and above them was a stone sky. 

The big cat merely glared at them, its eyes pin needles as it carefully watched their next move. It was also perplexed, how had these creatures appeared here with out any prior notice? And next to it no less? Taking a few cautios sniffs, it began to investigate but the draft coming in had no smell of foreigners. How they got in, it had no idea.

Watching the blood soak through her hood and onto the ground, the big sister immediately began pulling off what remained of her hooded disguise. If her back had not been disfigured from the previous strike, her skin would have been flawless and a breathtaking sight.

But now broken bones could be seen and muscle was strewn about. It was a horrible sight. The middle sister felt slightly dizzy looking at her back.

The big sister looked towards the beast returned her gaze, "Damn Cat! You dare attack us like this!"

The big sister stared daggers at the large feline, while the cat was licking it paws. Spirit Beasts like this primarily communicated through strength, and with its intelligence, it could think and reason. Its glanced around the clearing, eyes seemingly searching for something.

They stopped before long, realizing that if these weak humans could leave the same way they appeared, they would have done so already.

The only exit left is behind itself, in which case, they are far from being strong enough to get by. The cat tasted the blood on its paws and each taste held a rich flavor. The cats appetite was slowly getting worked up. Them dying here seemed to already be a forgone conclusion.

"Quickly, open your mouth and eat this!"

A small red pill was stuffed through clentched teeth by the eldest sister. It was a blood-returning pill, a low grade medicinal pill given to disciples, but of immense value to mortal realm practitioners.

Chewing the pill, the youngest sat up slowly with the help of her sisters, strength was returning but it was not enough to help in this fight.

A stone made its way into her hands and on it, were many ripples, arrows pointing in many directions. 

The oldest kept the feline within its sights, her eyes darkened as she felt the power eminating from the spirit beasts' body.

"This isn't good, there's a flow of energy flowing around it."

The middle sister paled slightly, wild animals could already fight mortal stage cultivators. If they can utilize the flow of spirit, they could even fight above their levels.

"I will hold the beast back, You two look for a way to escape, the stone should have a way!"

The middle sister nodded silently, wincing at the sudden movement, the smallest of the trio was lifted and place onto the back of the other.

Looking at the cat, the oldest gaze turned grave. 

"This creature is a Spirit beast, and definitely above Qi Gathering at that."

The other two where solemn at that news. Eldest sister was already in the Spirit Building stage of the Mortal Realm but spirit Beast where not like normal animals. Their attacks were strenghened greatly and humans in the same level were not their match.

"It's earlier than I would have liked, but those spiritual stones, I'll use them to hold the beast until you all escape"

"But if you do then-"

"If we don't do this, then none of use will leave!"

The eldest sister rebutted immediately, leaving no time for refusal.

A hand dug into a pocket and grasp a sparkling stone tightly. Pulling out a mid-grade spiritual stone, the Eldest Sister expression hardened as she began circulating her energy accordingly to her arts. A torrent of violent energy began to flow as she felt a surge of energy course through her body. The stone shriveled up only slightly before she put it back.

Veins began to bulge and even trace amounts of blood can be seen bubbling up to the skins surface. She felt her power reach its peak. She could feel her body reaching its limit and she needed to act quickly in order to dispel these effects.

The big cat immediately looked up, scrutinizing its prey before letting out a low growl. There was a glow of light upon the human which caught its attention.

Now that it looked carefully, Something was different about this human now.

The eldest immediately lunged forward, several times faster than before, striking at the cat before it had a chance to dodge. The sound of screeching metal rang out as her sword clashed several times with the claws of the beast. 

The other two could not keep up with the pace of the fight. All they could do was look.

"be-behind the cat is the ex-exit" was a voice no louder than internal monologue. 

Glancing back at the stone in her hand, there were fewer ripples than earlier, and the few rippes that remained seem too far from the center of the stone. The only one that remained showed an arrow directly in front of them.

"this..."

The middle sister was eying the fight in front of them. How could they even begin to keep up? Violent strikes lashed out, marking the ground and its surroundings. By the time they made it the otherside, their would be nothing but scraps of them left. Nevertheless, She began picking up the youngest, looking for any opportunity to leave the moment it arose.

The eldest figure put fourth great effort in this strike. Around her, time seemed to slow sligthly. The eldest figure put fourth great effort in this strike. But not to be outdone, the beastly feline would not give in easily.

"Ha!"

Roar!

Bang!

The sounds of swords clashed, loose rocks and gravel broke free and scattered amidst the air, along with a human and a beast back to their initial positions.

"Sister Hong, ah, Let me help!"

Several voices rang out with one carrying the other. They lept to the side of a bloody human. Getting ready to join, she was about to put her younger sister down when a hand stopped her.

"ha-hah, I'm fine, this blood isn't mines, but that foul cat!"

Glancing at the darkly patterned cat, whose appearance was equally hideous, showed fierce eyes constricted to pin-needles. The large feline seemed unwilling. It wanted nothing more than to continue but with its current state, it was only a deathwish.

It stared heavily at the three humans in front of it, eyes scrutinizing the other after its short exchange. Growling, in a low rumble while oozing nearly tangible bloodlust.

She continued to press on, "how about another round, lets see how you escape this time"

Standing up and pushing the debris that stuck to her, was the echo of a voice, filled with confidence. She acted full of energy, as if the last exchange had not occured.

The cat did not understand many things about its prey anymore. Who was to be hunted did not seem so clear either. Without waiting further, it stepped back a few paces. Then a few more paces, while growling intensely.

Darkness seeped around the rear paw of the cat and with each step it took backwards, its body was no longer as clear. Before long, not even its threatening low rumble could be heard. The beast had fled.

After seeing the large cat was no longer in sight, The eldest sister collapsed to a knee and spat a mouthful of blood. Just below her skin, in several spots on her face and body, a dark color began to appear. The other two looked at her worriedly.

Wasn't she fine just a moment ago?

"Lets go, the spirtual stone was too much. I can't withstand the energy for much longer. Where's the exit?"

"There is only one, and its where the beast was at earlier"

The eldest looked hard in that direction. It was nice that the fight had ended, but to follow after it seemed unwise.

"Is there really no other way?"

Glancing at the stone in the youngest hands. There really was no other way. Short of breaking the walls between these tunnels, the only exit was the one in front. The other option was too tiring an endeavor.

"Do we have to leave?" 

The middle sister spoke up, while letting the youngest down. The youngest injuries had yet to heal and the strongest person here needed time to correct the rampant energy in her body.

"We can't stay! One way in, no ways out. If something stronger than that cat comes in, we are all finished!"

The eldest refused such an idea, but the middle sister spoke reason,

"but if we leave and encounter another spiritual beast, we won't survive."

Both solutions were the reality and as they contemplated, an akward silence appeared between them. 

Kuogh!

The eldest sister coughed up another mouth full of blood, her complexion turning many shades of color. Forcibly raising her strength by using the stone caused her to suffer internal injuries. Immediately, she sat down in a meditative position and began circulating her energy. The middle sister sat down behind her to help, feeling around her body and helping soothe the problems.

They really couldn't afford to leave or stay, but staying seemed to be a better choice than leaving now. Unless she left both her junior and senior behind, her group was in no shape to travel.

The youngest was still weak from earlier. Even so, she also forced herself into a meditiative position. The blood was no longer flowing due to the pill but the searing heat on her back made it nearly unbearable to sit straight up. She would need better medicine for her body to mend completely.

She could only look around the cave they were in.

Some corners were far too dark and could hide anything. The ground she was above  was now a mix of stone and dirt. Marks from swords and claws marked the surroundings. Occasionally, she would spot a bone fragment of some creature poking up from the dirt. 

It was truly an unerving place to be in.

'That smile, from earlier. I wonder if i saw it correctly?'

Recalling that terryfying smile from earlier, left her freightened, and shortly after she was nearly killed. However despite that, the feeling she felt from that face terrified her even more than that beast.

The pain made her wince, but with the pill working, she could bear it. But the fear engrained itself in her bones. She was shaking just thinking of it. Even as she encountered the ruins of her family, she had not felt such fear.

Looking into the darkened corners once more, she seemed to see that same smile.

'To feel so anxious about it, I don't think even nightmare creatures could compare'

The smile slowly gew larger and a pair of ghoulish eyes reflected a dark black light.

'humph! So what if you are scary? We survived that spiritual beast, I'm no longer afraid!' Her body was unnearved but she fought mentally.

Mustering the courage to fight back, the youngest shouted at her imagination. They say the mind is often deluded by what it fears the most, but she, for one, was not so easily tricked!

"hehehe, ha-haha! Ha-hehe~"

'I must be crazy to even hear voices'

The ghoulish eyes had no good intentions and the cruel smile laughed madly. From the shadows, a hand with blood dripping down its sleeves emerged.

The youngest heart began to beat rapidly,

Then the sound of a step being taken could be heard from its depths. a foot had stepped fourth.

'Thi-this can not be only my imagination'

Immediately the youngest shot up, or atleast tried too but her muscles were frozen stiff, unable to move or shout in the sligthest. An icy feeling swept into her body as he realized she could only watch.

It was a nightmare come true. From the face with blackened eyes emerged the rest of the figure from what was a dark corner.

Looking at her sisters who were deep in meditation, she seemed to be the only one aware but she could do nothing but watch the man take step by step towards them. With each step, came fear and as it grew it turned to desperation.

The middle sister seemed to have sensed something and looked up, but it was too late. Their bodies seemed to seize as their eyes dilated. A hand settled above their heads as their bodies contorted. Within their bodies, black crystals blossomed as their bodies went rigid before collapsing onto the ground.

"What marvelous Energy, and from mere mortal realm practitioners at that! The heavens must be watching over me to have this fortune! haha..."

Laughter rung about the cave as a ball of blue light arose from the bodies. Wherever this light appeared the skin beneath seemed to have darkened in contrast. The light gathered and was several times larger than the one he gathered from all of his men whom he had killed earlier.

'Even without this hidden realm, there would have been no way I could pass these gems up!'

His eyes shifted towards the youngest sister, who eyes threatened to flood the world with tears.

"Ha! Don't blame me for this, blame the world, It seems I'm saying this alot today he-hehehe~"

A strong force seemed to grab at her inner soul, her energy channels were sapped dry of their spirtual energy and even her feelings seemed to have been taken with it. As the energy within her retreated, glass like structures began taking their place, breaking the balance within her body. She could not resist as a familiar light emmerged from beneath her skin, dropping lifeless to the ground. The energy merged with the other two balls of light and grew to be over ten times larger than the one from outside.

Looking at the magnificent ball of energy, The man felt that he had not properly eaten before in his life. If he could eat so much everytime he found prey, then he would have already reached the top of the world.

'Something this beautiful had to be admired! Truly a work of art!'

"A devil cultivator?"

A voice cut through the silence, turning around, the man saw what looked like a city official walking from the same darkness he had emerged from.

'An expert!'

But more importantly, he had never sensed his arrival. It had to be noted that he had arrived here nearly at the same time as those girls. He had been here all along.

It can't be he was also there the whole time right?

Seeing his entire figure stepping into the vague lighting, the man was tense, "To what do I owe the pleasure of having to meet our dear lord Qin here?"

Warrily adressing him, he had never heard of city lord Qin having combat abilities, much less any level of cultivation. Looking at him now, he even seemed weaker than those three he killed just now. Atleast thats how he should have been.

Qin Peizhi glanced around the cave, the marks around the cave became scenes in his mind as he deduced what had happened. Looking at the soulless bodies on the ground, it was a pity but there was nothing he could do.

Once they had their energy taken, it wasn't so simple as just returning it. Although kind, Qin Peizhi did not have any plans on going out his way to save others.

"I won't make things difficult, leave the stone those three took and your cultivation methods. Do that and I'll let you off for now."

Qin Peizhi's tone was magnanimous, but the man wasn't buying it.

"Merely an official, a politician's lapdog, not once in all these years have you been known for your strength, I don't believe you have the ability."

Lord Qin nodded in response. Others would rather see than to blindly believe...

From Qi gathering, to somewhere in Foundation establishment, Qin Peizhi's cultivation shot up past the Mortal Realm into the Earth Realm. He could feel the ambience in the cave change as if acknlowledging his formidable presence.

"You! You've been hiding your strength all this time?"

Using cowardly arts like this, he cursed City Lord inwardly. It was not a simple matter to hide your power and the method to do so was incredibly complex.

'Damn old man, why weren't you bluffing!?'

He lunged forward immediately, although it never occured to him to question why or how city lord Qin got into the secret realm, there was nothing they could possibly talk about at this stage. He knew that if the city lord was left alive. Qin Peizhi might let him off but would the sects he mediated do so as well?

Damaging their interests was a fatal mistake so inwardly, he knew the answer.

They exchanged many blows, Fist technique against fist technique, Sword clashed with sword. All the while, those lifeless eyes of the youngest, seemed to have just a small fire of light, eyes struggling to remain open as it watched the battle take place.

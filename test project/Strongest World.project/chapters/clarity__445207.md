A room adourned with many arrays began glowing in the morning light. As it creeped its way throughout the room, someone had begun to stir.

'Is it morning already?'

Jin Meixiu eyes wandered back to life. She had been watching over her daughter for so long. Now that she had rested, she had forgotten all about this sensation.

'Was I sleeping? How long has it been since I've done something like that?'

Potent beings like her did not sleep, or rather, the act of body refinement felt better than sleeping and would leave the mind refreshed. It seems at some point she had begun to rest instead of watch.

Recently, the marble like egg had not been as active and today she felt it was awfully still. Above her head, several array markers were glowing brightly and the newest one was also faintly visible. 

A single crack began growing on her arm. Slowly a life like tattoo began manifesting and a red eye began taking shape. As the appearance of a young child began taking shape in her mind, she immediately sent a voiceless transmision.

"Guangli, little hai, come quick!"

creak~

Immediately after she sent word, the door to the room swung open. 

A boy and his father rushed into the room, eyes observant as they looked around.

"She's going to hatch already?" Jin hai's voice spoke out in a whisper, afraid to speak any louder.

"mhmm"

crsha!

Cracks began appearing over the surface of the white egg. Large veins were visible from the outside and from inside the egg a shadow writhed around inside.

<Wake Up>

Inside the egg, the sleeping demon was had become fully aware but her body was not moving as she wanted. inside the egg prison, she felt anxious and crowded despite fitting comfortably inside. 

She no longer felt at ease growing like this. She had struggled thus far to be born, she would not wait any longer.

<Wake up>

More cracks on the surface of the stone white egg appeared, its marble like exterior began showing fragmentation as the demon inside began forcing its way out.

She could see it. Light. This was a different kind of sight, she had never seen this way before.

It was stunning. She wanted more and she began reaching out her hand. The shell around her began breaking as she began moving with purpose.

The sunlight from outside became more intense and she could feel her senses becoming overwhelmed. Her hands went from reaching towards the light to covering her eyes

<Wake up>

The intense feeling did not stopped and it continued until she could not see anything, even her own thoughts felt sluggish.

"She's awake! Sister is here!" A final voice called out but she could not make sense of who said it.

The bright light was overwhelming, but did not last forever. The vision of her family began to crumble, breaking into pieces.

A small, dainty hand released its hold over a face and reached up, reaching for the ever fading light.

It grew darker and darker as the hand began to desperately grab hold of whatever it could. 

Darkness consumed everything as reality set in all at once.


'Whose....Sister? Whose family was she reaching for?'

Question stirred in the mind as the exciting atmosphere froze and the scenary changed.

The arm that once held its self in the air, began struggling as it jutted back towards the earth. Water splashed when it landed as the muted sound resounded throughout the cave.

'Whats...going on? Who are they? Who...am I?'

Eyes struggled with extreme lethargy as they tried to open. They rippled a few times, with nearly enough energy to reveal what laid underneath but ultimately remained closed. As the scenes flashed amongst her mind, they didn't settle until a while later.

But she grew puzzled as her mind began clearing up.

'Where did these memories come from? Who is jin hai and those other people? My mother's name isn't Meixiu...what is...'

As she became aware, she could think rationally but was more perplexed. She had no family like this, as she was Lin Mei, the youngest sister of the trio.

Strength began seeping into her body yet she hadn't felt much return. Atleast, she could finally open her eyes, yet they were welcomed to a world of darkness. Hands Traveled around empty space but she still saw nothing.

"Not even a drop of energy." Pursed lips murmured, 'I can't even use my techniques to see...Thats strange, where was I? How'd I get-'

A rush of vigor flooded her body as she jolted up, or atleast she tried to.

"Big sis are you there? Where are you! You must respond!"

Struggling with her own depleted body, she called out but there was no response. A desperate voice filled the cave but the sound of trickling water was the only thing that responded.

'It-it can't be-they can't be dead! I survived, they should have woken up before I had! they... they should be here...'

Sniffles and sobs began bubbling to the surface, her worry could not be eased so easily. Her mind was in a mess, she seemed to remember trying to reach out to them, but as more memories began to pour in, nightmarish scenes played over and over in her mind. Terrifying gazes mocked her in silence and her inability to see only made the memories worse.

Curling up into a ball, the puddle she was in only grew further as tears ran down her face. 

She remembered everything. Her inability to move when it mattered the most, the light coming from there bodies as they fell lifeless to the ground, the searing pain of having her own vitality taken and the gradual weakness she felt.

'they are...they are...dead...' 



She thought about those memories she had seen, she didn't know why she had those memories but there was a certain warmth in them whenever she thought about them. 

A family. A living family. It was something she craved now more than anything.

Frustrated, a voice escaped her lips, "Why, why is this happening to me?"

"Why me! Why won't they leave me alone, I don't wish for any of this!"

She wanted nothing more than to stay this way and go back to dreaming. There was nothing in reality waiting for her here. No family, no friends, no people. There was only enmity, hatred and questions. There was nothing in life she could gain anymore. She had lost everything.

What felt like hours ticked by, lamenting her own fate until not even thoughts flowed through her mind. Her eyes half opened, and tears long dried. She didn't want to think and if possible not even be awake.


Slipping into timlessness, her mind was completely absent.

'What should i do?'

It was her first thought after some time and for a long time it was the only thought until Something had begun scratching at her senses. She always wanted to be a fighter, a beast hunter, someone powerful but she did not have the strength to even defend herself. It was always an after thought, but this itch grew stronger the more she thought about it. It was an idea that began to grow stronger in each passing moment until, festering something else within.

She wanted to become the stronger, strong enough to get revenge.

"Revenge"

As she spoke, she savored the word. It felt foreign yet familiar. She couldn't help but say it again.

"Revenge..."

This time, she said sligthly differently, the intonation stroke a different feeling in her mind.

"Ree-ven-ge..."

"....Revenge..."


The word had no echoe and could barely be heard over the soft splashing of water around her but they were filled with a resolve of sorts.

This was not something she would have come to think of before but things were different now. She was frightened but after what felt like dying once, she felt it would be foolish to let those feelings get in her way. Had she done so before, maybe, she could have saved her sisters in front of her.

'I must, resolve myself' She thought.

She thought about who or what put her in this situation but specific names were hard to come by.

'It should be simple, it should be simple. I don't need to think hard, everyone is guilty.'

Her loss meant everyone must pay the price. A twisted smile amongst hushed laughter mirred her image. Relishing in these thoughts, She had felt her first breath of fresh air and had wanted to sit up.

The pain she felt from before had dulled; however, in this moment, it suddenly flared as she began to sit up, but she pushed through it. She could feel a burning sensation permeating every tendon in her body while straining her muscles while she moved.

"He called it, a devil technique?" She shook her head, "Such a cruel technique should never be practiced and yet here I am, suffering due to it. Even if I had energy, I would be unable to use it. My energy channels seem..."

Now sitting cross legged, she began to self introspect. It was as bad as she had thought. She couldn't cultivate like this, and she may even be crippled. If she wanted revenge, she would have to fix this body before anything else.

Setting that aside, she squinted into her surroundings. Observing nothing but black, she gathered the rest of her strength and began to sweep her legs underneath herself. 

'If there is nothing, nothing else that I do, I must atleast make sure my sisters are buried properly.'

Two Hands pushed against the wet stone floor to stand. Staggering immediately, she caught herself before she could fall all fours. The darkness did not help at all with her balance. Shakily, one foot after the other, she moved ahead. Periodically she would stumble into a puddle, sloshing water all over her body, but she pressed on. Getting back up and moving forward.

Thump!

Her body collided with a cavern wall. Falling to the ground once more. More than the pain she had felt, was immediate surprise, "Was I always this close to a wall?"

She suddenly had a premonition.

Feeling around the wall before reaching for the floor, her hands scoured the ground for a loose stone. Hands pillaged the area until they brushed across a pointy bit. Picking it up, she brought it to wall in front of her and began scraping the wall with it.

The rubbing of stone echoed within the cave as the words, "Start" was written out. One by one, the characters were repeatedly written until she felt that it was enough.

Her fingers swiped over the stone face, feeling each detail. She could not quite make out what it said but it felt unnatural enough that she would notice it. 

'This should work'

As such, she started walking, one hand on the cave wall, one step in front. Several incense sticks could have burned and replaced before she felt those unnatural lines on the cave wall again.

'540 paces. This cave, its definitely smaller than the one i remember. And, I did not feel the exit that should have been here.'

'Rather, I did not feel an exit at all...Where am I?'

She grew concerned. She had no idea what happened once she passed out.

'Did that bastard move me? Or maybe that strange blue light moved her again?'

The more she thought, the more she believed the latter. In a place with no exit and smooth walls, she highly doubt a human could have brought her here. Not only that, but the darkness of this place meant that it should be completely sealed. There would be no escape.

'Then...are my sisters even here?'

She was worried about them, but if they were seperated, then she could not even grant them a proper burial.

But more importantly, she was very worried if she could escape. 

"Sister said the hidden worlds were seperate from the regular world. They existed seperately from everything else, even night and day would be different. Did the light take me back to my world or am I still stuck somewhere else?"

This was a big question. If she was in the hidden world then she would need to deal with unknown circumstances but if it was in her world, she would be more free to act.

But after walking for a bit, her body was fatigued. No energy, no food but there was a bit of water, She sat down and listened. First for where the water was. 

Drip, Drip, Drip...

With each drop of water she moved closer to where it was, until one step spashed a bit of water onto herself. She stopped and put her hands into the small puddle, scooping what she could for a drink.

Boom-booom-boom! 

Lin Mei shook-along with the cave, before continuing her drink. 

'Thunder' she thought. 'So noisy, I should be at the surface then right?'

Her eyes grew heavy after a drink. Her body weary and mind growing foggy. She decided it was time to rest. Later, she would leave, but she had not thought of how yet...

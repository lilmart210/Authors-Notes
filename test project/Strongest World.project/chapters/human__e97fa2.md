'Feels so soft, am I being carried?'

Lin mei felt extremely groggy, as if she had not awoken for eons. Her chest was pressed against someones back as they carried her quickly. One of her arms was slung over someones' shoulder and her legs where grasped firmly. The one arm that dangled to her side was in a wooden sling, braced from all movement but moved freely all togethor.

She took this moment to arouse, but kept herself limp like she had been this entire time. On one hand, she had been sleeping on rock. Where had she felt something like this before? She couldn't recall clearly. On the other hand, she felt comfortable but not safe. Carefully, her eyes opened to see what lies beyond them.

But there was nothing. It was only now that the color she had seen all this time was not the darkness of the caves or the blackness that pervaded in the absense of light. It was the nothingness of being unable to see.

She could only lament.

'Did I collapse after leaving? I hardly remember. They must have chased those bears away as I was escaping.'

But as she began collecting her thoughts, her hearts' pace picked up as she became alert.

'People!'

Thoughts raced amidst her head. She had thought often about what to do once she was back but she never planned for this situation. She was a fugitive and if she was still around the city, she would definiteily be spotted before long. She thought it was unlikely that anyone would recognize her right away but her circumstances would make someone think to check.

'Do I kill him? If we make it back to a city then I can only be jailed. If I can't give them what they are looking for then I'll be tortured, blamed then killed.'

This was unacceptable.

Her face had been resting on the lower neck of her carrier and the more she felt the warmth of their skin the stronger this desire grew.

'This is the best time, no this is the only chance!'

But as the temptation grew, she slowed her own thoughts. She could not be so impulsive. If there was more than one person she would be unable to fend for herself and if she could not cleanly kill the person carrying her, she would be unable to fend for herself after being dropped.

Not to mention, if they were regular humans, could they beat a bear that digs through stone with its claws? It was unlikely, no impossible. Normal Bears did not dig through rocks and the unawakened could not kill such a creature.

'But wait? Sister always spoke about the blue light taking them to a new world. I don't know how it works but we did move through space somehow. Forget about recognizing me, If this was really a new world, would they even speak the same language?'

She did not know but she knew she needed to check.

Her body began to tense, as if startled awake, and Lin Mei stumbled into a fit of coughs.

"Something wrong senior brother?"

Slowing his pace amongst the overgrowth, Long Yan took a glance over his shoulder.

"Did She wake up?"

Everyone took a look. This begger crawled out of the cave space and then collapsed. Dirty, injured, they were sure if they hadn't found her then and there she would undoubtedly die. In fact, they were not sure if she could survive the way back and was sligthly anxious about this.

It was unfortunate that they couldn't bring in the best parts of the Stone clawing bear, but they had decided to harvest the younger bears and the senior brother had crafted a rather unique story.

But this girl would need to survive for it to be true! Bringing back a corpse was not an option.

Setting Lin Mei on the ground with her back resting along an old tree, the quietest of voices made their way into their ears.

"Where...am...I?"

'Shes alive and she can speak!'

Everyone heaved a sigh of relief. Expressing Immense concern, Long Yan gave an immediate answer.

"We saved you while working on our trial. Once we are back at the sect, you will certainly be treated! Hang on, we are still a day away."

Lin Mei felt something heavy between her labored breaths. It was stuck on the left side of her chest where her arm had been broken. She pushed through this feeling and continued asking. She needed to know if she could stay with them or leave at once. Being abandoned here would certainly be better than being recognized later.

"Which sect, is that?"

"Traveling Cloud Sect."

'Traveling Cloud?'

Despite being unable to see, a light shined in her eyes. Although she did not recognize the name, it did not mean it was completely safe.

"Wheres extreme path city? Is it close? My family, they must be really concerned."

Extreme path? The group looked at each other, the next more confused than the last. None of them recognized it. They were familiar with many of the cities and towns as they would frequent them as disciples. Of the many cities and towns within several thousand miles, not one had such a name. In fact, cities were normally named after the people who lived there and there was no such person with that kind of domineering name.

Long Yan suddenly felt like they were running out of time. Although one of his junior sisters helped patch her injuries, it seems that there was more to her problems than they could see.

"It seems your memory is not so great, well don't think too much about it. Here, take this."

Long Yan's hands reached into his garment and procured a small bag. Inside the bag was a leaf wrapping. He carefully undid the wrapping under the eyes of everyone else.

The tall disciple, Pan Zhengshang commented, "Is that a vitality pill? Outer disciples only get so many and you've already decided to do this? Senior brother is too generous."

Disturbed from her thoughts, Lin Mei's attention was caught.

'A vitality pill?'

Aside from the ones they had taken, she knew pitifully little about pills and their purpose. They were not common items she could afford to begin with.

Lian Shuang eyes rolled as she defended her senior brother, "Of course he is. Hasn't he treated everyone well during this trip? If only you were more like him."

Zhengshang growled but said nothing further, instead turning his attention from the pill back to the tiny body in front of him.

With a squeeze, the pill broke into many small pieces. Long Yan picked up a single fragment and guided it to her lips.

"Here, open, this will help you recover faster."

But Lin Mei didn't budge. Be it weeks or months, she knew she would eventually get better. Until now that was how she always recovered. She didn't know what he was holding and couldn't see it either. She could heal physical injuries with time but she did not know if the same was true for poison. She refused to take such an obvious risk.

Seeing the rebellious behavior, Lian Shuang felt like Long Yan's kindness had fallen into a dogs maw.

"You should definitely accept this gift. humph! Someone like you will unlikely ever receive this kind of benefit again!"

"Stop it, we can't make her eat it."

Although he said that, Long Yan was put into a difficult position but he did not force it. Swallowing the remnants of the pill himself, he felt his vigor increase greatly. A thin arm made its way over his shoulder and with a heave, he hoisted the tiny body onto his back once more.

"Lets move quickly. If she won't eat it then we have to move that much faster."

Foot steps moved unceassingly and even when night fall came, they did not cease. Swiftly through brush, branches and dense undergrowth, the group navigated their way at a steady pace. Lin Mei could feel the calm breaths in and out as they moved. At the same time she began finding it dificult to breathe herself and the longer she stayed awake, the less energy she had.

She could feel the mending flow flare up at times before subsiding. She felt it thrum to life, before softening back. The throbing pain and stiffness she felt did not appear to worsen but it did not feel like it was healing.

'Is this an effect of these injuries? Can I even recover from this? Just one, just one attack from that creature and I'm like this...'

She felt bitter.

As she thought about these things, she felt some regret at her earlier sentiment but she did not stay awake long enough to dwell on that decision. Her consciousness began to muddle as she felt herself drifting into a state of oblivion. Her breaths became longer as she fell into a deep sleep...

"Oh, these are quite nice. So are these. I'll give your team a pass. So that means only teams two, three and seven will receive good marks. Heh, the rest of you can leave."

A well dressed man who appeared to be in his fourties was overlooking a table of goods. On it were a myriad of collected items but his eyes were only drawn towards certain elements.

"Deacon Tao, Wait!, wasn't it posted that any medicinal flower was useful? These flowers are clearly listed in the list of foundational herbs for medicine.

"Look at it again and tell me which herb is this? It looks like the root of something akin to jinsing but i'm afraid its just an ornamental flower. It can't even be used to make a cup of good tea. This kind of rubish, who would want it? I clearly stated what was needed and you dare bring back trash. What has the sect been recruiting these day? They can't recognize such basic materials."

The disciple ground down his teeth but he could not refute. Although he was a tier 2 outer disciple, but he had never focused on medicine. After all, he had no plans to become a physician or doctor.

'Wasn't there 8 teams?'

The deacon took a look around and noticed that he seemed to be missing a group. 

'Thats right, disciple Yan was on that team. There is no way he would forget about the deadline right?'

Right as that thought crossed his mind, he saw several figures leap from the forest boundary into his surroundings. A team of five people dashed into the clearing, messy and disheaved. Not even the senior of the group could keep his breadth steady.

He chewed his gums for a second as he observed their state of being. All of the disciples besides the man in front and a unusually tall disciple collapsed on the spot.

"It seems you all encountered some unexpected troubles, come, tell me about them."

But before they could respond, the same voice from before interjected, "Looks like Long Yan isn't cut out for any leadership role."

Snickering between remarks, he continued, "Look at how disheaveled his group is, he clearly cannot remember what day the assignment was due."

The mirth in his voice could not be hidden. Long Yan was not one to be outdone, His hands sliped from underneath the leg it was supporting, grabbing a bag at his waist. But as he was about to speak, a hand stopped him and carefully shifted the burden from his back instead.

Deacon Tao didn't spare even a glance to that disciple as he spoke, "Yi Zemin, Disciple Yan's skill is exceptional, how could your group who failed to bring back anything worth value compare?"

He eyed what one of the girls and big guy beside him was carrying on their backs. Two cubs wriggled but were secured tightly in bandages.

"I don't know what they've been through but clearly more effort was dedicated here than what your team had even considered."

Deacon Tao carefully laid a frail girl onto the ground as he continued his words.

"I'm certain whatever they have brought, it would be worth far more than what was produced so far, theres not need to even mention it. His team naturally passes."

At his words, both Yi Zemin and Long Yan swallowed any words they may have had. For Yi Zemin, he couldn't refute those claims. For Long Yan, the praise was too much. He took a quick look over the table and eyed the things that were lined up. He definitely saw something that would take better precedence over his goods. But maybe the deacon was aware of something he wasn't.

"Enough of that, I'm a doctor first and instructor second. Whats the story with this young girl? Why is she nearly at deaths door?"

Deacon Tao hands made their way to the make shift split and began undoing the bandages that held it in place.

Long Yan eyes made their way back to to the girl laying amongst the ground, "Deacon, Its like this..."

Long Yan iterated over their events, mentioning how they had began hunting for particular beasts and having to fight off many such animals deep in the forest.

"Once we understood that we were dealing with a family of Stone clawing bears, we decided that it would be better to find better prey. It would not be worth the risk to attack several adult bears without a stronger team. Not to mention that by targeting one of them earlier, they would be on high alert. It was completely unexpected that they would be living in such a group."

Long Yan was long winded in his explanation, but suddenly he grabbed his chest while looking agrieved, "But as we had decided to leave, we heard the voice of a young girl call out desperately, to our complete surprise, of the two adult bears in the cave, one of them had been holding her in their maw. Almost like, instinct, I decided that we had to attack. It was an exteneuos effort but we could not leave her be. After all, human life is worth a risky battle. That is the purpose of our strength."

Deacon Tao listened while his hands poked and prodded the body of the young girl. His lips swung side to side as he pulled his hands away.

'Thats odd, Disciple Yan's story seems a little off. At first glance, her scars seem fresh but those seem to be quite old, permanent and already closed. While her body has no other obvious signs of damage. However, she does seem to have some internal injuries, but there doesn't seem to be any signs of immediate puncture.'

He didn't place it at heart, of all the disciples that he had the chance to meet, he had the best impression of this disciple in particular. He would definitely gloss over such details if it meant a better relationship.

Deacon Tao asked, "Has she told you what happened to her? It doesn't seem like this is something a simple beast could have done."

Long Yan shook his head.

"She awoken a few times but each time she was less active than the last. She only answered questions the first time and at the time, it seemed like she was speaking nonsense."

An open palm with closed fingers extended towards the lower belly of Lin Mei. Deacon Tao decided that he would have to check thoroughly before he transfered her.

A black mist oozed from his palm and Deacon Tao had begun taking a better look inside. But the moment the mist sunk into her body, he snatched his hands away.

"T-This can't be! No way, no way, I must be mistaken."

Long Yan eyes darted back and fourth between the deacon and the girl. 

'What was he looking at? Did something happen? She's not...already dead is she?'

Deacon Tao mind was quickly perusing through his medical knowledge as he placed both hands on her stomach. More black mist appeared from his palms and sunk beneath her skin. A dark light diffused through the scars as they traveled deep beneath the body's surface. Deacon tao's eyes were shut as he recalled his studies and the body he navigated with his mind.

Deacon's Tao face had become all sorts of contorted the longer he went on. Sweat had dripped down his face and through his robes as he began muttering under his breadth. For a period of time, the others who had been waiting for the final results had begun crowding around. Seeing how deep in concentration the deacon was in made them feel like something big might be happening.

Soon, the light emanating from her body faded as the deacon pulled his hands free. They ran across his face and through his hair before collapsing back onto his lap.

He gave Long Yan a very long look.

"She's an Imperial Scholar, you've found an Imperial Scholar!"

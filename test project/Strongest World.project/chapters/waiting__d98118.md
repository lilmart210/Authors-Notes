Yu Lin sat on a nearby rock. His gaze traveled between a barrier and a small disk in his hands. Nearly a year ago he had been stationed here to await young master Jin return. Yet so much time has past and the young master had yet to come out.

Why was he here? Was there even someone past this barrier?

Yu Lin lamented, 'After helping rebuild the city, my greed got the best of me. If I had known trying to help out so much would land me in this position, I would have said nothing at all.'

Fox demons were normally used as personal messengers. Before he joined that ragtag group, he had been a confident for his previous boss. He would be at the forefront of information gathering and transport. The benefits were immense relative to his abilities.

But there were other fox demons stronger than him, so they naturally took his place here.

'I thought I would be able to build a relationship with the master but it seems I got regulated to being a door watcher, but what can I do ha-ha'

He thought about the many groups that pooled into this world, many of them were not as peaceful as the Jin family. With two powerful masters watching over such a small territory, there werent many people willing to disturb this area. Most actively avoided it. Inside this realm, there was countless space and treasures to be discovered. There was no need to fight over this small desert.

Because of this, he felt it was worth it to stay in the Jin Family territory. Atleast here he did not have to worry about which part of the road he would be burried under.

'hmm, it may not be bad to get a close relationship with young master Jin then. With those monsters as parents, the son would also be dignified and a center piece. Someone that could spend a year in seclusion must be a top master.'

An image of Jin Hai began taking place inside Yu Lin's mind. He imaged a dignified first son with all the heirs of a noble young master. If he could get close with such a figure, his future would be set forever.

Lost amidst his fantasies, Yu Lin failed to notice the barrier that has been erect for the last year fade away. In its place a young boy was walking out.

"Y-you are?" a voice stammered out.

"Ah? A brat, what are you doing here?" Yu Lin felt his mood sour as he was pulled out from his sweet dreams. He lazily glanced over at a boy. In his eyes, a kid no older than eight or nine appeared in his sights. 

"The Barrier!" Yu Lin shot up, He took a closer look at the boy. Blood red eyes looked him directly in his, his mood immediately took a turn for the better. There was only one person that had those eyes and that was the matriarch of the Jin family. For someone else to have it, it could be no one other than Jin Hai.

"Young master Jin! You've completed your retreat. The family heads have been awaiting your arrival."

Yu Lin was all smiles as he looked favorably at the young boy. Although younger than he originally had thought, his age was irrelevant.

"family head? oh, you mean mother and father!" Jin hai was lively at the thought of meeting his parents after so long. He took a glance at the sword by his side, it was finally time to return it.

Although excited at the news, Jin Hai had a questioning gaze, "But, who are you?"

"I'm Yu Lin. I've been stationed here by the matriarch to guide you to them once your training concludes. I'm here to help you with any questions or task you may have." 

Jin Hai began walking a few circles around Yu Lin. He as very intrigued by this demon in front of him. It had not been long since he had hatched, Jin Hai has rarely seen another person outside of his parents. There was nothing but training with mom, dad and empty grasslands. There wasn't even creatures there to eat the grass. He wasn't sure if other living beings existed until now.

Yu Lin was perplexed, "Young master, is something the matter?"

"Oh no, nothing."

Jin Hai continued to walk circles around Yu Lin. Yu Lin remained silent, his image of a dignified young master was slowly falling apart.

Rumble!

The ground began shaking for a few moments before subsiding. Yu lin was not bothered by it but Jin Hai became extremely curious.

Jin hai looked at the now stable ground and then at Yu Lin, "hey, whats that making the ground shake?" 

Yu Lin explained casually, "Its a Desert-stone worm. From time to time they move locations under the ground to areas with new desert stone. With the barrier down, there is probably untouched rocks over there. Its probably interesting in the location and they are one of the few demonic beast in these sandy plains."

Jin Hai's eyes monitored the rumbling ground in the surroundings, it seemed to have stopped in the direction that he came from.

A mischevious grin made its way onto Jin Hai's face, "Are they strong?"

Yu Lin thought Jin Hai was worried, he quickly assured him, "They are usually in the Flood demon realm and are rarely stronger than that. This subordinate here is a peak flood demon and can kill these creatures with ease. No need to be worried about anything troublesome."

Jin Hai's fighting spirit was stoked. From his aura, a red sword condensed in the air and fell into his outstretched hands. He looked at the direction the rumbling had stopped at and dashed forwards.

"Y-Young master, what are you doing?" Yu Lin followed after him. Yet after a few moments, he had was unable to close the distance between them.

'Incredibly fast! It can't be that his strength is higher than mines at his age, right?'

Jin Hai hand flicked the sword at the ground ahead of him, it pierced through the sand and deep into the desert stone beneath the surface.

Rumble! Rumble! Rumble!

The surface shook, the sound of sand flowing and stone breaking, sounded everywhere. Breaching the surface, A worm with interlocking stone as skin made its way out of the ground. Its size dwarfed anything nearby, hundreds of feet long and a dozen feet wide, it emerged from the sand with a fractured exterior, wailing at the sky.

Jin Hai took a step into the air, "Awesome! Its so big! This is a desert worm, worthy of being my first kill. he-he, today, you will become my stepping stone!"

Putting on airs, Jin Hai's red aura flared as another red sword began condensing in his hands. 

Yu Lin decisively stepped in front of him, blocking Jin Hai's view of the worm, "Young master, this creatures is at the peak of our realm, fighting it won't be easy. We should be returning to meet the masters in the city. They have been expecting your arrival for some time."

Big red eyes greeted Yu Lin and then navigated around his shape, "I won't be long, besides, I haven't finished training yet, I want to test my strength."

From the corner of Jin Hai's eyes, a blood red pupil began to emerge. It looked briefly at Yu Lin. Feeling threatened Yu Lin shrunk back alot. He felt an oppressive feeling began to overwhelm him. Despite being at the same stage as the boy, he felt that he did not stand a chance against him.

Yu lin gazed at the emerging pupil, 'Is this the Red Demons abilities?' Yu Lins thoughts surfaced on his face as he took a few more steps back. 

He had done some investigation and had discovered that that the matriarch was called a red demon. 

Before now, he knew nothing about variant abilities, but with the advent of the new world, there was tons of information to be gained. One such thing he learned about were Variant Demons. Some demons undergo a transformation and become a variant. It first manifest as a change of color, usually within the eyes and progress to other parts of the body. These abilities are usually unique to an individual.

But one look at Jin Hai and Yu Lin realized that those traits can defintiely be passed on. 

'Although, why is the kids hair black? The matriarchs hair is red, could it be his ability is weakened due to his father being human?'

He didn't know how these things work specifically, but he was wise enough not to ask.

Looking at the second pupil in Jin Hai's left eye travel and merge with the other pupil in the center of his eye, Yu Lin instinctively knew that red demons were nothing short of abnormal.

'These people, its best never to become enemies, only friends. Strange abilities like these, can only be awaken by talented individuals. It seems that the entire family can freely inherit these traits.'

Yu Lin didn't stop Jin Hai, In fact he couldn't. Although equal in cultivation, he knew he stood no chance in stopping him.

Everywhere Jin Hai eyes looked, the spirit in the surrounding air would become thin and scatter. 

The desert stone worm rigid eyes locked onto the pair as they stood within the sky, murderous intent was directed at both. Its mouth opened to revealed rows of jagged teeth, it wanted nothing short of devouring the pair.

But under the gaze of Jin Hai, its already broken stone skin began cracking even more. Some parts of it disintegrated directly into sand and flew away in the scorching wind. The cycle of decay had begun.

Nearby, Yu Lin could feel a slight gravity field descend over the desert. 

"Scatter and be destroyed, third move of the Blue Mountain sword technique, Retrieval!"

A peerless pressure spread out over the desert air. A burning red sword appeared in the air, its weight changed to become immeasurably heavy as it shot down towards the worm. Yu Lin seemed to recall the first time he had seen the Jin family patriarch attack an evolved desert stone worm. He had felt a similar pressure like this one, albeit many times stronger. Although the Jin family head was human, Yu Lin didn't dare think of him any less than the madam.

Roar!

The desert stone worm shrieked, stone pillars began emerging from the ground as they covered its body from the blinding red light.

Yu Lin stole a glance at the boy and then the falling attack, 'It won't be enough, the desert stone that these worms use are much tougher than in its natural state underground. Although strong, He will need to attack several more times.'

Chi-ka-ka-ka-ka...

Just then, Yu Lin heard the stone covering the worm crack and shatter, leaving the worm to face the falling strike unaided.

'What happened?' Yu Lin wondered.

Boom!

Red filled his vision, Yu Lin even found it somewhat hard to observe as rock, sand and stone flew out from the collision. The attack completely reshaped the terrain around them. With a sweep of his hand, the sand blocking his vision settled and what he saw made him beathe a cold air into his body.

"Young master Jin is peerless, just like his parents. He will certainly become a monarch in the future."

Below them, a thousand foot wide crater was the only thing left besides scattered pieces of the desert stone worm and a yellowish spirit core. All of which was embedded amongst the sides of the hole. The beast was slain in a single attack.

As Yu Lin watched Jin Hai descend to pick up the beast core, he wondered if he could do the same? 

'If I plan carefully, I can launch a preparred attack. With the aid of some treasures or formations, I could launch a single decisive blow...'

Yu Lin knew his limits, it was not possible. Jin Hai didn't require any preperation or prior knowledge, just overwhelming strength.

'Too fierce!'

From start to finish the battle was finished in two strikes. Yu Lin followed behind Jin Hai meekly, "Young Master Jin is as fierce as they come, but could you explain how you broke through the stone shield around the worm? This one has failed to see through your methods."

Jin Hai nose was pointed at the sky, "You have good eyes, its only natural you can't see. Its my left eye that weakens anything it looks at."

Jin Hai looked directly at Yu Lin. Yu Lin felt the energy within his body beginning to disperse. He immediately began circulating his spirit. Jin Hai's blood red pupil began retreating, slowly, back into the corners of his eye. With it out of sight, the sensation Yu Lin felt dissipaited greatly.

'So frightening?'

Yu Lin felt his scalp jump. Simply by looking at him, Jin Hai can disperse the energy in his body. This would leave him unable to endure even the weakest of attacks. His gaze hardened as he resolved to never fight a red demon or any variant demons for that matter. Their powers cannot be explained by reason.

"Oh, this is kinda pretty." Jin hai was playing with the core of the dead demon beast.

Eying the never ending expanse, Jin Hai asked Yu Lin, "What other creatures are there in the desert?"

Seeing the look on the young masters face, Yu Lin suddenly had a premonition that today was going to be a long day of hunting. 

"In response to young master, there are Desert Clam Demons, Desert Stone demons, sand golems and other demonic beasts. The strongest being the Fanged Scorpion. They posses poison that can disolve flood demons comletely if touched and seriously injure Awakened demons."

"So many! Ha-ha, it seems that I havn't given mother a present yet, these are pretty enough, so lets gather a few more before returning."

Yu Lin Sighed, 'Personally, I would never want to encounter another wild demonic creature if I could, yet this boy has excited seclusion, wanting nothing more than to continue fighting. This...'

"How about gathering just two more." Yu Lin picked up a rock, in his hands flashed a small carving knife. He flicked it a few times before the stone in hands turned into a life like creature, "This beast look familiar right? I have some skill with carving, while we search for a suitable target, I can show you how to carve figures out of stone. How about it?"

Jin Hai's eyes went big, "Really? Hey thats so cool, lets do that! Lets do that!" 

Yu Lin smiled gently, he had initially wanted to get close to Jin Hai, it seemed like it was easier than he had thought. Although His heart hurt to use those beasts cores as mere ornaments but it was worth it if he could get the favor of the young master.

The field of mist and fog had shrunken considerably. In this particular world, the ceiling was a stone roof with carvings litering the walls. What stood out immediately was the flawless white stone book that laid amongst the many jades on the floor.

'I am changing. This is change'

Looking at the forming book, the being had felt a sense of satisfaction.

The being had never ceased having these thoughts. After the first time it felt the desire to change, this was the only thing it had on its mind. its conciousness lingered more closely to the gemstones, severely decreasing the range of things it could see elsewhere. Although interested in observing the outside world, it felt that this was the right choice if it truly wanted to one day interact with it.

Suddenly some figures appeared within its vision. Just outside a void crack, there was a few beings hanging around the space.

The being jerked from its concentration, 'creatures...' 

After restricting itself to this small world, it had not observed any creature for a long time now. It was consumed with the desire for change that it completely forgot about the outside world. Now that a tear in space had appeared, it was as if someone spilled ink on a sheet of white paper, just begging for it to look. 

Taking a curious glance around the space, it saw a plethora of things in its vision. There was almost too much to look at. 

'So long since such a sight. They are changing as well.'

It felt envious, the creatures in the physical world changed so fast. It wanted to be like them. It wanted to change as well. Its desire grew the longer it stared.

Withdrawing its gaze to look at its own self, it began to focus. There was a strong sense of self coming from the book that laid within its mist. The being had a feeling that its vision of the outside world was starting to come from that stone book. Previously, this feeling came from the faint mist that lingered in the air, but now the book held that feeling.

Immersing itself inside of this feeling, it could hear a faint rythm. An understanding of sorts as a certain path entered its mind. Gradually, the slow flowing mist in the air gradually began picking up speed, rushing around the book before settling onto its surface. Stone pages began carving themselves as the size of it grew ever so sligthly under the wind.

An unknown period of time passed but the being had no notion of it. What was time? Is it something that could be felt? It didn't know and it did not matter, the being felt that time for it was irrelevant. It had already chosen its path forward and would continue on that road.

Amid its intense concentration, its senses was alarmed once more. The flow of the mist around itself slowed considerably as the murmurings of the wind became too faint to be heard, fully disrupting its concentration.

'What now?'

The beeing looked around carefully, but this time, he was a bit shocked. If before when those myriad beings came into contact with it, there would not be much interaction. Even when it tried so desperately to communicate they would simply ignore it. Now there was another small group at its crack in the void. 

Has it changed itself enough to interact with others? 

The being became alert and a strange feeling began running through its thoughts. It didn't know what this was but it felt that it was not bad.

The creatures seemed stalled at its entrance, just like before, but this time it seems that they are more active than previously. Suddenly, one of the beings seemed to have made up its mind about something, storming directly into the void crack.

Immediately after the creatures crossed the boundary, it tried to talk to them.

'xx xox uxdexxtanx?'

The creatures froze immediately, but this was normal. Seeing as they had not fallen over just yet, the previous emotion began blooming even stronger than before. It felt that its chance might have finally came. 

'Did they hear? Can they hear me?'

It knew it couldn't be too over bearing, sometimes when it spoke too much, the creatures fall over and change into something else. Something even less intelligent that could not communicate at all.

But it had time. Periodically, it would speak.

'Do xou xndexsxand?'

...

'xo yxu uxdxrstxnd?'

...

'is it no good?'

The being thought about it some more. But as it observed the creatures carefully, one of them reanimated and starting its walk towards it.

Seeing the direction it resumed walking, it had a wonderful realization, 'It knows thats me!'

It was delighted. Although not sure if the creature could understand its words, but this was progress! It began speaking more rapidly and trying new words, mimicking the speach that it had picked up along the way.

'xhax xs yxur xaxe?'

'wxx axx xou?'

...

'xx xx xx xxx'

Seeing the steps the creature took begin slowing, it felt a little worried. After saying so much, it seems it had some kind of an affect. With each word that it spoke, the creatures steps slowed its pace. 

'So it can hear me!'

It was excited, But this was not what it wanted. It wanted the creature to come closer, it wanted to interact. Now that it began to change itself, it wanted to become apart of the larger world. So the creature stopped its speaking, diving into silence.

An Outstretched hand made its way to the space above its pages. It held a certain charm and the being felt swayed by its intent. Red energy made its way onto those stone white pages, lacing themselves into the seams of the book. With each strand of energy that embedded itself, the being felt a certain comfort that it had not experienced previously. It was brought closer into the embrace of the creature.

Usually, there was an unspeakable silence, a certain desolence in its existence, but now it felt that there was something serene and peaceful. For the first time, it began to feel an emotion it had not felt before and it wanted more.

'Is this what interaction feels like? I was right. I needed to change to interact with the creatures. Now how do I communicate?'

It felt great about its progress but now it had a problem.

'Speaking with the creature causes them some kind of trouble. They are not as quick to interact with me when I speak. Was it speaking thats the problem? Then I cannot speak.'

It thought about this some more, it finally had a perfect creature to interact with, now it needed to do what it has always wanted. 

Amidst its thoughts, it felt a sudden shock, 'What is this? My vision seems...seems to be shrinking!'

Previously it could see outside the void crack very clearly, but now, its sight seemed to be shrinking rapidly. Retracting towards the book.

'Why am I changing? I can't control my vision? This-this-this is not the change I want'

It could feel it, something from within was screaming that it needed to stop this change. But the feeling of interaction felt too good, placating the rising emotion that arose. But as time went on, it could no longer supress it.

Periodically, it would give in and speak.

'Sxox'

...

'xxox'

...

'xtox'

...

'xxop'

As the creatures hands made its way across the surface of the book, rubbing the surface as red tendrils of energy slipped into its pages, the being suddenly realized that its consiousness was growing weaker as well.

A sense of alertness invaded its mind, it needed to stop the creature at once. It knew now that this feeling of interaction was a deception, it was doing something bad to its existence.

'Stop!'

The hand above its cover grounded to a halt, the eyes of the creature seemed to enter a sort of turmoil as its power began receeding. The sense of serenity vanished as its awareness came back in full swing. It was now alert and coherent again.

Not taking the chance for granted, it knew it had to do something fast. If it waited until the creature awoke, then it was likely it would finish what it had started. Putting its consciousness into a state of oblivion. It did not want to vanish.

'How do I continue existing?'

It thought about this, countless thoughts raced into its head as it pondered the question.

'I need to change. Not the change I have been doing, but a new change. But what?'

With its vision restored, the world around it entered its view. It could see countless towns people. It had always wanted to be like those creatures, yet it was not able to do so.

'Why, can I not be them?'

This thought suddenly emerged in its mind. 

'I can change, I can change into them!'

Once this thought took hold, it could not be supressed. It wanted to change into the same form as those creatures. 

'But I can't turn into any creature...'

It thought about its experience a moment ago. Its sense of existence and self had begun fading. Its not a feeling that it would ever want to experience willingly and it carved a sense of crisis deep into itself. It just knew that the end process would not be good for it. If it could feel such a thing, maybe the creatures felt it as well. It thought about the myriad creatures it had observed in its past. They would often be broken into parts of their original selves, devoured by other creatures or changing into a lesser intelligent form. 

Such a thing, it would not have been done willingly. It realized that not all changes are good changes.

Then it must pick a good change, one that would allow itself to continue living amongst the creatures.

The being began concentrating on this task. For the first time, it felt that it did not have enough time. Countless conversions ran though its mind as it began working towards its current task. The original formation of pages happening inside the stone book reversed as the pages began breaking apart, the stone jems in the wall began fading from their lustrious color as they all rapidly turned into a fine mist. 

'Then I will become a creature myself.'

It felt bold in its statement, But can it? It wasn't completely sure. Its vision enveloped the two creatures completely. It observed them carefully, but felt like it needed to look at more. Quickly, its vision stormed outside of the void crack.

There was another creature standing just outside of the space. Its vision enveloped it as well before spreading to other places. Its vision spread far and wide as it made note of all of the things around it. It wanted to become a creature, so it must understand them well. It had watched these creatures for so long, but it felt that it had never truly had a proper look until now. As it looked, it began noticing patterns arise amongst the various beings around and a sense of understanding hit it.

It was a sense of englightenment.

Concentrating back into its world, it felt that it could undergo this task.

Its vision landed on the blood of the creatures inside. It observed the energy they held and the blood that they bled. From what it observed, this was the source of all of these creatures. It seemed to hold a certain mystery and this made it nearly impossible to clearly understand. But as it watched it carefully, the inspiration within grew stronger.

The flow of energy in the cave had become disrupted, flowing towards the rapidly disintegrating book.

Cracks began appearing in the stone around the cave and the space started to grow unsteady. Despite this, neither creature inside the cave seemed to respond.

The being had undergone change before but this was the first time it decided to forcefully change itself. There was no feeling it could follow, only its intuition as it forcefully began melding itself a new form.

'This is it, but I'm...starting to fade....as well'

It was undergoing change but it was also loosing itself. It persevered, constantly refinning its new form with every thought of understanding it was gaining. It had to complete this or else it would fade away when the creatures woke up.

It could not stop, energy, mist and blood revolved in a circle, forming into a hard shell as it replaced the broken book. With each consumed page, the luster of the shell grew brighter and it expanded until it was the size of a small basket.

As quickly as it was changing, its vision and consious was fading. A certain emotion began creeping into its beeing but it ignored it and continued on.

'to fade. So this is what it was like. I only hope, I have changed...properly...'

It wasn't until the book itself shattered that the being's consiousness fadded completely. Falling into a certain obscurity, that its thoughts stopped for the first time.

The moment the book shattered, both the creatures glazed eyes came roaring back to life.

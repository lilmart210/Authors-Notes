The end always justifies the means. Good, bad, evil and rightousness are merely facads to gather strength. Since ancient times, those that gathered enough of it, buried those unknown to the future.

In a world governed by balance, everything exists between the tension between Structure and Decay. To live is to fight entropy; to grow strong is to embrace imbalance.

Born from the desperation to exist, Jin Dandan steps into the world of emotions and flesh. Experiencing desires, tribulations and grand occasions, she felt the need to cling on, even if it was just a little longer.

But this wish could not be achieved. Falling into a cycle of decay due to her own imbalance, she will drag anything down with her if it means she can stand back up.

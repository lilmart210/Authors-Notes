A formless consciousness has watched the world since its inception — a mental-energy treasure, a crystal spirit, that cannot touch anything. Its first attempt to live through someone else, the demonic cultivator White, ends with White sweeping the world as Devil King White until the crown prince and his three advisors bring her down. The spirit spills into her killers: those who follow the advisors' will become Imperial Scholars, those who succumb to White's become devils.

Reborn into a red demon egg, it hatches early as Jin Dandan, daughter of Jin Guangli and Jin Meixiu. Unable to cultivate until her incubation finishes, she buries herself in history books while Qin Peizhi's hunt for the World Revealing Stone and the auction goods pulls her family into the open.

She leaves for the human lands and the Cloud Sect, where the history she read and the history that happened stop matching.

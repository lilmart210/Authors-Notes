Overview ⋯
The main character — a mental-energy treasure (a crystal spirit) reborn into a demon egg. She hatched early, at 5, so she cannot cultivate until her incubation finishes; her knowledge of the world is incomplete. Fiercely intelligent, obsessed with history books and war chess.
Origin ⋯
Her “soul” is a manifestation of her crystal form. She originally inhabited Mei Lin's body. Devil King White was her first resurrection attempt — foiled by humans, which bred White's hatred for them.
Abilities ⋯
Divine Spiritual Awareness (abnormal variant). Can “take” pale-blue spirit-light from creatures to strengthen her senses. Plays war chess by moving real people around the board as puppets.
